The Tale of This Bazaar

In the year of the Seventh Moon, when the sands whispered secrets to the wind, a merchant known only as The Keeper opened a bazaar between the folds of time.

No map marks its location. No compass points its way. Only those with wonder in their hearts and curiosity in their souls will find the gates standing open, lanterns flickering in the eternal twilight.

Each rug was woven by moonlight. Each bottle holds a captured storm. Each lamp remembers a thousand wishes. And each spice carries the flavor of a world that exists only in dreams.

Enter, traveler. The bazaar has been waiting for you since before you were born.
