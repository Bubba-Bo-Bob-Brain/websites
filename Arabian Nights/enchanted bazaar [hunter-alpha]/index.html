The Legend of the Bazaar

They say the Bazaar was not built — it appeared, on the night of a thousand stars, in the space between the waking world and the realm of dreams. Its archways materialized from moonlight and memory, its stalls assembled themselves from stories told around a thousand campfires.

The merchants who tend its winding corridors are not ordinary folk. Some are centuries old, preserved by the very magic they trade. Others stumbled in from distant lands, drawn by a melody only their hearts could hear — a melody that still echoes through the cobblestone passages, guiding the worthy to wonders beyond mortal reckoning.

Here, gold is merely a formality. The true currency is wonder, courage, and the willingness to believe in that which defies explanation. Every transaction changes both buyer and seller, for in the Enchanted Bazaar, nothing is merely bought — everything is destined.

The Bazaar is always open. The question is not whether you can find it — but whether it wishes to find you.
