Step Into the Realm of Marvels

Beneath the amber glow of a thousand lanterns, merchants from realms both seen and unseen gather to trade in wonders. Here, a whispered word can summon storms, a woven thread can carry you across the desert of stars, and the scent of a single spice can unlock memories you never knew you possessed.

Flying Carpets

السجاد الطائر
❧

The Shah's Ascension

Woven in Samarkand, 847 AH

Crimson silk threads spun from the cocoons of moon-moths, this carpet carries three passengers to the threshold of the seven heavens. The tassels weep starlight when ascending.

3,000 dinars

✦ The carpet hums with the memory of a sultan who rode it to ask the moon for her hand in marriage. She declined, but left her perfume in the weave.

Dune's Whisper

Woven by the Blind Weavers of Empty Quarter

Sand-colored wool that renders its rider invisible to all but the wind. Perfect for crossing hostile territories or escaping the eyes of the envious.

1,800 dinars

✦ Once stolen by a thief who used it to rob every house in Baghdad in a single night. The carpet remembers each window it entered and weeps sand at dawn.

Mother-of-Pearl Skiff

Harvested from the floor of the Emerald Sea

Not a carpet but a woven membrane of nacre, buoyant as hope. Glides above water as easily as air. The pearls in its weave sing at midnight.

4,500 dinars

✦ The singing pearls are the voices of drowned lovers. They will guide you to treasure, but ask in return that you listen to one complete song of longing.

Bottled Djinn

الجن المعبأ
❧
📜

Ibn Rushd the Rememberer

Bound in Cordoba, 595 AH

A djinn of prodigious memory who has read every book lost to fire and time. Ask him any question; he knows the answer, though he may recite the entire text that contains it.

5,000 dinars

✦ He was bound not by a sorcerer but by his own curiosity—he read the sealing spell and couldn't resist completing it. He dreams of footnotes.

🍯

Zaytuna the Unsatiated

Pressed from the olive groves of Jinnistan

A culinary djinn who can transform any ingredient into a feast for fifty. She cannot taste food herself and lives vicariously through the pleasure of others.

2,200 dinars

✦ Her greatest ambition is to prepare a meal so perfect that the scent alone will satisfy her. She has been trying for three thousand years. Close, she says. Very close.

⚡

Sā'iq the Swift

Captured in a lightning bolt over Mount Qaf

Delivers messages to any address in any realm, including dreams, memories, and the future. Cannot be intercepted, though occasionally he delivers to the wrong century.

3,500 dinars

✦ Once delivered a love letter to its recipient's grandmother by mistake. She wrote back. A romance bloomed across eighty years. He keeps their letters in his lightning.

Enchanted Lamps

المصابيح السحرية
❧

The Lamp of Unlived Lives

Forged from the tears of a parallel self

Rubbing this lamp shows you visions of the life you might have lived had you chosen differently at your most pivotal moments. The flame burns blue with possibility.

7,000 dinars

✦ The merchant who sells this lamp is living her unlived life. In another world, she is a lamp. They write to each other through the flame.

Qandil al-Haqq

Lit from the first fire ever kindled

In its light, no lie can be spoken. Shadows cast by this lamp reveal the true shape of things—werewolves, hidden doors, the weight of a guilty conscience made visible.

6,500 dinars

✦ The flame was stolen from the court of a truth-obsessed king who died of it—he learned everything about everyone and could no longer bear to rule them.

The Lonely Light

Born from the last wish of a childless widow

This lamp houses no djinn—instead, it absorbs loneliness. Carry it, and you will never feel alone. The flame dances with invisible friends, whispers encouragement, remembers your birthday.

Priceless

✦ The widow wished for someone who would never leave her. The lamp granted it imperfectly—she became the lamp. She is happy. She has so many friends now.

Impossible Spices

البهارات المستحيلة
❧

Za'faran al-Nisyan

Harvested from flowers that grow only on graves

A single thread in tea erases one specific memory of your choosing. The flower remembers in your place, and blooms more beautifully for each sorrow it absorbs.

900 dinars per dram

✦ The merchant cannot remember why he sells this. He has used it too many times. The flowers in his garden know his whole life. He tends them tenderly.

Felfel al-Qadar

Ground from peppercorns that ripen at midnight

Induces prophetic dreams, but only of mundane futures—what you will eat for breakfast, the color of the next stranger's shoes. Many have found salvation in such small certainties.

450 dinars per dram

✦ A philosopher once ate nothing but this pepper for a year. He predicted every meal and every foot-covering. He died content, having proven that small knowledge is still knowledge.

Summaq al-Kalam

The berries wept by the Tree of Babel

Grants fluency in any language for one day, including those of animals, stones, and the recently deceased. Side effect: you will understand every language you hear, wanted or not.

1,200 dinars per dram

✦ The tree still weeps because it understands every word of every language, including the secret names of God that the angels speak. The berries are its way of sharing the burden.

The Merchant Who Bought Tomorrow

"I traded my yesterdays for tomorrows," said the man with no shadow, "and now I live always in the golden light of what is coming, never in the shadow of what has been. Would you like to make the same bargain? I warn you: the price is your reflection."

The Woman Who Collected Dawns

"Each dawn is different," she whispered, opening her carpet bag to show jars of light, "and each one forgets the day before. I remember for them. I have dawns from cities that no longer exist, from lovers' first mornings, from the day before the great fire."

The Mapmaker of Lost Places

"I sell maps to places that have been forgotten," said the blind cartographer, his fingers tracing routes in the air. "The country where your childhood dog still waits. The house where you left your courage. The garden where your unspoken words grow as flowers."
