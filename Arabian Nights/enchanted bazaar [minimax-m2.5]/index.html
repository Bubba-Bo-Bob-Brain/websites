The Legend of Souk al-Hasheen

أسطورة سوق الحشين

Long before the first sultan raised his palace walls, there existed a marketplace that appeared only to those whose hearts burned with wonder. The Souk al-Hasheen — the Bazaar of the Enchanted — trades in goods that defy the laws of nature.

It is said that the great vizier himself once lost his way within these mystical corridors and emerged three centuries later, his beard grown long, his pockets filled with coins bearing the faces of forgotten kings.

The merchants here accept no common gold. Payment must be made in memories, in dreams, in promises kept. For what is wealth but the accumulation of experiences that make us human?
