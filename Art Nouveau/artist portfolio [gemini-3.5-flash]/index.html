The Sinuous Philosophy

The Line is a Living Soul

"The straight line belongs to humanity's rigid ambitions. The curved line belongs to the divine, twisting through the petal of the orchid and the crest of the wave."

In our creations, we eschew the harsh angles of the industrial age. We look to the slow, organic growth of ivy, the iridescent structural magic of insect wings, and the rich textures of molten gold. Every canvas, tapestry, and stained glass pane crafted at Maison Alistair serves as a portal back to the living, breathing world of symbolic naturalism.
