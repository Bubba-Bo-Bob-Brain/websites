The Visionary

In the twilight of the 19th century, a new spirit awakened. It was a time when art was not merely seen, but felt—a living entity intertwined with the natural world. My work seeks to capture this ephemeral beauty, blending the structural elegance of the Golden Ratio with the wild, untamed grace of the vine.

Inspired by the stained glass windows of Chartres and the intricate patterns of nature, each piece is a meditation on the organic form. We explore the "Whiplash Curve," that energetic line that defines the Art Nouveau movement, turning the static canvas into a flowing river of emotion.

"Nature is not a collection of objects, but a continuous flow of energy."
