The Artist

Élise Moreau (1875-1923) was a pioneering figure in the Art Nouveau movement, known for her ethereal depictions of nature and feminine forms. Her work seamlessly blends organic motifs with symbolic imagery, creating compositions that feel both timeless and deeply connected to the natural world.

Inspired by the flowing lines of nature and the rich colors of precious stones, Moreau's paintings and illustrations capture the essence of the Art Nouveau aesthetic. Her mastery of gold leaf techniques and attention to botanical detail set her apart as a true visionary of her time.
