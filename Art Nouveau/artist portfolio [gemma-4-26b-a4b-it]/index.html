The Prologue

In the intersection of the organic and the eternal, I find my muse. My work seeks to emulate the whiplash curve—the sudden, rhythmic movement of a growing vine, the heavy grace of a lily in bloom, and the shimmering gold of a setting sun.

Each piece is a study in symmetry, asymmetry, and the divine geometry found in the wild, unbridled beauty of nature.
