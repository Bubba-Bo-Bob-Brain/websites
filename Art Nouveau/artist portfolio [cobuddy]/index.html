About the Artist

Eloïse Maren is a contemporary Art Nouveau artist whose work revives the sinuous elegance of the Belle Époque through luminous compositions of intertwined botanical forms, ethereal figures, and gilded ornamental frames.

Trained in Vienna and Paris, her paintings draw from the flowing lines of Alphonse Mucha and the gilded symbolism of Gustav Klimt. Each piece is a meditation on the marriage of nature and human emotion, rendered in muted jewel tones with hand-applied gold leaf accents.

Her works are held in private collections across Europe and have been exhibited at the Musée d'Orsay, the Belvedere Palace, and the Mucha Museum in Prague.
