About the Artist

Inspired by the flowing lines of Alphonse Mucha and the golden elegance of Gustav Klimt, my work seeks to revive the spirit of Art Nouveau in a modern context. Each piece is a celebration of nature, femininity, and the intricate beauty of the world around us.

With a background in traditional illustration and digital art, I blend classical techniques with contemporary tools to create pieces that feel both timeless and fresh.
