Isabelle Moreau

Art Nouveau Artist & Illustrator

Inspired by the organic mastery of Alphonse Mucha and the gilded opulence of Gustav Klimt, my work explores the intersection of nature and ornamentation. Each piece is a tribute to the Art Nouveau movement — a celebration of sinuous lines, whiplash curves, and the eternal dance between flora and form.

Working primarily with watercolor, gold leaf, and mixed media on archival papers and vellum, I strive to create works that transport the viewer to a world where every curve tells a story and every petal holds a secret.
