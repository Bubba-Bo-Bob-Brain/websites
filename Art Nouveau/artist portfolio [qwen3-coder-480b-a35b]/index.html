The Artist

Aurélie Moreau draws inspiration from the natural world, translating its organic forms into ethereal compositions that celebrate the timeless beauty of flora and femininity.

With a background in classical painting techniques and a deep appreciation for the Art Nouveau movement, her work bridges historical tradition with contemporary sensibilities.

"In every curve of a stem, in every spiral of a seashell, there lies a story waiting to be told through color and form."
