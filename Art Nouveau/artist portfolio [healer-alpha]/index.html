About the Artist

Born from a fascination with the liminal space between the natural world and decorative fantasy, Elara Thornwood creates works that celebrate the eternal dance of flora and form. Her art draws deeply from the wellsprings of Art Nouveau masters while weaving her own contemporary mythology.

Each piece begins with wandering through wild gardens and ancient forests, sketching the sinuous curves of vines and the delicate architecture of flowers. Back in her studio, these observations transform into visions where nature becomes ornament and ornament becomes alive.

Working primarily in oils, tempera, and mixed media with generous applications of gold leaf, Elara's work has been exhibited in galleries across Europe and North America.

The Creative Process

From garden sketch to gilded masterpiece

I

Botanical Wanderings

Each journey begins in nature's garden. Sketches are made of wildflowers, twisting vines, and the elegant forms that will later transform into ornamental motifs.

II

Composition & Flow

Back in the studio, sketches merge and flow into compositional studies. The whiplash curves are refined and the delicate balance between realism and ornamentation is struck.

III

Layered Transmutation

Layers of oil and tempera build depth and luminosity. Colors are mixed to achieve those characteristic jewel tones that seem to glow from within.

IV

The Golden Touch

The final transformation: gold leaf is carefully applied, catching light and bringing the composition to life with luminous highlights.
