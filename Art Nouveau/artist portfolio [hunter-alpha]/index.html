The Artist

A life devoted to beauty

E.V.
“

I seek to capture the eternal dance between the organic and the ornamental - the moment where a petal's curve becomes a golden line.

”

Elara Voss is a contemporary artist whose work bridges the lush romanticism of the Art Nouveau movement with modern sensibilities. Working from her studio in the Vienna Woods, she creates intricate botanical illustrations that echo the masterworks of Alphonse Mucha and Gustav Klimt.

Her pieces are distinguished by their meticulous attention to natural forms and lavish use of gold leaf, which catches the light like stained glass in a forgotten cathedral.

Medium Watercolor, Gold Leaf and Ink
Based In Vienna, Austria
Active Since 2008
