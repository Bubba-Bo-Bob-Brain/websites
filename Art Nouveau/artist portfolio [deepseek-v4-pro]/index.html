Aurelian Voss

Born in the twilight of the 19th century, I found my voice in the sinuous lines of Art Nouveau. Trained in Vienna and Paris, my work seeks the sacred in the natural — the curve of a stem, the arch of a neck, the shimmer of light through stained glass. Every brushstroke is a prayer to beauty.

My atelier in Brussels welcomes collectors and dreamers alike, where each commission becomes a collaboration between the patron's vision and nature's infinite ornament.
