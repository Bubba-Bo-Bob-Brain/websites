Nyame — The Sky Father

Akan Tradition

Before time breathed its first sigh, Nyame the All-Powerful held the cosmos in His divine palm. He sent His daughter Asase Ya to create the Earth, where humanity would dance between the stars and the soil.

"Obi nka Nyame, na Onnyankopon"
Let no one doubt God, the Great Provider

Osiris — Lord of the Afterlife

Ancient Kemet

The first king of Egypt, Osiris taught humanity agriculture, civilization, and the sacred arts. Though betrayal sent Him to the underworld, He became the judge of souls, gating the path to eternal rest.

"Osiris lives, He lives forevermore"
Book of the Dead

Orunmila — The Oracle of Destiny

Yoruba Tradition

Witness to creation itself, Orunmila holds the sacred knowledge of Ifá—the 256 Odù, the verses that encode all possible destinies. He whispers to the diviner's bones, revealing the paths we must walk.

"Ifá ni ooo, aje ni ooo, olodumare ni ooo"
Ifá is the foundation of all wisdom

Artifact Details

The golden Oba bronze plaques of Benin represent some of the finest metalwork in African history. Created using the lost-wax casting technique, these works depicted the Oba (king) and his court, serving as both royal commemoration and spiritual protection.

Material Bronze, Copper, Gold
Technique Lost-wax Casting
