Benin Bronze Mask

BA-1487-QUEEN
Origin Kingdom of Benin, Nigeria
Period 15th Century CE
Material Cast Bronze
Dimensions 52cm × 32cm × 18cm

This Queen Mother mask represents Idia, mother of Esigie, the Oba who ruled Benin in the early 16th century. The mask showcases the sophisticated bronze-casting techniques developed by Benin artisans, using the lost-wax method to create intricate details that honor the divine feminine power of the queen mother.
