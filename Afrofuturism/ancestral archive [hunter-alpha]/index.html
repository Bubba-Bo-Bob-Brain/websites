⟐ Millennial Timeline ⟐

12,000 years of African civilization — past, present, and prophesied future

~10,000 BCE

Saharan Rock Art

The earliest known depictions of African life — hunters, herders, and dancers etched into stone across the green Sahara.

~3,100 BCE

Unification of Kemet

Upper and Lower Egypt unite under Narmer, beginning one of humanity's greatest civilizations along the Nile.

~2,500 BCE

Great Pyramids Rise

The monuments of Giza are constructed — celestial alignments encoded in stone, a bridge between earth and cosmos.

~1,000 BCE

Kingdom of Kush

The Nubian kingdom rises to power, eventually conquering Egypt and establishing the 25th Dynasty of pharaohs.

~300 BCE

Aksumite Empire

The Aksumite Empire emerges in the Horn of Africa, developing its own script and erecting towering stelae.

~300 CE

Great Zimbabwe

Construction begins on the massive stone city that would become the heart of a vast trading empire in Southern Africa.

~800 CE

Timbuktu & The Golden Age

West Africa's great intellectual center flourishes, with the University of Sankore housing hundreds of thousands of manuscripts.

~1200 CE

Mali Empire

Sundiata Keita establishes the Mali Empire. Mansa Musa's legendary pilgrimage to Mecca displays Africa's immense wealth.

~1600 CE

Dahomey Amazons

The Kingdom of Dahomey's all-female military regiment — the Mino — become one of history's most formidable fighting forces.

~1800 CE

Resistance & Renaissance

Haitian Revolution inspires liberation movements. African diaspora communities preserve and transform cultural traditions.

1960 CE

Year of Africa

Seventeen African nations gain independence. A continent reclaims its sovereignty and begins a new chapter.
