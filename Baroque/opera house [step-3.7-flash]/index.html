Season Performances

La serva padrona

Giovanni Battista Pergolesi

March 15 – April 2, 1724

A witty intermezzo following a clever maid as she outmaneuvers her master to win his hand, featuring the iconic arias that defined the opera buffa genre.

Rinaldo

George Frideric Handel

April 10 – April 28, 1724

Handel's first Italian opera for the London stage, a spectacular tale of knightly valor, sorcery, and forbidden love during the First Crusade.

Orlando furioso

Antonio Vivaldi

May 5 – May 22, 1724

A dramatic epic following the knight Orlando's descent into madness over his unrequited love, featuring Vivaldi's virtuosic violin writing and elaborate staging.

Giulio Cesare in Egitto

George Frideric Handel

June 1 – June 20, 1724

A lavish baroque masterpiece depicting the romance between Julius Caesar and Cleopatra, filled with dazzling arias and dramatic war staging.

Stars of the 1724 Season

Isabella Rossi

Soprano | Serpina in La serva padrona

Acclaimed Italian soprano celebrated for her vibrant portrayals of opera buffa heroines, with sold-out performances across Venice, Naples, and Vienna.

Francesco Bernardi

Castrato | Rinaldo in Rinaldo

Famed for his extraordinary 3-octave vocal range and dramatic intensity, Bernardi is the leading castrato of the Imperial court.

Antonio Vivaldi

Conductor & Violinist | Orlando furioso

The Red Priest of Venice, composer of over 500 concertos, returns to Vienna to conduct his own dramatic opera for the royal season.

Francesca Cuzzoni

Mezzo-Soprano | Cleopatra in Giulio Cesare

Renowned for her expressive phrasing and emotional depth, Cuzzoni brings regal grace and vocal fire to the role of the Egyptian queen.
