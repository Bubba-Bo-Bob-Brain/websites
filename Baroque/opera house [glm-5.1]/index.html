The Season

♦

We cordially summon your presence to eight magnificent productions that shall stir the very depths of your soul. From the sublime arias of Monteverdi to the triumphant choruses of Handel, this season promises an unparalleled journey through the most exalted realms of Baroque operatic art.

Performances of the Season

♦
Opening Night

L'Incoronazione di Poppea

Claudio Monteverdi

12 October 2024

7:30 in the Evening

The crowning of an empress through deceit and desire. Monteverdi's final masterpiece unveils the intoxicating corruption of power at the Roman court.

Poppea — Lucia Caravaggio Nerone — Allesandro Vitelli Ottavia — Maria della Pietà
Grand Spectacle

Giulio Cesare

George Frideric Handel

8 November 2024

7:00 in the Evening

The triumphant Roman general meets the bewitching Queen of the Nile. Handel's most sumptuous opera unfolds in a blaze of political intrigue and impossible love.

Cesare — Francesco del Bosco Cleopatra — Isabella Fiorenza Cornelia — Elena Caravaggio
Tragic Masterwork

Dido and Aeneas

Henry Purcell

14 December 2024

7:30 in the Evening

When the Trojan prince abandons the Carthaginian queen, a lament of such devastating beauty echoes through the ages — the most sorrowful aria ever composed.

Dido — Victoria Ashford Aeneas — Marcus Vane Sorceress — Nadia Volkova
Festive Gala

L'Orfeo

Claudio Monteverdi

11 January 2025

7:00 in the Evening

The father of opera's eternal tale — a musician's descent into the underworld to reclaim his beloved, and the tragic power of a backward glance.

Orfeo — Allesandro Vitelli Euridice — Lucia Caravaggio Caronte — Giovanni Ferrara
Spectacular Revival

Rinaldo

George Frideric Handel

15 February 2025

7:30 in the Evening

Crusader and enchantress, sorcery and redemption — Handel's first London opera bursts with martial splendour and ravishing arias of love and war.

Rinaldo — Francesco del Bosco Armida — Nadia Volkova Almirena — Isabella Fiorenza
Mythological Vision

La Calisto

Francesco Cavalli

22 March 2025

7:00 in the Evening

Jupiter's infatuation with a mortal nymph ignites a cascade of divine deception and earthly passion — Cavalli's sensuous Venetian masterpiece.

Calisto — Lucia Caravaggio Giove — Marcus Vane Diana — Isabella Fiorenza
Enchanted Evening

The Fairy Queen

Henry Purcell

19 April 2025

7:30 in the Evening

A masque of bewildering enchantment adapted from Shakespeare's Dream — Purcell's semi-opera weaves mortal folly and fairy magic into a riot of spectacle.

Titania — Victoria Ashford Oberon — Giovanni Ferrara Corydon — Allesandro Vitelli
Season Finale

Serse

George Frideric Handel

24 May 2025

7:00 in the Evening

A king's capricious desire sparks a garden of romantic chaos. Handel's most tender comedy opens with the most celebrated aria in all of opera.

Serse — Francesco del Bosco Romilda — Isabella Fiorenza Arsamene — Maria della Pietà

Illustrious Artistes

♦
LC

Lucia Caravaggio

Soprano

The Venetian nightingale whose crystalline high notes have summoned standing ovations from La Fenice to the Royal Opera House. This season she embodies Poppea, Euridice, and Calisto.

Poppea Euridice Calisto
FB

Francesco del Bosco

Countertenor

A voice of almost supernatural beauty — the heir to the great castrati tradition. His Cesare and Rinaldo have been proclaimed definitive by critics across three continents.

Cesare Rinaldo Serse
IF

Isabella Fiorenza

Soprano

Fire and tenderness in equal measure — Fiorenza's Cleopatra has been called the most seductive portrayal of the age. Her trills rival the nightingale itself.

Cleopatra Almirena Diana Romilda
VA

Victoria Ashford

Mezzo-Soprano

England's most commanding dramatic voice — her Dido's Lament has moved audiences to tears from Glyndebourne to the Met. A presence of devastating emotional authority.

Dido Titania
MV

Marcus Vane

Baritone

A voice like burnished bronze — dark, resonant, and achingly human. Vane brings tragic nobility to every role, from Aeneas to the king of the gods himself.

Aeneas Giove
NV

Nadia Volkova

Contralto

The Siberian sorceress — Volkova's portrayal of villainesses and enchantresses radiates a hypnotic menace that has thrilled audiences worldwide.

Sorceress Armida
