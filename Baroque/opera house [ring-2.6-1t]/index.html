❧ This Season's Program ❧

The Fifty-Fourth Season of the Teatro della Corona d'Oro opens its gilded doors to the discerning patrons of the arts. Under the illustrious direction of Maestro Lorenzo Vivaldi-Grimaldi, our orchestra and company present a season of extraordinary breadth — from the soaring bel canto of Bellini to the thunderous dramas of Verdi, from the ethereal enchantments of Mozart to the passionate innovations of Puccini.

Each evening within these hallowed walls is a communion of light and shadow, voice and silence, the mortal and the divine. We invite you to surrender to the splendor of opera — to let the music wash over you like golden light through cathedral glass.

The Grand Hall

Built in 1867 during the height of the Second Empire, the Teatro della Corona d'Oro stands as one of Europe's most magnificent opera houses. Its horseshoe-shaped auditorium seats 2,142 patrons beneath a ceiling fresco depicting Apollo and the Muses. The legendary acoustics, designed by the mathematician Joseph Ernest Lamb, remain unmatched to this day.
