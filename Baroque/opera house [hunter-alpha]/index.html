Giuseppe Verdi

❦

A tale of love, sacrifice, and the fleeting nature of earthly pleasures. The courtesan Violetta surrenders everything for a love society deems impossible.

Prince Tamino embarks upon a mystical quest armed only with a magical flute, guided by three celestial ladies to rescue the fair Pamina from the sorcerer Sarastro.

A Monument of Art

Designed in the grand tradition of the European court theatres, La Grande Opéra stands as a monument to the transcendent power of the performing arts. Its gilded auditorium, crowned by a magnificent crystal chandelier weighing over three thousand kilograms, seats eight hundred and fifty souls beneath a painted ceiling depicting Apollo and the Muses.

The Grand Foyer, adorned with mirrors in the manner of Versailles and illuminated by twelve thousand candles, offers a setting of unparalleled magnificence for the interval promenade. The Salle des Glaces, our celebrated reception hall, provides a fitting prelude to an evening of artistic transcendence.

Capacity: 850 seats across five tiers of seating, including 10 private boxes and the Royal Gallery.
