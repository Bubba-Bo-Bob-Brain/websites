The 2025 Season

A Celebration of Baroque Masters & Romantic Epics

Welcome to a season of unparalleled grandeur, where the ghosts of Vivaldi, Handel, and Monteverdi whisper through gilded halls. Our 2025 program presents seven extraordinary productions, each performed in the authentic style of the era, with period instruments and historically informed interpretations.

The History

Founded in 1689 by Duke Alessandro Grandioso, our theatre has hosted the world's greatest performers for over three centuries.

🏰

Getting Here

Located in the heart of the historic district. Nearest metro: Teatro Centrale. Limited valet parking available.

🍷

The Saloon

Join us in the gilded Saloon da Champagne before performances and during intermission for champagne and canapés.

👔

Dress Code

We encourage formal attire. Black tie is traditional for evening performances. Jackets are required in the Royal Boxes.
