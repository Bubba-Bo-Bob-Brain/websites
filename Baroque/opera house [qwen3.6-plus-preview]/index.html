The tale of Orpheus's descent into the underworld, rendered with unprecedented emotional depth. A work that invented opera itself, performed in its entirety for the first time this century.

Purcell's tragic masterpiece — the Queen of Carthage's doomed love for the Trojan prince. "When I am laid in earth" shall shake the very foundations of the theatre.

Giulio Cesare

Caesar's Egyptian campaign transformed into a spectacle of political intrigue and passion. The Royal Orchestra presents Handel at his most gloriously theatrical.

The legendary Ballet of the Night, wherein the Sun King himself once appeared as Apollo. Recreated with forty dancers, elaborate machinery, and celestial spectacle.

Le Théâtre

A Palace of the Arts

The Théâtre Royal de l'Opéra stands as the most magnificent playhouse in all of Europe. Five tiers of ornate boxes rise in sweeping curves, each adorned with crimson velvet and gilded decoration. The gallery above accommodates the common folk, whilst the Royal Box commands a view of unparalleled splendour.
