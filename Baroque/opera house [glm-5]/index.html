Season MDCXCII • Anno Domini 1692

"Where Majesty Meets Melody"

His Most Christian Majesty Louis XIV, by the Grace of God King of France and Navarre, hereby commands your gracious presence at the Royal Académie des Opéras for a season of unparalleled theatrical magnificence.

❧ The Season's Performances ❧

I

Armide

Jean-Baptiste Lully

Première: XIV Octobre, MDCXCII

Heure: Sept of the Clock

The enchantress Armide holds the valiant Renaud captive in her magical gardens. A tragédie en musique in five acts with prologue, featuring the celebrated machine of the enchanted palace.

Soprano Haute-contre Chorus
II

Médée

Marc-Antoine Charpentier

Première: IV Décembre, MDCXCII

Heure: Six of the Clock

The sorceress Medea, abandoned by Jason, unleashes her terrible vengeance upon Corinth. A masterpiece of French Baroque featuring spectacular infernal scenes and the famous flying chariot.

Tragédie Dance Machines
III

Alphonse

Henri Desmarest

Première: XX Janvier, MDCXCIII

Heure: Seven of the Clock

The King of Spain's son seeks his lost love amidst courtly intrigue. A newly commissioned work celebrating the glorious union of France and Spain through sublime music.

Pastorale Romance Nouveau
IV

Le Carnaval de Comus

André Campra

Première: III Mars, MDCXCIII

Heure: Eight of the Clock

A divertissement of masquerades and merriment for the Carnival season. The god Comus leads revellers through a night of enchantment, featuring the celebrated dancers of the Académie.

Divertissement Ballet Comédie
