L'Histoire de l'Opéra Royale

Founded in 1669 under the patronage of Louis XIV, the Opéra Royale was conceived as a temple to the arts, where music, dance, and spectacle would unite to glorify the Sun King’s reign. Designed by the legendary Charles Le Brun, its gilded halls and celestial frescoes were meant to evoke the heavens themselves.

Today, we honor this legacy with performances that transport audiences to the Grand Siècle, where every aria is a brushstroke in a living Baroque masterpiece.
