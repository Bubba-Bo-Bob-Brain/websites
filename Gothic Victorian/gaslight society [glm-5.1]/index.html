The threshold of ignorance. Here stand the unproven, those who have heard the whisper but not yet seen the face behind it.

Those who have glimpsed the Veil's shimmer and returned with madness intact. They study the first principles of etheric manipulation.

Blood-bonded scholars who have offered a piece of their vitality to the Order. Their arts turn toward the deeper current.

Society Edicts

"To break these laws is to be unmade from the record of the living and the dead alike."

⚜ ⚜ ⚜
I

The Edict of Silence

No member shall speak of the Order's proceedings to any soul outside their circle. What is witnessed in the chambers remains in the chambers — upon pain of the Severing.

II

The Edict of the Threshold

None may enter a séance of a circle higher than their own without express invitation from a Hierophant. To do so unbidden is to invite the formless into one's own flesh.

III

The Edict of the Bleeding Quill

All records of the Order shall be inscribed in the prescribed manner — with iron gall ink upon vellum prepared by the Atrium's designated scriveners. No other medium shall preserve the truth.

IV

The Edict of the Unnamed Hour

Séances of the Obsidian Sanctum and above shall commence only at the thirteenth hour, when the boundary between the seen and unseen is at its most permeable. None shall depart until the rite is concluded.

V

The Edict of the Severed Name

Should any member betray the Order, their name shall be struck from all records, their portrait burned, and their very memory dissolved through the Rite of Erasure. They shall cease to have ever been.
