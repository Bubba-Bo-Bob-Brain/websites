Order Chronicle

"The history of those who walk in shadow"

1867

The Founding

The Aurelian Order was formally established in a candlelit chamber beneath the streets of London by seven seekers of hidden truth. Lord Alistair Crowthorne was elected as the first and, as yet, only Grand Hierophant.

1871

The First Inner Circle

The hierarchy was formalized with the creation of the Inner and Outer Circles. The sanctum beneath the Order's hall was expanded with funds from unnamed benefactors.

1876

The Incident at Blackwood Manor

An unauthorized summoning attempt by a rogue adept resulted in the temporary dissolution of the Outer Circle. New safeguards and vetting procedures were implemented under the Elder Council's decree.

1882

The Cartographer's Discovery

Archibald Sinclair mapped the ley-line convergence beneath the Order's sanctum, confirming centuries-old suspicions about the site's significance. The sanctum was rededicated accordingly.

1889

The Present Age

The Order flourishes in this present age. Membership has swelled to twelve Awakened souls, with three aspirants currently undergoing evaluation. The Grand Invocation approaches...
