The Codex

Laws, admonitions, and the sacred duties of the initiated

The Seven Admonitions

❦
  1. I.

    Thou shalt never speak the true name of the Order to any uninitiated soul, upon penalty of immediate expulsion and the Forgetting.

  2. II.

    Thou shalt attend all seances appointed for thy circle, save in cases of grave necessity communicated to a Veiled Master no less than three days prior.

  3. III.

    Thou shalt never attempt a ritual beyond the station of thy circle without direct supervision from a member of higher degree.

  4. IV.

    Thou shalt keep the Sanctum's location inviolate. No map, no description, no hint shall ever pass thy lips or pen regarding the path to our chamber.

  5. V.

    Thou shalt treat all brethren with the respect due to their station, regardless of their worldly rank or title. Within these walls, only spiritual attainment matters.

  6. VI.

    Thou shalt maintain thy personal wards and protections at all times. The Order shall not be held responsible for spiritual injury resulting from negligence.

  7. VII.

    Thou shalt submit to the judgment of the Sunless Throne without appeal. Their word is final, their wisdom infallible, their mercy boundless.

So sworn by candlelight and sealed in blood.
