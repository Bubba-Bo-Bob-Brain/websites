§ The Arcane Codex §

Sacred knowledge, protocols, and the Laws of the Conclave

I

The Prime Edict

"What is revealed within the Circle shall remain within the Circle. To speak of the mysteries to the uninitiated is to invite the dissolution of one's own soul across the seven planes."

— Enacted by Grand Hierophant Blackwood, 1847

II

The Law of Correspondences

"As above, so below; as within, so without. The microcosm reflects the macrocosm. To understand the smallest mystery is to glimpse the architecture of all creation."

— From the Hermetic Foundations, transcribed 1849

III

The Rite of Succession

"Should the Grand Hierophant pass beyond the veil, the Oracle shall divine the successor. The new Hierophant must have spent no fewer than seven years in Circle III or above, and must have successfully completed the Trial of the Obsidian Mirror."

— Amended by the Council of Adepts, 1862

IV

Prohibited Practices

The following arts are forbidden within Conclave grounds and by Conclave members:

  • Invocation of entities whose true names are not known
  • Necromantic resurrection without unanimous Circle approval
  • Blood pacts with entities of the Infernal Plane
  • Temporal manipulation beyond three days hence or past
  • The creation of homunculi exceeding six inches in stature

— The Black Codex, Chapter VII, Verse 13

V

The Sevenfold Path of Initiation

Each ascending Circle requires a trial unique to its mysteries:

  1. VII → VI: The Trial of Sight — Scry the location of a hidden object
  2. VI → V: The Trial of Voice — Channel a coherent spirit communication
  3. V → IV: The Trial of Will — Resist psychic intrusion for one lunar cycle
  4. IV → III: The Trial of Knowledge — Pass the examination of the Forbidden Library
  5. III → II: The Trial of Shadow — Navigate the Labyrinth of Echoes unaided
  6. II → I: The Trial of Transcendence — Survive the Obsidian Mirror
