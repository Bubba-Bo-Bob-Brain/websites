Bestiary of the Slavic deeps

Gatherings from the deep forests and dark waters of Slavic Myth and Folklore

Leshy
ᛋ Danger: 9/10

The Leshy is a male woodland spirit, master of the forest, who can change his size from a blade of grass to the tallest tree. He leads travelers astray, mimics voices, and herds animals. He is both protector and punisher of those who disrespect the wild.

Deep Taiga Shapeshifter Horned Crown
Rusalka
ᚠ Danger: 7/10

A water nymph, the spirit of a drowned maiden. She dwells in rivers and lakes, her song enchanting men to the water’s edge, where she drags them to a watery grave. She is both beautiful and deadly, her hair ever-damp with riverweed.

Rivers & Lakes Siren Water Lily
Baba Yaga
ᚢ Danger: 10/10

The ancient crone witch who dwells in a hut that stands on chicken legs, deep in the forest. She flies in a mortar, wielding a pestle, and her fence is made of human bones. She is both a giver of wisdom and a devourer of the unwary.

Walking Hut Witch / Crone Mortar & Pestle
Zmey
ᚦ Danger: 10/10

A multi-headed dragon, often with three, six, or nine heads. He breathes fire, hoards treasure, and demands maidens as tribute. He is the embodiment of chaos and destruction, dwelling in dark mountains or deep lakes.

Mountains & Lakes Dragon Three Heads
Vodyanoy
ᚨ Danger: 8/10

A water spirit, king of the rivers and lakes. He appears as a bloated old man with frog-like features, green hair and beard. He drowns swimmers, breaks dams, and demands offerings. He rides a catfish and dwells in deep pools.

Deep Pools Water Spirit Catfish Steed
Kikimora
ᚱ Danger: 4/10

A house spirit, often appearing as a small, hunched woman with a comb and spindle. She is a trickster, tangling threads, breaking pottery, and causing nightmares. She can be appeased with offerings of bread and salt.

Hearth & Home House Spirit Spindle & Comb
Domovoi
ᚷ Danger: 3/10

A protective household spirit, appearing as a small, bearded man. He lives behind the stove and ensures the family’s prosperity. If angered, he hides items and makes noise at night. He is generally benevolent and even mournful when the family moves.
