Index of Creatures

☩
☽ ☽ ☽ ☽ ☽

Leshy

Лѣ́сной
Forest Spirit • Lord of the Woods

Master of all that grows and rustles beneath the ancient canopy. His laughter is the wind in the pines, his voice the creak of old oaks.

☽ ☽ ☽ ☽ ☽

Rusalka

Руса́лка
Water Spirit • The Drowned Maiden

Born from those who perished in dark waters, she dances beneath the moon, her combing of long hair a warning to the living who wander too near.

☽ ☽ ☽ ☽ ☽

Baba Yaga

Баба Я́га
Witch • The Bone-Legged

She flies in her mortar, sweeping away her tracks with a broom. Her hut stands on chicken legs, surrounded by a fence of bones crowned with skulls that glow in the darkness.

☽ ☽ ☽ ☽ ☽

Zmey Gorynych

Змей Горы́ныч
Dragon • Three-Headed Flame

Ancient and terrible, his three heads breathe rivers of fire that turn forests to ash. Iron scales defy mortal weapons, and his roar shakes mountains to their roots.

☽ ☽ ☽ ☽ ☽

Vodyanoy

Водяно́й
Water Spirit • The River Father

Ruler of rivers and lakes, his beard trails pondweed and his crown is woven of reeds. He drags the unwary beneath the black water to join his drowned court.

☽ ☽ ☽ ☽ ☽

Domovoy

Домово́й
Household Spirit • Hearth Keeper

Small and grey-furred, he dwells behind the stove and guards the home. Treat him well and livestock will prosper; offend him and expect chaos in the night.

☽ ☽ ☽ ☽ ☽

Kikimora

Кики́мора
Household Spirit • The Unseen Knitter

She hides behind the stove, spinning endless thread with her thin fingers. At night she whispers and tangles, and those who see her face are marked with misfortune.

☽ ☽ ☽ ☽ ☽

Koschei the Deathless

Коще́й Бессме́ртный
Undead Tyrant • Death Hidden in a Needle

His death is not his own — hidden in a needle, inside an egg, inside a duck, inside a hare, inside an iron chest, buried beneath an oak on a forgotten island.

☽ ☽ ☽ ☽ ☽

Bereginya

Береги́ня
Riverside Spirit • The Guardian

Benevolent protector of riverbanks and shores, she appears as a woman rising from the water, her hair flowing like currents. She guards travelers and blesses those who respect the waters.
