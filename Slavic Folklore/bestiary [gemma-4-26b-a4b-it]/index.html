Leshy

The Forest Guardian

A shapeshifting forest spirit, master of the wild woods. He can appear as a tall pine or a tiny blade of grass. To encounter him is to lose one's way in the endless green.

"He laughs when the traveler wanders, and weeps when the trees fall."
ᛗ ᛗ ᛗ ᛗ

Rusalka

The Pale Drowner

Spirits of women who died untimely deaths near water. Their beauty is a trap, their laughter a siren song that pulls the unwary into the crushing depths.

"Beware the ripples when the moon is high."
ᛗ ᛗ ᛗ ᛗ ᛗ

Baba Yaga

The Iron-Toothed Crone

A powerful witch residing in a hut that stands on giant chicken legs. She is neither purely evil nor good, but a force of nature that demands a price.

"She flies in a mortar, steering with a pestle."
ᛗ ᛗ ᛗ ᛗ ᛗ

Zmey

The Sky Serpent

Multi-headed dragons of immense power. They command the storms and guard the treasures of the heavens, breathing fire and lightning.

"Where the clouds bleed red, the Zmey flies."
