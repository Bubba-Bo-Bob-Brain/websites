Leshy

Леший • Lord of the Green Wilds

ᚦ
Threat Class: High

T

The Leshy is the sovereign master of the deep forests. Capable of altering his stature to match either a blade of grass or the tallest pine, he rules over all beasts and woodland pathways. Travellers who do not show proper respect to his dominion are doomed to wander in endless circles, led astray by mimicking voices and laughter mimicking those they love.

He is neither wholly evil nor benign; he protects his woods fiercely from greedy loggers and hunters who slay more than they need to survive.

  • ᚹ The Salt Circle: Spreading dry salt around a campsite keeps the forest guardian from stepping over your boundary.
  • ᚱ Inverted Garments: If hopelessly lost in his woods, strip off all clothing and put them on backwards and inside-out. This breaks his illusions and clears the path.
  • ᚺ Fire & Iron: He fears the scent of forged steel and sparks of pure, clean fire.
"We heard grandfather's voice calling from the cedar grove. Yet grandfather had died three winters past. When Pyotr stepped into the mist to seek him, the tree canopy seemed to stretch and shift. He did not return. We found only his boots, filled with freshly sprouted green moss." — Journal of a Taiga Hunter, 1842
