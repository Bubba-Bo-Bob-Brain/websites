Deep Wood

Leshy

Ле́ший
Menace
ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ

The Lord of the Forest

Shapeshifter and guardian of all woodland beasts. Appears as a man with skin of bark, feet of roots, and crown of branching antlers. He measures his power not in violence but in confusion — leading the unwound in circles until moss grows on their boots.

Known Haunts

Old-growth spruce; the treeless circle where no mushroom dares grow; boundary stones overthrown.

Bane & Boon

Offer bread with salt, speak your name backward, wear your clothes inside-out. He cannot abide the sign of the cross or iron laid cold in running water.

Recorded by: V. of the Birch Grove, 187█
Dark Water

Rusalka

Руса́лка
Menace
ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ

She Who Dwells in the Drowning Deep

Neither fully ghost nor spirit, the Rusalka is becoming — a woman transformed by water and wrong. Her song compels not through charm but through recognition; she sings the names you forgot you had.

Known Haunts

Millponds where suicides gathered; river bends where willows weep into the current; any place three streams meet on the eve of Ivan Kupala.

Bane & Boon

She fears the dried fern, the aspen stake, and honest tears shed without audience. Her comb, if stolen, grants power over all flowing water.

Recorded by: Sister M. of the Convent of St. Anna, 1902
Otherworld

Baba Yaga

Бáба-Ягá
Menace
ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ

The Bone Mother, The Threefold Death

She is the question that devours the answer. The fence of bleaching skulls is not warning but prayer — each petitioner hoping their light lasts longest in her gaze. She grinds the bones of the merely dead with pestle of rowan, for she serves a function older than mercy: she threshes the worthy from the chaff.

Known Haunts

The border of the living wood where three realms touch; moving always, found by need rather than seeking. Her hut turns to face the supplicant.

Bane & Boon

Answer her questions with truth that costs something. Bring not gold but service. She cannot refuse the properly humble, nor harm the truly foolish — the fool's protection is the world's oldest magic.

Recorded by: The Archivist (self-censored), ██/██/████
Threshold

Domovoi

Домово́й
Menace
ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ

The Old Man Behind the Stove

Not quite ancestor and not quite god, the Domovoi is the house remembering itself. He smells of bread crust and cooling iron. Families survive his temper; prosper by his favor. He is most dangerous when forgotten — then he rattles pans, tangles yarn, and leads the cow to strange pastures.

Known Haunts

Behind the stove's warm brick; under the threshold; the corner where broom meets floor. Follows the family if a coal from the old hearth lights the new.

Bane & Boon

Leave porridge with butter, never sweep dirt out after sunset, speak of him as "He" or "The Master." He protects against fire, thief, and the evil eye. Offend him and find your boots full of ash, your mirrors cracked from within.

Recorded by: A.K., passed down through three generations of household
Otherworld

Zmey Gorynych

Змей Горы́ныч
Menace
ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ

The Three-Headed, Fire-Breathing Woe

Each head argues with the others, yet all agree on destruction. He is ancient war given scales and appetite — guarding not for love of gold but from territorial fury. The earth steams where he sleeps. Thunder is his snore.

Known Haunts

The mountains that have no name in any human tongue; caverns where the stone runs with perpetual melt; any place the aurora touches earth in summer.

Bane & Boon

His three heads war constantly — trick one against another. The herb of sleep (poppy milked at midnight) blinds all three nostrils. His blood, if obtained, forges blades that cut shadow itself.

Recorded by: The last scribe of the Novgorod fire-temple, date unknown
Dark Water

Vodyanoy

Водяно́й
Menace
ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ

The Drowned One, King of the Green Palace

Where the Rusalka is tragedy given form, the Vodyanoy is appetite — the hunger of still water for air-breathing lungs. His palace of weed and bone rises from the deepest millponds. He keeps drowned men as servants, their lungs full of water, their eyes reflecting green.

Known Haunts

Abandoned mill-wheels; whirlpools that spin counter to the current; any body of water where three churches' bells can be heard on still days.

Bane & Boon

He covets living women and cursed gold. Pass his pool with a horse (he fears their hooves) or with bread baked on Thursday (the oven's heat offends his damp). His golden comb, if stolen, parts any water.

Recorded by: Miller K., whose grandfather never spoke of what he saw in the wheel-pit
Threshold

Kikimora

Кики́мора
Menace
ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ

The One Who Spins at Midnight

Where the Domovoi tends, the Kikimora tangles. She is the household's shadow-self, the work that undoes itself. Her presence is known by the spindle that turns without hand, the milk that sours between cow and pail, the child that wakes screaming of green teeth.

Known Haunts

The space behind the stove where ashes collect; the unmarried woman's corner; any room where a spindle was ever broken and cursed.

Bane & Boon

She can be bargained with: leave flax unspun, speak her name without fear, accept her as part of the household rather than intruder. Her thread, properly obtained, weaves garments of protection against drowning.

Recorded by: Midwife S., from a woman who survived seven years in her company
Otherworld

Alkonost

Алконо́ст
Menace
ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ ᛒ

Her Voice Is the End of Sorrow

One of the two birds of paradise, sister to the Gamayun. Where the Gamayun speaks prophecy, the Alkonost sings forgetfulness. Her voice is not music but the memory of music — heard once, all other sound becomes ash in the ear. Sailors steer toward her, smiling.

Known Haunts

The eastern edge of the world where sun is born; the tree at the center of the thrice-ninth kingdom; any place where sorrow has grown too heavy for human bearing.

Bane & Boon

She cannot be fought, only survived. Wax in the ears, thoughts of home, the name of a mother spoken aloud — these are shields of varying strength. Her egg, if found in paradise, grants one hour of her voice: enough to end any war, any grief, any will to live.

Recorded by: The deaf monk Roman, who alone could approach without weeping
