The Leshy

Dominator Sylvarum

ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ
Considerable
"The Guardian of the Deep Wood"

Origin & Nature

The Leshy is the master of the great forests, a shape-shifting spirit who can assume any form from the tiniest mushroom to the tallest pine. He stands as tall as the trees themselves when angered, and is said to command the wolves and bears as his woodland army.

Characteristics

  • Aspect: Can appear as any creature or human, but often depicted with antlers
  • Voice: Echoes strangely through the trees, speaking backwards
  • Domain: All forests, but especially ancient, uncharted woods
  • Companions: Wolves, bears, and forest birds

☠ Abilities ☠

  • ◈ Can steal the voices of travelers
  • ◈ Causes disorientation in his domain
  • ◈ Commands all forest creatures
  • ◈ Can grow to immense size

✧ Weakness ✧

Wearing one's clothes backwards or inside-out confuses the Leshy. He can be appeased with offerings of bread and salt left at the forest's edge.

The Rusalka

Nympha Aquarum

ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ
Severe
"The Drowned Maidens Rise at Rusalka Week"

Origin & Nature

Rusalki are the spirits of women who died unbaptized, drowned, or were murdered by their lovers. They emerge from the water at the arrival of spring, particularly during Rusalka Week (Semik), when they climb from the rivers to comb their hair with combs of gold and silver.

Characteristics

  • Aspect: Pale, beautiful women with flowing hair, sometimes with fish tails
  • Voice: Melodious singing that echoes across the water at twilight
  • Domain: Rivers, lakes, ponds, and millponds
  • Time: Most active from spring equinox until the end of June

☠ Abilities ☠

  • ◈ Enchantment through song and beauty
  • ◈ Pull victims beneath the water
  • ◈ Cause madness and obsessive loves
  • ◈ Drain the life force of children

✧ Weakness ✧

Rusalki fear fire and cannot cross burning pyres. They can be appeased with offerings of flowers, particularly blue irises, and are weakened by the sound of church bells.

Baba Yaga

Magistra Tenebrarum

ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ
Extreme
"She Who Dwells Beyond the Seventh Hedge"

Origin & Nature

Baba Yaga is the most fearsome witch in all Slavic folklore, a crone of immense power who dwells in a hut that stands on chicken legs deep within the forest. She flies through the night in a mortar, wielding a pestle as a weapon and sweeping away her trail with a broom of birch.

Characteristics

  • Aspect: An ancient crone with iron teeth, a tremendous nose, and wild iron-grey hair
  • Voice: A cackling shriek that echoes through the forest
  • Abode: A hut on chicken legs, surrounded by a fence of human bones
  • Companions: Her faithful cat, the fire that burns without fuel, and the river that flows upstream

☠ Abilities ☠

  • ◈ Mastery of all dark magic and sorcery
  • ◈ Can transform into various beasts and elements
  • ◈ Commands the elements and natural forces
  • ◈ Possesses the secrets of life and death

✧ Weakness ✧

Baba Yaga can be bargained with, but only if one shows proper respect and completes her impossible tasks. The phrases "I have come of my own accord" and "I have eaten my fill" are protective charms against her power.

§ III
Universal throughout Slavic lands, from Russia to Bulgaria

The Kikimora

Infestans Domus

ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ
Moderate
"She Spins Woe into the Midnight Thread"

Origin & Nature

The Kikimora is a household spirit, often the daughter of a drowned Rusalka or a stillborn child, who haunts homes and drives inhabitants to madness through her endless nocturnal spinning. She is believed to live behind the stove or in the cellar, emerging only when the household sleeps.

Characteristics

  • Aspect: A small, gaunt woman with long unbound hair or sometimes a chicken's head
  • Voice: Spinning sounds and scratching at night; sometimes whistles or sings
  • Domain: The home, especially kitchens, cellars, and behind stoves
  • Nature: Mischievous rather than malicious, but capable of real harm

☠ Abilities ☠

  • ◈ Causes nightmares and sleeplessness
  • ◈ Tangles yarn and unravels needlework
  • ◈ Can inhabit and possess households
  • ◈ Brings misfortune to the home

✧ Weakness ✧

Kikimory fear the rooster's crow and the smell of rosemary. Keeping the house clean and leaving a small offering of bread and honey keeps them appeased.

Serpents & Dragons

Zmey Gorynych

Draco Tribus Capitibus

ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ
Extreme
"The Three-Headed Fire Serpent of the Mountains"

Origin & Nature

Zmey Gorynych is a fearsome three-headed dragon that dwells in caves among the mountains, breathing fire and storm clouds. Each of his three heads can speak and see independently, and he guards treasures beyond mortal reckoning. He is often sent by dark forces to ravage the land and steal princesses.

Characteristics

  • Aspect: A massive serpentine dragon with three distinct heads and bat-like wings
  • Voice: Each head speaks with a different voice; thunderous when angry
  • Domain: Mountain caves, unreachable peaks, and places where fire meets stone
  • Treasure: Hoards unimaginable wealth; kidnapped maidens

☠ Abilities ☠

  • ◈ Breathes fire and storm clouds from each mouth
  • ◈ Regenerates heads when severed (unless the neck is sealed)
  • ◈ Commands lesser serpents and beasts
  • ◈ Can summon storms and darken the sky

✧ Weakness ✧

Zmey can only be slain by cutting all three heads simultaneously, then immediately sealing the neck with the flesh of a sacrificial ram. The hero Ivan Tsarevich famously defeated Zmey with the help of the Firebird and his own courage.

Night & Dreams

Babauchka

Mater Noctium

ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ
Considerable
"The Mother of Sleep, Weaver of Dreams"

Origin & Nature

Babauchka is the grandmother of sleep and dreams, an ancient spirit who appears as a moth-like crone hovering over sleeping children. She weaves dreams into the minds of mortals, sending pleasant visions to the good and nightmares to the wicked.

Characteristics

  • Aspect: A giant moth with the face of a weathered crone, or a shadowy grandmother figure
  • Voice: A soft humming that induces sleep; whispers in dreams
  • Domain: The realm of sleep, dreams, and the space between waking and rest
  • Appearance: Most often seen by children at the foot of the bed

☠ Abilities ☠

  • ◈ Weaves both blessed dreams and terrible nightmares
  • ◈ Induces deep, inescapable sleep
  • ◈ Can show visions of past and future in dreams
  • ◈ Children especially susceptible to her influence

✧ Weakness ✧

Iron and salt at the bedside ward off Babauchka's darker dreams. Speaking her name before sleep invites her attention, for good or ill. The good are advised to pray before bed; Babauchka respects the devout.

Hearth & Home

❧

The Domovoi

Genius Domesticus

ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ ᛋ
Benign
"The Faithful Guardian of the Hearth"

Origin & Nature

The Domovoi is the beloved household spirit of Slavic homes, the guardian of the family and keeper of the hearth. He is the spirit of the ancestral home, bound to the family line and dwelling behind the stove or beneath the threshold. He cares for the home and its inhabitants as a devoted servant of the family.

Characteristics

  • Aspect: A small, shaggy figure with a long beard, glowing eyes, or a kindly old man
  • Voice: Creaking, knocking, and shuffling sounds; occasionally whispers warnings
  • Domain: The home, especially the stove, threshold, and cellar
  • Companion: Often seen with a cat, his faithful ally

✦ Blessings ✦

  • ◈ Protects the home from misfortune and evil spirits
  • ◈ Warns of danger through signs and omens
  • ◈ Brings good fortune and prosperity
  • ◈ Maintains harmony among family members

✧ Weakness ✧

The Domovoi is offended by disorder, quarrels, and impiety. Moving the stove or renovating without ceremony may anger him. Offerings of kutina (sweet rice pudding) on holidays keep him content and protective.

Universal household belief across all Slavic peoples
