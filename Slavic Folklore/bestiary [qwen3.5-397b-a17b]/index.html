Leshy

The Forest Guardian

Master of the taiga, shapeshifter of bark and bone. He leads travelers astray with whistles and laughter, only to return them if offered salt and bread. Do not wear your clothes inside out in his domain.

Trickster Shapeshifter

Rusalka

The Drowned Maiden

Spirits of unmated women who died tragic deaths. They dwell in the depths, combing their hair with silver combs. Their beauty is a lure; their touch brings a drowning madness.

Siren Vengeful

Baba Yaga

The Bone Leg

She flies in a mortar, steering with a pestle. Neither good nor evil, she is the wild chaos of nature. She may eat you, or she may grant you the fire of life, depending on your manners.

Witch Oracle

Zmey

The Fire Breather

A dragon of immense power, often possessing three heads that regenerate if cut. It kidnaps princesses and hoards gold, requiring a hero of pure heart (and sharp sword) to defeat.

Dragon Catastrophic

Domovoy

The House Spirit

The guardian of the home, living behind the stove. If kept happy with milk and bread, he protects the family. If angered, he brings misfortune and fire.

Guardian Domestic
