Leshy

Danger:
⚠️⚠️⚠️⚪⚪

The Leshy is the lord of the forest, a shapeshifting spirit who protects woodland creatures and trees. He appears as a tall man with a beard of leaves and moss, sometimes taking the form of animals.

Habitat

Deep, ancient forests

Offerings

Honey, mushrooms, berries

Protection

Respect nature, leave offerings

Weakness

Clothing worn inside-out

"He who enters the forest without respect shall find himself lost forever."

Rusalka

Danger:
⚠️⚠️⚠️⚠️⚪

A water nymph born from the soul of a young woman who died tragically, usually by drowning. Rusalkas inhabit lakes, rivers, and ponds, luring men to their deaths with enchanting songs.

Habitat

Still waters, especially at twilight

Offerings

Flowers, ribbons, jewelry

Protection

Cross yourself, wear red thread

Weakness

Iron, religious symbols

"Her song is sweet as honey, but her embrace is cold as death."

Baba Yaga

Danger:
⚠️⚠️⚠️⚠️⚠️

An ancient witch who flies through the skies in a mortar, living in a hut that stands on chicken legs. She is neither wholly good nor evil, often testing the worthiness of those who seek her aid.

Habitat

Deep forest clearing, bone-legged hut

Offerings

Young heroes, magical tasks

Protection

Courtesy, cleverness, pure heart

Weakness

Politeness, outsmarting her riddles

"Turn around, turn around, thrice you must spin, to find what you seek, you must let me in."

Zmey

Danger:
⚠️⚠️⚠️⚠️⚪

A serpentine dragon with multiple heads (usually three), capable of breathing fire and speaking wisdom. Zmeys can be either benevolent guardians or destructive forces depending on how they're treated.

Habitat

Mountains, caves, royal palaces

Offerings

Princesses, treasures, respect

Protection

Heroic deeds, magical weapons

Weakness

Severing all heads simultaneously

"With each head, wisdom multiplies; with each breath, destruction amplifies."
