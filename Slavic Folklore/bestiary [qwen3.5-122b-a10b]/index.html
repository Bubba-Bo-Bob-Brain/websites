The Slavic Bestiary

Chronicles of the Deep Forest & Dark Waters

Welcome, traveler, to the Grimoire of Shadows. Herein lie the names and forms of spirits that dwell beyond the village boundaries. Read with caution, for some of these names are dangerous to speak aloud.

The Leshy

Лѣсъ

The master of the forest. A shapeshifter who can change his size to match the tallest pine or the smallest mushroom. He wears his coat inside out and his boots on the wrong feet to confuse travelers.

Danger
⚡ ⚡ ⚡ ⚡ ⚡
Domain Deep Woods

Baba Yaga

Баба-Яга

The crone of the woods who dwells in a hut standing on chicken legs. She flies in a mortar, rowing with a pestle and sweeping away her tracks with a broom.

Danger
⚡ ⚡ ⚡ ⚡ ⚡
Domain The Bone Hut

The Rusalka

Русалка

A female spirit of the water, often the soul of a drowned maiden. She has pale skin and long green hair. She lures men to the riverbank to tickle them to death.

Danger
⚡ ⚡ ⚡ ⚡ ⚡
Domain Rivers & Lakes

The Zmey

Змей

A multi-headed dragon, a guardian of ancient treasures and a bringer of storms. Unlike Western dragons, the Zmey can be a noble warrior, though often a tyrant.

Danger
⚡ ⚡ ⚡ ⚡ ⚡
Domain Mountains & Sky

The Domovoy

Домовой

The household spirit. A small, bearded man who lives behind the stove. If treated well with milk and bread, he protects the home. If angered, he becomes a poltergeist.

Danger
⚡ ⚡ ⚡ ⚡ ⚡
Domain Hearth & Home

Koschei the Deathless

Кощей Бессмертный

An ancient sorcerer and skeleton king. His soul is hidden inside a needle, which is inside an egg, inside a duck, inside a hare, inside an iron chest.

Danger
⚡ ⚡ ⚡ ⚡ ⚡
Domain The Underworld
