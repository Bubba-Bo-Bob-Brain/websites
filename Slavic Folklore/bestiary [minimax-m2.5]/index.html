I

Leshy

The Forest Guardian
Danger Level:
⚔⚔⚔ ⚔⚔
Guarded

The Leshy is the ancient guardian of the deep forests, a towering figure made of living wood and moss. He stands as tall as the oldest pine, his body wrapped in bark and vines, his eyes glowing with the light of the moon.

❧ Nature & Habits

Unlike many spirits, the Leshy is not inherently malevolent. He protects his forest from those who would despoil it, leading travelers astray with his shape-shifting abilities. He can grow to enormous proportions or shrink to the size of a blade of grass.

◈ Weakness

Birch wood and the making of the sign of the cross will cause him to retreat. He cannot count past three—knowing this, some have escaped his labyrinthine forest paths.

Reported Encounters: 247
II

Rusalka

The Drowned Maiden
Danger Level:
⚔⚔⚔⚔ ⚔
Dangerous

Rusalki are the spirits of young women who died before marriage, often by suicide or by drowning. They dwell in rivers and lakes, their pale faces gleaming beneath the water's surface, their long hair flowing like water weeds.

❧ Nature & Habits

These vengeful spirits emerge on the night of the Green Holiday (Rusal Week) to dance in the moonlight. They lure young men to the water's edge with their enchanting songs, pulling them beneath to drown.

◈ Weakness

They cannot cross running water and are weakened by sunlight. Offerings of bread, cloth, or ribbons tied to trees may appease them.

Reported Encounters: 389
III

Baba Yaga

The Witch of the Woods
Danger Level:
⚔⚔⚔⚔⚔
DEADLY

Baba Yaga is the most fearsome witch in all the Slavic lands, dwelling in a hut standing on giant chicken legs. She has iron teeth, a nose that scratches the ceiling, and flies through the air in a giant mortar, steering with the pestle.

❧ Nature & Habits

She devours children and guards the boundary between the worlds of the living and the dead. Yet she can be bargained with by the brave and clever—those who complete her impossible tasks may earn her favor and wisdom.

◈ Weakness

Her hut can be commanded by the words "Turn your back to the forest, your front to me." She cannot refuse a traveler who approaches on bread crumbs, though she will try to eat them afterward.

Reported Encounters: 78
IV

Zmey Gorynych

The Three-Headed Dragon
Danger Level:
⚔⚔⚔⚔⚔
DEADLY

This terrible dragon dwells in a cave beyond the fourteenth kingdom, guarding a princess and hoarding gold. He has three heads, each capable of different breaths—fire, poison gas, and scalding steam.

❧ Nature & Habits

Zmey Gorynych demands tribute of cattle and maiden's tears. He sleeps with one eye open, and his scales are harder than any mortal steel. Only by cutting off all three heads can he be truly slain.

◈ Weakness

His death lies in an egg, which lies in a duck, which lies in a hare, which lies in an iron chest buried beneath a great oak. The egg itself is deadly to carry—if it breaks on one's body, they become the next dragon.

Reported Encounters: 23
V

Domovoi

The Hearth Guardian
Danger Level:
⚔ ⚔⚔⚔⚔
Benevolent

The Domovoi is the ancestral spirit of the home, dwelling behind the stove or beneath the threshold. He appears as a small, bearded old man with kind eyes, watching over the family that has tended his hearth for generations.

❧ Nature & Habits

He protects the home from malevolent spirits and ensures good fortune. If respected with offerings of bread and honey, he will keep the household safe. If neglected, he may become mischievous or even hostile.

◈ Weakness

He cannot leave the home and must accompany the family when they move. The old hearth must be carried to the new home in a clay pot, or the spirit will not follow.

Reported Encounters: 512
VI

Vila

The Forest Nymph
Danger Level:
⚔⚔ ⚔⚔⚔
Unpredictable

Vila are the beautiful sisters of the forest, mountain, and water. They ride deer as steeds, carry silver bows, and possess the power of healing. Their voices can enchant all who hear them sing.

❧ Nature & Habits

These spirits dwell in sacred groves and mountain peaks. They are fierce protectors of nature and will shoot any who harm the forest with their poisoned arrows. Yet they can fall in love with mortal men.

◈ Weakness

They cannot refuse a request for healing. If captured (though this is nearly impossible), they become loyal wives, but they will return to the sky if betrayed.

Reported Encounters: 156
VII

Vampir

The Blood Drinker

⚔⚔⚔⚔⚔
DEADLY

❧ Nature & Habits

These creatures are created when the dead are improperly buried, died unbaptized, or lived sinful lives. They return to their families first, killing relatives before moving to strangers.

◈ Weakness

Garlic, holy water, and hawthorn stakes drive them back. They cannot enter without invitation and cast no reflection. A Vampir can be killed by staking, decapitation, or burning.

Reported Encounters: 134
VIII

Strigoi

The Restless Dead
Danger Level:
⚔⚔⚔ ⚔⚔
Haunting

Strigoi are the spirits of the restless dead, those who died with unfinished business or were wronged in life. They appear as ghostly wraiths, their hollow eyes weeping blood, their moans carrying on the wind.

❧ Nature & Habits

These spirits haunt crossroads, graveyards, and the homes of those who wronged them. They can possess the living, cause illness in livestock, and bring terrible nightmares to their victims.

◈ Weakness

Iron, salt, and consecrated ground keep them at bay. Their remains can be dug up and burned, or a stake of mountain ash driven through their heart to free them to the afterlife.

Reported Encounters: 298
IX

Koschei the Deathless

The Eternal Tyrant
Danger Level:
⚔⚔⚔⚔⚔
IMMORTAL

Koschei the Deathless is an ancient evil that cannot be killed by any conventional means. He rules over a kingdom of darkness, stealing princesses and hoarding power across centuries.

❧ Nature & Habits

His death is hidden far beyond the mountains, in a needle inside an egg inside a duck inside a hare inside an iron chest buried beneath an oak tree on an island. Without knowing this secret, he is invincible.

◈ Weakness

Though immortal, he can be defeated through cleverness. His death must be searched for across many lands. Once found, the needle must be broken—then Koschei will finally die.

Reported Encounters: 12
X

Upyr

The Unholy Spawn
Danger Level:
⚔⚔⚔⚔⚔
DEADLY

The Upyr is a creature of pure malevolence, born of unholy unions or dark sorcery. Unlike the Vampir, these creatures have no ties to humanity and exist only to spread death and corruption.

❧ Nature & Habits

They can change form at will, possessing victims or appearing as loved ones. Their touch blights the land, and places they inhabit become barren wastelands where nothing grows.

◈ Weakness

Only fire can truly destroy them. They can be temporarily banished with religious rites, but will always return. Some say they can be killed by a silver bullet blessed by a dying priest.

Reported Encounters: 67
