ABOUT THIS COLLECTION
This is a tribute to the golden age of gaming — when arcades smelled like quarters, CRT TVs hummed at 60Hz, and every kid knew the secret codes to unlock hidden levels.
Pixel art was an art form. Limited cartridges meant every byte mattered. We saved our scores on handwritten lists. We traded games at school.
These games weren't just entertainment — they were portals to other worlds, proof that imagination has no resolution limit.
