📺 ABOUT RETROZONE 📺

- OUR STORY -

► WELCOME TO THE RETROZONE! ◄

Established in the year 1994, we are your premier destination for all things retro gaming. From the beeping sounds of Pac-Man to the revolutionary 16-bit era of Sonic and Mario.

Our mission is to preserve the golden age of video games and keep the pixelated flame alive for new generations of gamers.

► HISTORY TIMELINE

1972 Pong released - The revolution begins
1985 Super Mario Bros - A legend is born
1990 SNES launches - 16-bit era begins
1991 Sonic the Hedgehog - SEGA strikes back
1994 RETROZONE founded - Our journey starts
