Potions & Elixirs

Dreamwalker's Draught

Night-blooming 3 Moon Cycles

Ingredients

  • Lavender petals (dried)
  • Chamomile flowers
  • A pinch of mugwort
  • Moonwater (collected at full moon)
  • Honey from wild bees

Preparation

Combine lavender and chamomile in a glass jar. Add moonwater until herbs are covered. Seal and let sit under moonlight for three cycles. Strain and sweeten with honey. Take one teaspoon before sleep.

Sunrise Revitalizer

Morning Ritual 7 Days

Ingredients

  • Lemon balm leaves
  • Rose hips
  • Ginger root (sliced)
  • Raw honey
  • Spring water

Preparation

Layer lemon balm, rose hips, and ginger in a jar. Cover with honey and spring water. Seal and shake daily for one week. Strain and store in a dark bottle. Take each morning with the rising sun.

Memory Moss Tincture

Cognitive 6 Weeks

Ingredients

  • Rosemary sprigs
  • Ginkgo leaves
  • Forest moss (oak-dwelling)
  • High-proof spirits
  • Amber bottle

Preparation

Chop rosemary and ginkgo finely. Combine with moss in jar. Cover completely with spirits. Seal tightly and store in a dark cupboard for six weeks, shaking gently each new moon. Strain through cheesecloth into amber bottle.
