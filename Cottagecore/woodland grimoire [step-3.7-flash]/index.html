Moonlit Sleep Tincture

✦ Beginner ⏱ 3 Nights

Ingredients

  • Chamomile flowers 2 large handfuls
  • Lavender sprigs 6-8 stems
  • Valerian root 1 tbsp, chopped
  • Wild honey 2 tbsp
  • Moonwater 1 cup (full moon)

Instructions

Combine all dried herbs in a clear glass jar. Pour moonwater over them until fully submerged. Add honey and stir clockwise with a wooden spoon. Seal with wax and place on a windowsill where moonlight can touch it for three consecutive nights. Strain through muslin and bottle in cobalt glass. One teaspoon before bed ensures restful dreams.

📜

Best prepared during the waning moon. Store away from direct sunlight. Label with the date and lunar phase.

🌹

Rose Petal Love Elixir

✦✦ Intermediate ⏱ 1 Moon Cycle

Ingredients

  • Red rose petals 1 cup (fresh)
  • Damask rose water ½ cup
  • Crystalized ginger 1 tsp
  • Cinnamon stick 1 small
  • Apple cider vinegar 1 cup

Instructions

Layer rose petals in a sterilized jar, sprinkling with ginger between layers. Warm the rose water gently—never boil—and pour over petals. Add cinnamon and vinegar. Seal with a cork and store in a dark cupboard. Shake daily for one full moon cycle. Strain and sweeten with honey if desired. A few drops on the pulse points opens the heart.

📜

Use only roses grown without pesticides. Harvest at dawn when dew still clings to petals.

Forest Guardian Brew

✦✦✦ Advanced ⏱ 6 Weeks

Ingredients

  • Oak bark small piece
  • Hawthorn berries 1 cup
  • Elderflowers large handful
  • Rosemary 4 sprigs
  • Honey to taste

Instructions

Gather oak bark from a fallen branch—never strip a living tree. Simmer gently with hawthorn berries for an hour. Remove from heat and add elderflowers and rosemary. Cover and steep until cool. Strain and bottle. Add honey as a sweetener and preservative. Keep in a cool, dark place. Sip a small glass each morning for protection throughout the day.

📜

Never harvest from the first tree you see. Ask permission of the forest and leave an offering of bread or honey.

Sunbeam Joy Infusion

✦ Beginner ⏱ 1 Day

Ingredients

  • Calendula petals 1 cup
  • Lemon balm large handful
  • Orange peel 2 strips
  • Honey 3 tbsp
  • Spring water 2 cups

Instructions

Place all herbs in a clear glass pitcher. Pour boiling water over them and cover. Leave in direct sunlight for one full day—yes, an entire day! The sun transforms this simple infusion into liquid sunshine. Strain and sweeten with honey. Drink warm or cold. This infusion carries the memory of summer even in the darkest winter days.

📜

Prepare on the brightest day of summer for maximum potency. Can be frozen in ice cube trays for winter use.

🔮

Dreamweaver's Vision Tea

✦✦ Intermediate ⏱ 1 Night

Ingredients

  • Mugwort 1 tsp dried
  • Anise seed ½ tsp
  • Peppermint 1 tsp
  • Honey to taste
  • Spring water 1 cup

Instructions

Steep mugwort, anise, and peppermint in hot water for ten minutes. Strain and sweeten with honey. Drink just before sleep while focusing on a question you wish the dream realm to answer. Keep a journal by your bed to record any visions. Warning: dreams may be vivid and emotionally intense.

📜

Not recommended for pregnant women or those with sleep disturbances. Begin with half doses.

Handwritten Remedy Notes

Jotted in the margins of my grandmother's journals, these quick remedies have served our family through countless winters.

Colds & Coughs Common Ailment

Thyme & honey syrup: Simmer thyme sprigs in water for 10 minutes, strain, add equal parts honey. A spoonful every few hours. Add a slice of lemon if you have it. For chest congestion, rub camphor oil on the chest and cover with a warm flannel.

— From Mother's journal, 1987
Minor Cuts & Scrapes First Aid

Comfrey poultice: Crush fresh comfrey leaves into a paste. Apply directly to the wound and cover with a clean cloth. The allantoin promotes rapid healing. Change every few hours. For deeper wounds, use yarrow as a styptic—chew a leaf and apply the pulp.

— Tested in the field, many times
Headaches Quick Relief

Peppermint compress: Steep peppermint tea, cool slightly, soak a cloth in it. Place across the forehead. A drop of lavender oil on the temples helps too. If it's a tension headache, rub rosemary oil into the temples and behind the ears. Darken the room and sip chamomile tea slowly.

— For the days when you can't get to the apothecary
Anxiety & Restlessness Gentle Aid

Lemon balm tea: Steep a large handful of fresh lemon balm in hot water for 10 minutes. Sweeten with honey. Sit with the cup in both hands, breathe in the steam, and drink slowly. Place a sprig of lavender under your pillow that night. This is the gentlest of remedies—it works with patience, not force.

— For the overthinking mind
Insomnia Nighttime

Chamomile dream pillow: Fill a small cloth bag with dried chamomile, lavender, and a pinch of rose petals. Place under your pillow. Before sleep, write your worries on a scrap of paper and burn it safely, releasing them to the smoke. A warm cup of valerian tea helps those first few restless hours.

— A remedy my grandmother taught me
Digestive troubles After Meals

Peppermint & fennel tea: Equal parts dried peppermint and fennel seeds. Steep in hot water for 7 minutes. Cover while steeping. Drink slowly after meals. A small glass of warm water with a squeeze of lemon before eating also helps prepare the digestive fire.

— For rich feasts and overindulgence
