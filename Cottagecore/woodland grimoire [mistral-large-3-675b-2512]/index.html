Potions & Remedies

Dreamer's Brew

  • 1 tbsp Chamomile
  • 1 tsp Lavender
  • 1 pinch Mugwort

Steep the chamomile and lavender in boiling water under the light of a crescent moon. Add mugwort as the brew cools, whispering your dreams into the steam. Drink just before bed, and tuck the used herbs beneath your pillow.

✨ Visions of the future

Warming Winter Tonic

  • 2 tbsp Elderberry Syrup
  • 1 slice Ginger
  • 1 cinnamon stick

Simmer ginger and cinnamon in water until the kitchen smells of spice and warmth. Strain into a cup, add elderberry syrup, and stir clockwise three times to seal in the magic. Drink while wrapped in a thick wool blanket.

🔥 Banishes winter chill

Summer Love Elixir

  • 1 handful Rose Petals
  • 1 tsp Honey
  • 1 pinch Saffron

Crush rose petals in a mortar with honey and saffron, humming a tune that makes your heart skip. Mix with sparkling water under a full moon, and serve in crystal glasses. Share only with those who make your soul alight.

💘 Stirs the heart

Seasonal Foraging

Spring

Violets bloom in secret corners of the meadow. Their petals are edible and carry the essence of early dawn. Beware the lookalike larkspur—its purple flowers are poisonous!

Summer

Chamomile dots the fields like tiny suns. Harvest in the morning when the dew has dried, but the heat hasn’t stolen their fragrance. Dry them in paper bags to preserve their magic.
