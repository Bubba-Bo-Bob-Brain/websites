🌙 October 13th

Dear heart,

Today I gathered elderberries by the creek where the willow weeps. The air smelled of rain and decaying leaves—perfect for preservation magic.

Remember: three circles widdershins before harvesting. The spirits appreciate courtesy.

Syrup of Protection:

  • 1 cup elderberries (dried or fresh)
  • 3 cups spring water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • Raw honey to taste

Simmer until reduced by half. Bottle with intention.

Mood: Contemplative 🌧️ Drizzling
🌿 June 3rd

The lavender is early this year. I woke to find the garden saturated in purple, bees drunk on nectar.

Made a sachet for sleep—dried blossoms mixed with mugwort and a pinch of salt. Sewed with blue thread for tranquility.

Mood: Joyful ☀️ Bright
⚠️ WARNING

Do NOT confuse monkshood with comfrey. The leaves are similar but the former will stop your heart.

Test: Monkshood has a helmet-like shape. Comfrey is hairy. When in doubt, ask the plant three times.
