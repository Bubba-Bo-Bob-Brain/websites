Magical Potions

🧪

Dream Weaver's Elixir

For prophetic dreams

Ingredients:
  • Mugwort (dried)
  • Lavender flowers
  • Moon-charged water
  • Amethyst dust
Instructions:

Steep mugwort and lavender in moon water under a full moon. Grind amethyst to powder and stir clockwise three times. Drink before bed, keeping a journal by your pillow.

⏱ Brew: 3 nights ⭐ Difficulty: Moderate
🌿

Glimmering Health Tonic

Vitality and recovery

Ingredients:
  • Elderberries (fresh)
  • Rose hips
  • Hawthorn berries
  • Honey (wild)
  • Cinnamon stick
Instructions:

Simmer berries and hawthorn with cinnamon for one hour. Strain, sweeten with honey while saying words of healing. Drink at dawn and dusk for seven days.

⏱ Brew: 7 days ⭐ Difficulty: Easy
✨

Fae's Gilded Glow

Radiant enchantment

Ingredients:
  • St. John's Wort
  • Marigold petals
  • Golden honey
  • Sun-rain water
  • Gold leaf (edible)
Instructions:

Collect St. John's Wort at midsummer. Infuse in sun-rain water for a lunar cycle. Add marigold and golden honey. Bottle with a sun symbol and float a gold leaf on top.

⏱ Brew: 1 lunar cycle ⭐ Difficulty: Complex
🔮

Oracle's Second Sight

Clarity and visions

Ingredients:
  • Eyebright herb
  • Mugwort (silver variety)
  • Blue lotus (if accessible)
  • Clear quartz shard
  • Willow bark
Instructions:

A powerful brew. Grind all herbs except lotus, simmer with spring water. Add quartz on the new moon. Strain, then float lotus petals. Sip slowly while gazing into a scrying mirror.

⏱ Brew: 3 moons ⭐ Difficulty: Master Level

Pressed Flower Folio

Violet

Viola odorata

Love & Protection

Found in shaded woodlands, these modest purple blooms carry the scent of love. Press between parchment pages for two weeks. Sprinkle dried petals in sachets for attracting affection.

Wild Rose

Rosa canina

Beauty & Passion

Hips rich with vitamin C, petals that hold the essence of romance. Gather at dawn when dew still clings. Dry petals for rose water, or simmer hips for a warming tea.

Lavender

Lavandula angustifolia

Peace & Purification

The scent of calm itself. Hang bundles to dry in dark, airy places. Place sachets under pillows for sweet dreams. A sprig in the bath washes away worries.

Chamomile

Matricaria chamomilla

Healing & Courage

Little suns that grow in lawns. Gentle yet powerful. Steep for tea to soothe nerves and stomach. Add to baths for skin that glows with health.

Foraging Wisdom

Mushroom Moon Calendar

Mushrooms appear when the moon is in the sign of the water element. Check lunar phases before hunting in the ancient woods. Morels favor the new moon in spring.

🔍

Look near dead oak trees for chicken of the woods.

Wild Berry Mapping

Blackberries thrive on south-facing hedgerows. Note your favorite patches in a leather-bound journal—wild berry bushes return to the same spots year after year.

📍

Raspberries prefer partial shade and moist soil.

Root Harvesting Ritual

Dig roots after the first frost when their magic is concentrated. Always leave an offering—a strand of hair, a drop of mead, a whispered thanks to the earth spirit.

⛏️

Use a digging fork to avoid damaging root networks.

Wintergreen Whispering

Even under snow, wintergreen persists. Its leaves contain methyl salicylate—a natural pain reliever. Harvest only a few leaves from each plant, never more than a third.

❄️

Crush leaves to release the minty aroma for identification.

Handwritten Remedy Notes

14th Moon, Year of the Oak

To soothe anxious hearts:

Steep passionflower and lemon balm in warm (not boiling) water for 10 minutes. Add honey and a squeeze of lemon. Drink while watching the sunset. Repeat for three evenings.

— A.

28th Moon, Year of the Oak

For waning moon wound salve:

Infuse yarrow and plantain in olive oil for 13 days, shaking each sunrise and sunset. Strain, add beeswax (1:4 ratio). Cool in moonlit jars. Apply to cuts and bruises with a clean cloth.

— A.

3rd Moon, Year of the Oak

Cold & flu tea blend:

Equal parts elderflower, elderberry, yarrow, and mint. A pinch of cayenne for circulation. Steep 15 minutes. Drink hot with honey and rest. This combination has served my family for generations.

— Grandmother Willow

17th Moon, Year of the Oak

Beauty moon water:

Place rose quartz and amethyst in a glass jar with rose petals and a few drops of lavender oil. Fill with spring water. Leave under full moon overnight. Use as a facial mist for radiant skin.

— A.
