Potent Potions & Brews

Elixirs distilled from moonlight and morning dew

Moonpetal Sleep Tonic

Celestial Brew

A gentle draft that invites peaceful slumber and vivid dreams of silver-lit forests.

Key Ingredients:

  • Moonpetal flowers, gathered at midnight
  • Chamomile steeped in starlight water
  • A whisper of lavender essence
  • Three drops of honey from dream-bees

Brewing Method:

Heat the starlight water until it shimmers. Add moonpetals and let steep while the clock strikes thirteen. Stir widdershins three times, then add chamomile. Whisper your intention into the steam. Finally, drizzle honey in a figure-eight pattern.

Sweet Dreams Best taken at twilight

Emberheart Warming Cordial

Hearth Fire Tincture

Warms the spirit from within, perfect for cold winter evenings by the fireside.

Key Ingredients:

  • Dried ginger root, sliced thin
  • Cinnamon bark from the old grove
  • Orange peel dried in autumn sun
  • Raw honey from summer bees
  • A dash of cayenne (sparingly!)

Brewing Method:

Simmer all ingredients in moonwater for one hour. The mixture should turn golden-amber. Strain through muslin while still warm. Bottle in amber glass and age for seven days near the hearth.

Inner Warmth A spoonful calms the chill

Thornveil Protective Charm

Guardian's Brew

Creates an invisible shield of sage and hawthorn, warding against ill winds and wandering spirits.

Key Ingredients:

  • White sage leaves, sustainably gathered
  • Hawthorn berries at peak ripeness
  • Saint John's Wort in full bloom
  • River stones kept in moonlight

Brewing Method:

Create a sacred circle facing east. Place all ingredients in a copper cauldron. Add spring water collected at dawn. Simmer while reciting protective verses. Allow to cool naturally, then strain under starlight.

Protection Renew each new moon

Verdant Vitality Elixir

Forest Essence

Harness the life force of the ancient forest. Restores energy and brings radiant health.

Key Ingredients:

  • Nettle leaves, young and vibrant
  • Spirit essence of pine needles
  • Dandelion root, roasted golden
  • Alfalfa for verdant strength
  • Lemon verbena for brightness

Brewing Method:

Harvest ingredients at dawn when dew still clings. Gently bruise leaves to release their essence. Cover with cool spring water and let infuse for twelve hours. Add roasted dandelion root and simmer lightly. Strain and preserve with apple cider vinegar.

Energy & Health Morning sips bring vigor

Pressed Flower Collection

Beauty captured between pages, secrets preserved in time

Wild Rose

Rosa canina

The rose carries love in every petal. Pressed between pages of old journals, it retains its gentle fragrance for years. Said to attract good fortune when carried in a locket.

♥ Love ☀ Joy ✧ Protection

English Lavender

Lavandula angustifolia

The herb of devotion and serenity. Ancient Romans used it to scent their baths. Place beneath your pillow for dreamful sleep, or in sachets to bless the home with peace.

☾ Peace ✧ Purification ♡ Sleep

Sweet Violet

Viola odorata

The violet symbolizes modesty and spiritual awakening. Violet water was favored by queens for its subtle beauty. Carry to attract love, or plant at thresholds for blessing.

♥ Love ✧ Wisdom ☽ Dreams

Common Daisy

Bellis perennis

The innocent daisy brings joy to any arrangement. Associated with motherhood and childbirth in old traditions. Weave into crowns for midsummer celebrations.

Seasonal Foraging Guide

Walking with the wheel of the year

Wild Nettle

Early Spring

Young shoots are tender and nutritious. Wear gloves when harvesting! Steam like spinach or brew into energizing tea.

Violet Blossoms

March - April

Gather for soothing syrups and crystallized decorations. The leaves make a gentle tea for cold relief.

Dandelion Greens

Spring - Fall

The entire plant is edible! Greens before flowering, roots roasted after. A true survival superfood.

Cleavers (Goosegrass)

April - June

Perfect for lymphatic cleansing tonics. Stick to your clothes like magic! Makes a refreshing spring beverage.

Elderflower

June - July

Make magical cordials and fritters. The scent is intoxicating! Always harvest sustainably, leaving plenty for the spirits of the tree.

Wild Chamomile

July - August

The apple-scented calm of chamomile. Identify by crushing between fingers - you'll smell the difference! Perfect for sleep teas.

Rose Petals

June - August

Gather unsprayed blooms for rose water, jams, and heart-opening syrups. The rose is the flower of love itself.

Wild Berries

July - September

Blackberries, raspberries, and currants abundant in hedgerows. Make tinctures, jams, and antioxidant-rich elixirs.

Elderberries

August - September

Powerful immune boosters! Make syrups that ward off winter colds. Never eat raw - always cook thoroughly.

Hawthorn Berries

September - October

The tree of the heart. Heart tonic and blood pressure regulator. Make into preserves or drying for winter teas.

Rosehips

October - November

Nature's vitamin C! Hips must be soft after frost. Make syrups, teas, and immune-supporting preserves.

Pine Needles

Year-round

Make soothing pine needle tea (not during pregnancy!). High in vitamin C and wonderful for respiratory support.

Evergreen Boughs

Winter Months

Cedar, pine, and fir for smudging and respiratory steams. Bring the forest indoors during the quiet months.

Dried Citrus Peels

Stored from Autumn

Save peels from autumn harvests. Brighten winter teas, add to potpourri, and use in protective rituals.

Bark & Buds

Late Winter

Willow bark for pain relief (nature's aspirin!). Birch buds for skin preparations. Always harvest mindfully.

Planning Season

Deep Winter

Study seed catalogs, repair tools, organize dried stores, and dream of spring gardens. The witch rests, but planning continues.

Handwritten Remedy Notes

Scraps of wisdom from my personal grimoire

A note on feverfew...

"For the aching head and fever's grip, chew three leaves of feverfew on an empty stomach. Bitter it may be, but relief comes swift as a witch's curse. My grandmother swore by this cure, and so do I."

- W.W.

Mint for digestion...

"When the belly rebels and gassiness blooms, peppermint comes to the rescue. Steep fresh leaves in hot water for five minutes. Sip slowly while sitting by the window, watching the clouds drift by."

- W.W.

On valerian root...

"Valerian root, dried and powdered, added to warm milk before bed. The root smells rather foul, they say like old socks, but sleep comes like a gentle wave."

- W.W.

Elderflower water...

"Soak elderflowers in rainwater for three days in the sun. Strain and bottle. This water heals burns and soothes tired eyes. Keep a bottle in the cottage always!"

- W.W.

Turmeric golden milk...

"Warm milk with turmeric, ginger, cinnamon, and honey. A golden embrace for aching joints and weary souls. Drink while wrapped in your grandmother's shawl."

- W.W.

A prayer for planting...

"Great Mother Earth, accept these seeds into your loving embrace. May they root deep and grow strong. May we tend them with gentle hands and grateful hearts. Blessed be."

- W.W.
