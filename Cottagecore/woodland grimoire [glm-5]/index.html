Dreamwalker's Draught

For peaceful slumber

Ingredients:

  • Valerian root - 3 pinches
  • Dried chamomile - 1 handful
  • Moon-petal lavender - 7 buds
  • Starlight honey - 2 drops

Steep herbs in water warmed by evening fire. Stir thrice widdershins as the moon rises. Let cool beneath starlight for one hour. Drink before sleep for dreams of gentle meadows.

Healer's Meadow Salve

For wounds and weariness

Ingredients:

  • Calendula petals - 1 cup
  • Plantain leaves - fresh gathered
  • Beeswax - 2 ounces
  • Olive oil - infused with morning sun

Infuse oil with herbs over seven sunrises. Strain through cheesecloth at dawn. Melt wax in double cauldron, blend with oil. Pour into tins while whispering healing charms.

☀ Sun's Peak

Braveheart Cordial

For courage in dark times

Ingredients:

  • St. John's Wort - 5 sprigs
  • Ginger root - sliced thin
  • Rosemary sprigs - from oldest bush
  • Raw honey - from brave bees

Harvest St. John's Wort at noon when shadows are smallest. Combine with ginger and rosemary in brandy base. Let rest 28 days through full moon cycle. Sip when heart trembles.

Self-Love Syrup

For inner warmth

Ingredients:

  • Rose petals - dried at dawn
  • Vanilla bean - split lengthwise
  • Cinnamon stick - broken in two
  • Self-written kindness - on paper

Simmer roses in spring water. Add vanilla and cinnamon. When cooled, write one thing you love about yourself on paper, burn it, and stir the ash into your syrup. Sweeten to taste.

For the Common Cold

Dear Sister Witch,

When the winter chill finds its way into your bones, prepare this warming brew:

Take fresh ginger, sliced thin as moth wings. Add juice of one lemon, and honey from your kindest hive. A pinch of cayenne to warm from within. Steep in near-boiling water, covered, for ten minutes.

Drink hot, wrapped in wool, by the fire's glow.

Yours in warmth,
Mother Hedgewood

Anxiety Tincture

To quiet the troubled heart:

Mix equal parts tincture of:
— Lemon balm (for gentle calm)
— Passionflower (for circling thoughts)
— Oat tops (for frayed nerves)

Take 30 drops in water, thrice daily, until peace returns. Best made under a waning moon.

— Gran's Recipe, 1952

Sleep Sachet

For those who cannot find rest:

Sew a small pouch of natural linen. Fill with:
— Dried lavender buds (1 part)
— Hops flowers (1 part)
— Chamomile (1 part)
— A small piece of moonstone

Place beneath your pillow. Refresh monthly under new moon.

— Auntie Sage
