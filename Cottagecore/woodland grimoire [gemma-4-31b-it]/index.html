Moonlight Dew Elixir

A shimmering tonic to grant the drinker clarity of sight during the new moon.

  • 3 drops of Silver-leaf nectar
  • 1 pinch of crushed stardust
  • A handful of night-blooming jasmine

Steep the jasmine in a crystal vial under the direct light of a waning moon for three hours. Stir counter-clockwise with a rowan twig.

"Be careful not to stir too fast, or the dew will turn to frost!"
Common

Forest Heart Tonic

Restores the spirit and heals the weary traveler's limbs.

  • 2 sprigs of wild thyme
  • 1 slice of ancient oak bark
  • Pure spring water

Simmer over a low peat fire until the liquid turns the color of a deep emerald forest. Strain through a linen cloth.

"Tastes like rain and old memories."
