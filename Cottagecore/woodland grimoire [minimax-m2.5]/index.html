Under the New Moon

For aching bones and weary spirit

Take equal parts dried elderflower, meadowsweet, and mint. Steep in hot water for ten minutes whilst humming a song of comfort. Drink thrice daily, wrapped in your grandmother's shawl.

— A remedy from Old Martha

— From my own journal
