Handwritten Remedies

Healing Poultice for Wounds

Beginner

Ingredients:

  • 2 handfuls of plantain leaves
  • 1 tbsp calendula petals
  • 1 tsp honey
  • ¼ cup olive oil

Instructions:

  1. Gently crush plantain leaves to release juices
  2. Mix with calendula petals and honey
  3. Add olive oil to form a thick paste
  4. Apply directly to clean wounds
  5. Wrap with soft cloth and secure

Note: Best applied under moonlight for enhanced healing properties

Moonbeam Sleep Tea

Intermediate

Ingredients:

  • 1 tsp chamomile flowers
  • ½ tsp lavender buds
  • ¼ tsp mugwort
  • Pinch of moonstone powder

Instructions:

  1. Combine all dried ingredients in a mortar
  2. Grind gently under the full moon
  3. Steep 1 tsp blend in boiling water for 5 minutes
  4. Strain and drink before bedtime
  5. Say your sleep incantation while sipping

Caution: May cause vivid dreams. Not recommended during new moon

Witch's Journal Entries

October 15th, Under the Harvest Moon

Today I discovered a new variety of moonflower growing near the old oak. Its petals shimmer with an otherworldly glow. I've collected some for my Dreamweaver elixir - I suspect it will enhance its prophetic properties.

I also found tracks near the stream - too small for deer, too large for rabbit. Perhaps a fae visitor? I left an offering of honey cakes just in case.

September 3rd, When the Mist Rolls In

The autumn equinox approaches, and my garden is abundant with harvest. The elderberries are particularly plump this year - I've made three batches of Elderberry Wine for the coming winter.

A young hedgehog visited my porch last night. I left him a saucer of milk and some bread crumbs. Such sweet creatures deserve our protection.
