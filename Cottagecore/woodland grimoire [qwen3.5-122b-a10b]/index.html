The Gilded Thistle & Moss

A Compendium of Botanical Whimsy & Remedies

Spring

Elixir of Awakening

"For when the winter frost clings too tightly to the bones."

  • 3 drops Morning Dew
  • 1 pinch Crushed Mint
  • 1 sprig Fresh Lavender

Preparation:

Stir counter-clockwise under the light of the new moon. Add the mint only when the liquid turns a pale violet. Drink while warm.

The Sun-Kissed Bookmark

"Preserving the heat of July for the cold nights of December."

  • 1 Dried Poppy
  • 1 Goldenrod Petal
  • 1 Sheet of Vellum

Preparation:

Press between heavy tomes for three days. Arrange in a spiral pattern. Seal with a drop of beeswax.

Mushroom & Moss Stew

"A hearty meal for the forest folk."

  • Handful Chanterelles
  • 2 tbsp Forest Floor Moss (Cleaned)
  • 1 cup Rainwater

Preparation:

Simmer slowly. The moss should dissolve completely, leaving a thick, earthy broth. Season with crushed acorns.

Candlelight Salve

"For chapped hands from the winter chill."

  • 2 oz Beeswax
  • 4 oz Olive Oil
  • 1 tsp Dried Sage

Preparation:

Melt wax over a gentle flame. Infuse with sage for an hour. Pour into small tins. Let harden in the snow.
