Handwritten Remedies

Moonlight Elixir

For restful dreams

Take 3 drops of silver moonflower at dusk.

Stir a pinch of crushed amethyst into honeyed water.

Sip under the light of a waxing moon.

⏳ 10 mins ✨ Beginner

Lavender & Rose Petals

For love charms

Press between waxed parchment for 7 days.

Whisper wishes as you seal in a glass jar.

Carry under your pillow for sweet dreams.

🌙 1 week ✨✨ Intermediate

Midsummer's Bounty

Harvest under the full moon

Pick St. John's Wort at noon for protection.

Avoid foxglove—it sings to the fae.

Leave an offering of bread and salt.

🌿 Seasonal ✨✨✨ Advanced

Grandmother's Cold Tonic

For winter ailments

Steep thyme and sage in boiling water.

Add a spoon of wild honey.

Drink while wrapped in a wool shawl.

☕ 15 mins ✨ Beginner

Handwritten with love in the Whispering Woods

May your cauldron never boil over
