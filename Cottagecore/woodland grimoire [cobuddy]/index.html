✧ Potion Recipes ✧

Brewed under the full moon with utmost care

Moonpetal Elixir

A shimmering violet draught distilled from flowers that only bloom when the moon is full. Best taken at midnight.

Moonpetals ×3 Silver dew Nightshade drops

Instructions:

Crush petals at dawn. Dissolve in silver dew. Add three drops of nightshade under a full moon. Stir thrice widdershins.

Verdant Whisper Tonic

A fresh green potion that helps one hear the language of trees. Consume only in forests, lest the trees grow jealous.

Foxglove leaves Spring water Bee balm

Instructions:

Steep foxglove in spring water for seven hours. Strain through linen. Add crushed bee balm. Drink before entering the grove.

Sunfire Honey Mead

A warm golden concoction that glows faintly in the dark. Perfect for cold winter evenings by the fire.

Sunflower hearts Raw honey Cinnamon bark

Instructions:

Simmer sunflower hearts in honey until golden. Add cinnamon bark. Cool under starlight. Warm before drinking.

Roseblood Cordial

A passionate crimson drink that strengthens the heart and courage. Made from thorn roses, it bites the tongue sweetly.

Thorn roses ×5 Beetroot juice Clove oil

Instructions:

Remove thorns carefully. Bruise petals with silver spoon. Mix with beetroot juice. Infuse with clove oil for three days.

Dreamer's Lavender Draught

A gentle purple slumber potion. One sip and the waking world melts away into the softest dreams.

True lavender Chamomile Linden blossom

Instructions:

Bundle lavender and chamomile. Steep in warm linden blossom tea. Sweeten with a touch of honey. Drink at bedtime.

Frostbloom Spirit

A crystalline blue spirit harvested from the first frost. Clears the mind and sharpens the sight.

Frost crystals Iceland moss Peppermint

Instructions:

Collect frost at sunrise. Dissolve in melted snow. Add powdered Iceland moss. Finish with crushed peppermint leaves.

✎ Handwritten Remedy Notes ✎

Pages from the witch's personal journal

3rd of October, Year of the Hare

Today I found the rarest patch of moonpetals by the old stone wall. They only bloom when the moon is full and no mortal is watching. I collected three blossoms — no more, for the fae watch and punish greed. Already the petals are curling inward, preserving their secrets.

The cauldron needs mending. Old Hester says the crack lets the magic leak out. I've applied moonbutter and three strands of spider silk. We shall see if it holds through the next brewing.

Remember: always stir widdershins. The clockwise brews are for the uninitiated.

17th of June, Year of the Stag

The foxglove is thriving this year. Too many visitors to the cottage garden have driven the fae to the deep grove. I've set out a dish of cream and honey near the moonwell. Perhaps they'll return by autumn.

Made a fresh batch of Verdant Whisper Tonic. The color is exactly right — a clear, fresh green like new leaves. If it turns cloudy, add a pinch of salt from the sea caves.

1st of November, Year of the Owl

Samhain approaches. The veil grows thin. I've hung bundles of dried mugwort above every door and window. The candles burn low on the hearth. Tonight, the dead walk and the living dream.

Recipe for the night: three parts ash from the bonfire, one part salt from tears, a whisper of the names of those we've lost. Brew in silence. Pour onto the earth.

The cottage feels alive tonight. I am not alone.
