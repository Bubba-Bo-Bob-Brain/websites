✿ The Art of Pressing Flowers ✿

i

Gather at Dawn

Collect your blooms just after the morning dew has lifted. The petals are at their most vibrant then.

ii

Prepare the Press

Layer parchment paper between heavy books. I favour my old botanical encyclopaedia.

iii

Patience, Dear

Two to three weeks minimum. Resist the urge to peek! The flowers know when you are watching.

iv

Mount with Care

A dab of archival paste on the stem. Handle with tweezers and steady hands.
