Moonlight Elixir

✦✦✧✧✧ 3 days

A shimmering draught of moon-kissed herbs, best brewed under a waxing crescent. Promotes restful dreams and clarity of mind.

Ingredients

  • Moonflower petals (7, dried)
  • Silver thistle root
  • Dew collected from fern fronds
  • Honey from night-blooming jasmine

Instructions

Crush the moonflower petals under starlight. Steep with thistle root in warm dew for one night. Strain thrice through silk. Sweeten with jasmine honey. Bottle under the moon's gaze.

Ember-Tongue Tonic

✦✦✦✧✧ 2 hours

A warming brew that kindles inner fire. Perfect for cold nights and weary spirits.

Ingredients

  • Dried ginger root (1 thumb)
  • Cinnamon bark (2 strips)
  • Fireweed leaves (handful)
  • Red clover blossoms
  • A pinch of cayenne

Instructions

Simmer ginger and cinnamon in water for 20 minutes. Add fireweed and clover, steep for 10. Strain, add cayenne, and sip slowly while the fire crackles.

Dreamer's Draught

✦✧✧✧✧ Overnight

A gentle infusion for prophetic dreams and peaceful slumber.

Ingredients

  • Lavender buds (2 tbsp)
  • Chamomile flowers (handful)
  • Mugwort leaves (small pinch)
  • Lemon balm sprigs
  • Moon water

Instructions

Combine herbs in a muslin bag. Place in a cup and pour warm moon water over. Steep for 7 minutes while stirring widdershins. Sip before sleep, and remember your dreams.
