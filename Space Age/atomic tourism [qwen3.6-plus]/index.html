Unforgettable Moments

Experiences You Can't
Find on Earth

Zero-G Swimming

Float weightlessly in our spherical pool. Water forms perfect spheres you can swim through — a sensation impossible on Earth.

Earthrise Breakfast

Enjoy a gourmet continental breakfast as the Earth rises over the lunar horizon — the most photographed moment in history.

Space Dining

Our celebrity chefs create extraordinary cuisine adapted for microgravity. Shrimp cocktail never tasted so thrilling.

Observatory Deck

Gaze through our 24-inch telescope at nebulae and galaxies with clarity impossible through Earth's atmosphere.

Mars Rover Safari

Explore the Martian surface in our pressurized rovers. Visit ancient riverbeds and the towering Tharsis volcanoes.

Lunar Golf

Drive a golf ball across the lunar surface in 1/6th gravity. Watch it soar for miles in the vacuum — no air resistance!
