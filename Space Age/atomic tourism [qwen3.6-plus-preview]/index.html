Why Fly With Us

The Cosmic Horizons Difference

For over a decade, we've been the premier choice for families, honeymooners, and adventurers seeking the ultimate space experience.

Pressurized Luxury Cabins

Every seat is a first-class experience with plush seating, panoramic viewports, and complimentary refreshments from our zero-G bar.

Trained Space Guides

Our crew includes certified astronauts who'll narrate your journey, point out celestial landmarks, and ensure your comfort throughout.

Gourmet Space Dining

Enjoy dehydrated delicacies reimagined by Michelin-starred chefs. Our menu features everything from Space Stroganoff to Lunar Lasagna.

Flexible Scheduling

Launch windows every Tuesday and Friday. Book up to 6 months in advance or take advantage of our standby orbital waitlist.

Radiation Shielding

State-of-the-art electromagnetic shielding protects you from cosmic rays. Safety is our highest priority — certified by the ITA.

Family-Friendly Voyages

Children ages 8 and up are welcome. Our Junior Astronaut Program keeps young explorers engaged with educational activities and fun.

Commander Bob, Navigator Betty, and their young cadet Tommy (plus Astro, the first canine in commercial orbit) have been the beloved mascots of Cosmic Horizons since 1963.
