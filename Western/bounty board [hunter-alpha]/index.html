OUTLAW PROFILES

— Know Your Enemy —

№001 EXTREME

BLACKJACK McCOY

aka "The Phantom"

Age 38
Height 6'2"
Build Lean
Eyes Steel Gray

Former cavalry officer turned outlaw after the war. Known for meticulous planning and leaving no witnesses. Has evaded capture in three states. Considered extremely dangerous — shoot on sight authorized.

Distinguishing Marks:

  • Scar across left cheek
  • Missing right ring finger
  • Wears black duster coat

Known Associates:

Dust Devil Jones Coyote Pete
№002 HIGH

SCARLETT VALE

aka "The Widow"

Age 32
Height 5'6"
Build Slender
Eyes Green

Con artist and suspected poisoner. Has been married four times — each husband deceased under mysterious circumstances. Inherited considerable wealth. Charming but deadly. Uses her appearance to gain trust before striking.

Distinguishing Marks:

  • Beauty mark on right cheek
  • Always wears black gloves
  • Southern accent

Known Associates:

Various bankers
№003 EXTREME

IRON HORSE HANK

aka "The Locomotive"

Age 45
Height 6'5"
Build Massive
Eyes Dark Brown

Former railroad worker who turned to crime after being cheated of wages. Leads a gang of twelve men specializing in train robberies. Has successfully robbed seven Union Pacific trains. Known for wearing an engineer's cap during heists.

Distinguishing Marks:

  • Burn scars on both hands
  • Gold tooth, front left
  • Deep voice, speaks slowly

Known Associates:

The Jones Gang Blackjack McCoy

SHERIFF'S LOG

— Official Dispatches —

DUST CREEK COUNTY SHERIFF'S OFFICE

Official Dispatch Record — 1887

MAR 18 11:47 PM
URGENT

Reports of gunfire near the Henderson ranch. Deputy Walsh dispatched with two men. Suspected sighting of Iron Horse Hank heading south toward the border. All available deputies to report for duty at dawn.

— Sheriff J. Beaumont
MAR 16 3:22 PM
WARNING

Wells Fargo stagecoach robbed twelve miles east of town. Three men on horseback, faces covered. Driver shot but expected to survive. Strongbox taken — contents estimated at $4,000 in gold certificates. Dust Devil Jones believed responsible.

— Deputy T. Walsh
MAR 14 9:15 AM
INFORMATION

New bounty posters received from Cheyenne regarding Iron Horse Hank. Reward increased to $7,500 — highest in territorial history. Posters distributed to all county offices and telegraph stations.

— Sheriff J. Beaumont
MAR 11 6:30 PM
CAPTURED

Two-bit Tommy Griggs apprehended attempting to steal horses from the livery stable. Recognized from Yuma County warrant. Held in county jail awaiting transfer. Bounty of $150 to be collected.

— Deputy R. Chambers
MAR 08 2:00 PM
WARNING

Travelers from the south report a woman matching Scarlett Vale's description boarding at the Silver Dollar saloon in Mesa Springs. Telegraph sent to Pima County sheriff. Citizens advised to exercise extreme caution — subject is known to carry concealed weapons and poison.

— Sheriff J. Beaumont
MAR 05 10:45 AM
INFORMATION

County commission approves funds for additional deputy position. Applicants must be proficient with firearms and possess own horse. See Sheriff Beaumont at the courthouse to apply.

— County Clerk H. Morrison
FEB 28 11:15 PM
URGENT

Blackjack McCoy spotted at the Dry Gulch crossing. Posse formed immediately. After six-hour pursuit, suspect escaped into the Superstition Mountains. Lost trail at dawn. One deputy wounded — expected to recover. McCoy remains at large.

— Sheriff J. Beaumont
