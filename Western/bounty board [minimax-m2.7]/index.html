📜 SHERIFF'S DISPATCH LOG 📜

March 15, 1887

BLACKHEART BART SPOTTED NEAR COYOTE CREEK

Reliable informant reports Bart was seen with a gang of 4 near the old abandoned silver mine. Citizens advised to lock doors and stay indoors after dark. Posses being formed.

VERIFIED
March 14, 1887

REWARD DOUBLED FOR "SILVER TONGUE" SAM

Territory Marshal has doubled the reward for Sam Wickham's capture. New reward of $2,500 offered for information leading to arrest. Sam seen heading north.

URGENT
March 12, 1887

TRAIN HOLDUP FOILED AT SNAKE GULCH

Outlaws attempted to stop the 3:45 express but were thwarted by quick-thinking conductor. One bandit wounded, two fled into the hills. Deputies in pursuit.

RESOLVED
March 10, 1887

POSSE WANTED FOR HUNTING GANG

Six-gun salute to any able-bodied men willing to join the hunt for the Caine brothers. Apply at Sheriff's office. Pay is $3 per day plus found.

RECRUITING
March 8, 1887

CAUTION: BURNING OIL LANTERNS

Recent fires attributed to faulty lantern oil. Citizens advised to purchase only from licensed vendors. Sheriff's office conducting investigations.
