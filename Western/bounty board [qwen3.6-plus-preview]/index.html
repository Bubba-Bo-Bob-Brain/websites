☠ OUTLAW PROFILES ☠

Know Your Quarry — Detailed Dossiers from the Marshal's Office

"Black Jack" Morrow

Alias: The Prairie Phantom

AT LARGE ☠ EXTREME DANGER ☠
Age: 34
Height: 6'1"
Weight: 175 lbs
Hair: Black, shoulder-length
Eyes: Steel Gray
Distinguishing: Scar across left cheek, missing pinky finger on right hand

Known History

Born in the hills of Tennessee, Morrow came west after the War, seeking fortune and finding only trouble. He first gained notoriety in 1876 when he held up the Missouri Pacific mail train single-handedly. Since then, he has been linked to no fewer than twelve train robberies and is suspected in the deaths of three Federal officers.

He is known to travel light, favoring a Winchester Model 1873 and a pair of Colt Peacemakers. He is an excellent rider and knows the desert trails like the back of his hand.

Wanted For

  • Train Robbery (Federal Offense)
  • Murder of Federal Officer (3 counts)
  • Grand Larceny
  • Resisting Arrest
Bounty $5,000 Payable upon presentation to any Federal Court or Marshal's Office

Silas "The Serpent" Vance

Alias: The River Rat

AT LARGE ⚠ HIGH DANGER ⚠
Age: 29
Height: 5'9"
Weight: 155 lbs
Hair: Dark brown, closely cropped
Eyes: Dark Brown
Distinguishing: Snake tattoo coiled around left forearm, speaks with a Southern drawl

Known History

Vance earned his moniker through his uncanny ability to slip through any trap set for him. A master of disguise and misdirection, he has operated along the Rio Grande for three years, targeting stagecoaches carrying payroll shipments.

He is also suspected of running a sophisticated counterfeiting operation, flooding frontier towns with false silver dollars. His gang is believed to consist of no more than four men, all fiercely loyal.

Wanted For

  • Stagecoach Robbery (6 counts)
  • Counterfeiting U.S. Currency
  • Conspiracy
  • Assault with Deadly Weapon
Bounty $3,500 Payable upon presentation to any Federal Court or Marshal's Office

Martha "Calamity" Cross

Alias: The Prairie Rose

AT LARGE ⚡ ARMED & DANGEROUS ⚡
Age: 27
Height: 5'6"
Weight: 130 lbs
Hair: Auburn, worn in braids
Eyes: Green
Distinguishing: Burn scar on right shoulder, expert horsewoman, never seen without her rifle

Known History

Martha Cross rode with Quantrill's Raiders as a teenager before striking out on her own. She is one of the most capable riders in the territory and can hit a silver dollar at fifty paces with her Sharps rifle.

She escaped from the Ford County jail in 1878 by fashioning a key from a nail and has been evading capture ever since. She is known to frequent the saloons of Dodge City under assumed names.

Wanted For

  • Horse Theft (8 counts)
  • Bank Robbery (2 counts)
  • Escape from Custody
  • Impersonating a Federal Officer
Bounty $2,000 Payable upon presentation to any Federal Court or Marshal's Office

📜 SHERIFF'S DISPATCH LOG 📜

Official Communications from the Marshal's Office — All Entries Sealed & Recorded

March 12, 1880 0600 Hours URGENT
From: Deputy Marshal Ezekiel Thorn, Dodge City Station

Sighted "Black Jack" Morrow riding south along the Santa Fe Trail, estimated 3 men accompanying. Heading toward the New Mexico border. Requesting additional riders from Fort Union. Morrow is traveling light — likely planning another train holdup within the week.

RECEIVED & LOGGED
March 8, 1880 1430 Hours STANDARD
From: Sheriff Wyatt Earp, Tombstone

Martha Cross was spotted at the Oriental Saloon on the night of March 6th. She fled on horseback when recognized by a local rancher. Currently believed to be heading toward the Dragoon Mountains. A posse of 5 men has been dispatched in pursuit.

IN PROGRESS
February 28, 1880 2100 Hours CRITICAL
From: U.S. Marshal Crawford, Fort Smith

Jedediah Graves and his gang struck the Iron Mountain Express outside Little Rock. Two guards killed, approximately $12,000 in payroll taken. Graves was described as "laughing as he rode away." All available marshals in the Arkansas and Missouri territories are to converge on the Ozarks. Dead or alive — preference for dead at this point.

ALL POINTS BULLETIN
February 15, 1880 0930 Hours STANDARD
From: Texas Ranger Captain McNelly

The Dalton twins have been spotted near Abilene, rustling cattle from the King Ranch. They are moving north with a herd of approximately 40 head. Recommend setting up a roadblock on the Chisholm Trail. The twins are not to be approached alone — they travel with at least two armed associates.

FORWARDED TO RANGERS
February 1, 1880 1700 Hours STANDARD
From: Telegraph Office, Santa Fe

Silas Vance was seen purchasing supplies in Albuquerque on January 30th. He inquired about routes to El Paso. Likely planning to cross into Mexico. Bounty hunters in the area are advised that Vance is extremely cunning and should not be confronted without adequate numbers.

NOTED
January 18, 1880 1100 Hours STANDARD
From: Marshal's Office, Fort Smith

General advisory: The spring thaw will open the mountain passes. Expect increased outlaw movement along the Butterfield Overland route. All stations are to increase patrols and telegraph any suspicious activity immediately. The Governor has authorized double pay for overtime during this period.

CIRCULATED TO ALL STATIONS
