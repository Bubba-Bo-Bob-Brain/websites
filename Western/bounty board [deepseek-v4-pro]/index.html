"Blackjack" Jesse Crawford

MAXIMUM DANGER
Alias Blackjack Jesse
Known For Train robberies along the Southern Pacific line
Weapon of Choice Twin Colt Peacemakers
Gang Affiliation The Crawford Gang
Bounty $5,000

Wanted for the cold-blooded murder of three railroad guards during the Sand Creek train heist. Known to gamble excessively and cheat at cards. Has a distinctive black ace tattoo on his right hand.

Belle "The Viper" Starr

HIGH DANGER
Alias The Viper
Known For Elaborate horse theft rings and arson
Weapon of Choice Derringer hidden in a garter
Gang Affiliation Independent
Bounty $2,500

Suspected of burning down three ranch houses and stealing over 40 prized horses. Charms her way into ranches before striking. Often wears a green velvet dress.

"El Diablo" Rodriguez

MAXIMUM DANGER
Alias El Diablo (The Devil)
Known For Brutal bank raids and kidnappings for ransom
Weapon of Choice Sawed-off shotgun
Gang Affiliation Los Diablos (12 members)
Bounty $10,000

The most wanted outlaw in the territory. Raided the Dust Creek Bank and took the mayor's daughter hostage. Believed to be hiding in the Devil's Pass mountains. Approach with extreme caution.

October 27, 1885

Sheriff Masterson: Received telegram from Marshal Duncan. "El Diablo" spotted near Devil's Pass with at least 8 riders. Deputized 4 men. Will ride out at dawn.

October 25, 1885

Sheriff Masterson: Belle Starr's horse theft ring struck again. Old Man Henderson lost his 6 best mustangs. Found a green velvet ribbon near the corral. Matches her description.

October 22, 1885

Deputy Hayes: "Blackjack" Jesse Crawford seen gambling at the Silver Creek Saloon. By the time I arrived, he'd already shot a man over a card game and fled. Victim was the dealer.

October 20, 1885

Sheriff Masterson: Reward for "Whiskey" Jack Turner increased to $1,800 after he held up the Dusty Trail stagecoach. Stole a strongbox containing mining company payroll.

October 18, 1885

Sheriff Masterson: Posted new wanted posters. The bounty board is getting full. Need more deputies. Sent letter to the territorial governor requesting assistance.
