"Blackheart" McCoy

Real Name: William H. McCoy

At Large
Age 34
Height 5'11"
Weight 175 lbs
Eyes Grey
Hair Black
Distinguishing Marks Gunshot scar, left cheek. Brand mark on right forearm.

Background

Former Confederate scout turned outlaw following the cessation of hostilities. Led the notorious "Coyote Gang" in a string of robberies across the Arizona and New Mexico territories. Responsible for the Tombstone Stage Depot Massacre of '83, claiming seven lives. Known to frequent saloons and gambling halls. Last confirmed sighting: drinking at the Crystal Palace Saloon, Tombstone.

Known Associates

  • "Copperhead" Gus — gang member
  • "Dust Devil" Danny — gang member
  • Maria "La Sombra" — suspected accomplice

Sheriff's Dispatch Log

Daily Record of the Dusty Gulch County Sheriff's Office

MAY 14 1885
URGENT

Blackheart McCoy Sighting — Tombstone

Deputy Richards reports a confirmed sighting of William "Blackheart" McCoy at the Crystal Palace Saloon, Tombstone, at approximately 9:15 PM. Subject was observed in the company of two unknown males. Subject departed southbound on horseback. Posse assembled and dispatched at dawn. All patrols in the southern sector are to be on high alert. — Sheriff J.T. Holloway

MAY 13 1885
REPORT

Stagecoach Robbery — Monument Valley Route

Stagecoach No. 7 operating the Monument Valley to Flagstaff route was robbed at gunpoint at approximately 2:30 PM. Three passengers relieved of valuables totaling an estimated $800. Coach driver reports two assailants, one matching the description of "Copperhead" Gus. No shots fired. No injuries. Investigation ongoing. — Deputy M. Cooper

MAY 11 1885
MEMO

New Wanted Poster Distribution

Per instructions from the U.S. Marshal's office, updated wanted posters for "Scarlet" Jane and "Dust Devil" Danny have been received and posted at the following locations: General Post Office, Silver Dollar Saloon, Dry Goods Mercantile, and the Livery Stable. Rewards have been updated accordingly. — Clerk P. Williams

MAY 09 1885
URGENT

Bank Robbery — Prescott Savings & Trust

Prescott Savings & Trust was robbed at approximately 11:00 AM. Two armed men entered through the rear service entrance. Approximately $4,200 in currency and an undetermined amount of gold dust taken. One bank teller sustained a minor injury from being struck with a pistol butt. Description of primary suspect consistent with "Dust Devil" Danny. Federal authorities notified. — Sheriff J.T. Holloway

MAY 07 1885
REPORT

Territorial Marshal Inspection

U.S. Marshal William Bradford conducted a routine inspection of this office. All records found in order. Marshal Bradford emphasized the priority of apprehending the "Coyote Gang" members, particularly McCoy and Copperhead. Additional federal resources may be deployed to the region. — Sheriff J.T. Holloway

MAY 04 1885
MEMO

Supply Requisition Approved

Territorial authorities have approved requisition for: 6 × Colt Single Action Army revolvers, 500 rounds of .45 Long Colt ammunition, 2 × Winchester Model 1873 rifles, handcuffs (3 pair), and medical supplies. Expected delivery via freight wagon within the fortnight. — Deputy M. Cooper
