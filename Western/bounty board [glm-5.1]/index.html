Sheriff's Dispatch Log

Official record of territorial law enforcement activities

OCT 14 1883
URGENT DISPATCH

Bitter Creek Gang — Renewed Activity

Reports of "Mad Dog" Maddox and three armed men seen near the old silver mine north of Bitter Creek. Tracks suggest movement toward Deadwood. All lawmen in the region are to be on highest alert. Civilians advised to travel armed and in groups.

Marshal Ezekiel Cross Official Territory Dispatch
OCT 12 1883
ROUTINE REPORT

Cattle Rustling — Triple Bar Ranch

Deputy Hawkins investigated report of 14 head of cattle stolen from the Triple Bar Ranch. Brands altered with running iron. Tracks led south toward the Cheyenne River before being lost in rocky terrain. Suspect "Red River" Sam's methods match. Bounty increased from $2,000 to $2,500.

Deputy Silas Hawkins County Report #1883-047
OCT 09 1883
CAPTURE REPORT

"Whisper" Dan Apprehended

Bounty hunter Jeb Colton delivered "Whisper" Dan Carver to the county jail. Carver was captured without incident at a boarding house in Rapid Creek. Outstanding bounty of $1,500 paid to Colton from the territorial fund. Carver awaits trial for petty theft and trespassing.

Sheriff Tom Hargrove Capture Report #1883-019
OCT 05 1883
URGENT DISPATCH

Stagecoach Attack — Deadwood Express

The Deadwood to Cheyenne stage was held up at gunpoint 8 miles south of Sundance. Two armed men, one possibly "One-Eye" Morales based on description, took $3,200 in payroll and a strongbox containing gold dust. Driver and passengers unharmed but shaken. Pursuit party lost trail at Whitewood Creek.

Marshal Ezekiel Cross Official Territory Dispatch
OCT 01 1883
ROUTINE REPORT

Forgery Ring — Cheyenne Banks

Three banks in Cheyenne have reported counterfeit bearer bonds passed in the past two weeks. Quality of forgeries is exceptionally high, suggesting a skilled operator. Investigation points toward "Silver Tongue" Sadie. Her bounty has been raised from $2,000 to $3,000.

Deputy Silas Hawkins County Report #1883-044
SEP 27 1883
CAPTURE REPORT

"Slippery" Pete Thornton — In Custody

Pete Thornton, wanted for horse theft, was captured by Deputy Miller after a short chase through the stockyards of Abilene. Thornton's bounty of $500 was paid. He has been sentenced to 3 years in the territorial penitentiary. His partner, "Grizzly" Barnes, remains at large and is believed to have fled north.

Deputy Josiah Miller Capture Report #1883-018

Legends of the Territory

Tales of the most notorious outlaws and lawmen to walk these lands

1850 — 1875

The Phantom Rider of the Badlands

A mysterious figure who robbed over 30 stagecoaches across the northern territories. Never identified, never caught. Some say the rider was a ghost — others say it was a woman. The Phantom Rider vanished in '75, taking the secret of their identity to the grave… or so it seemed. Recent copycat crimes suggest someone may have taken up the mantle.

Total Stolen: Estimated $120,000
1868 — 1882

Marshal "Iron Jaw" Patterson

The most feared lawman in Dakota Territory. Ulysses Patterson brought in over 200 outlaws during his 14-year tenure. Survived 6 gunfights, 3 knife attacks, and a hanging. Lost his jaw to a shotgun blast in '79 and wore an iron plate thereafter. Retired in '82 after the Deadwood Siege. His legend lives on in every badge worn in this territory.

Bounties Collected: $340,000+
1871 — 1878

The Bitter Creek Massacre

On a cold November night in '78, the Bitter Creek Gang rode into the mining settlement of Prosperity Ridge. By dawn, 17 souls lay dead and the bank was empty. It was the single bloodiest robbery in territorial history. The gang scattered afterward, but "Mad Dog" Maddox has reformed the crew and rumors of their return chill the blood of every settler within 100 miles.

Lives Lost: 17 | Stolen: $28,000
