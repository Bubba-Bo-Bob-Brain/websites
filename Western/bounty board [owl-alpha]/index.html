Sheriff's Dispatch Log

SILVER CREEK SHERIFF'S OFFICE Official Territory Records
May 15, 1876 - 3:45 PM
URGENT

Multiple witnesses report sighting of BLACKJACK MURPHY at the Broken Spur Saloon. Suspect was armed and appeared to be meeting with unknown individuals. Deputy Wells dispatched to investigate. All citizens advised to exercise extreme caution.

May 12, 1876 - 9:00 AM
UPDATE

LASSIE DAWSON sighted near Millerton ranch. Three horses reported stolen overnight. Tracks lead northeast toward the canyon country. Posses being organized.

May 10, 1876 - 6:30 PM
WARNING

Stagecoach line reports increased bandit activity on the eastern road. Travelers advised to carry armed escort. EL BANDITO believed responsible for latest string of robberies. Territory marshal notified.

May 8, 1876 - 2:15 PM
NOTICE

"SNEAKY" PETE apprehended by Deputy Morrison at the Lucky Strike card game. Suspect resisted but was subdued without injury. Currently held in county jail awaiting trial. $500 reward to be paid to informant.

May 5, 1876 - 11:00 AM
URGENT

THE DEVIL's gang struck again - territorial bank hit in broad daylight. Seven men killed including two lawmen. Governor has authorized additional $5,000 reward. Federal marshals en route. This office declares state of emergency.

May 3, 1876 - 4:00 PM
UPDATE

MA HENDERSON's sons spotted at the horse market attempting to sell branded livestock. When confronted, suspects fled on horseback. Sheriff Callahan personally leading pursuit. Citizens reminded to report all suspicious horse sales.

April 28, 1876 - 10:30 AM
NOTICE

New telegraph line now operational. This office can now coordinate with neighboring territories. All bounty hunters must register before conducting operations within Silver Creek jurisdiction.

April 25, 1876 - 7:45 PM
WARNING

Dust storm approaching from the west. Visibility expected to drop significantly. All patrols to return to station. Stagecoach service suspended until further notice.
