OUTLAW PROFILES

FILE #B-1887

Black Bart

Charles E. Boles • "The Gentleman Bandit"

Age 42
Height 6'2"
Wanted Since 1878
Bounty $5,000

Black Bart was a Wells Fargo stagecoach robber who operated in Northern California and southern Oregon. Despite his criminal activities, he was known for his politeness and his poetry, which he sometimes left behind after robberies.

He was last seen near the Sacramento Trail and is believed to be hiding in the Sierra Nevada foothills.

Sheriff's Notes

Known to wear a long black overcoat, derby hat, and carry a sawed-off shotgun. Leaves poems at crime scenes. Non-violent unless cornered.

FILE #L-1886

Lily Langtry

"The Crimson Rose"

Age 28
Height 5'7"
Wanted Since 1885
Bounty $7,500

Lily Langtry is a cunning and charismatic outlaw known for using her charm to rob banks and stagecoaches across Arizona Territory. She leads a gang of female outlaws called the "Crimson Roses."

Last spotted in Tombstone, Arizona. Believed to have connections with the Cochise County Sheriff's Office through bribery.

Sheriff's Notes

Extremely dangerous. Uses poisoned knives and derringer pistols. Always travels with 2-3 female accomplices. Reward increased for information leading to arrest.

SHERIFF'S DISPATCH

DEADWOOD

Sheriff's Office Dispatch Log

October 1887 • Vol. XII
Oct 12, 1887
Bounty Posted: New wanted poster for BLACK BART added to board. Reward increased to $5,000. Citizens report suspicious activity near Sacramento Trail.
— Sheriff Clayborne
Oct 10, 1887
Stagecoach Robbery: Wells Fargo stage ambushed 5 miles west of town. Driver killed, safe emptied. Estimates $8,000 stolen. Evidence points to Dutch Meyer's gang.
— Deputy R. McCullough
Oct 8, 1887
Posse Formed: Twelve-man posse assembled to pursue KID CURRY toward Rattlesnake Ridge. Supplies distributed: rifles, ammunition, 3 days rations. Will return by Oct 15.
— Sheriff Clayborne
Oct 5, 1887
Telegraph Received: From US Marshal in Cheyenne. Confirmation: Jesse James sighted in Colorado Territory. Reward of $10,000 still stands. Information leading to capture will be generously rewarded.
— Clerk W. H. McCoy
Oct 3, 1887
Jail Break Attempt: Two prisoners attempted escape using smuggled file. Foiled by night watch. Both transferred to Yankton federal prison. Reminder: All visitors must be searched.
— Jailer T. Grimshaw
Oct 1, 1887
Monthly Reward Payout: Total $12,500 paid to bounty hunters this month. Three outlaws brought in: Two dead, one alive (preferred). Encourage citizens to turn in weapons before entering saloon.
— Sheriff Clayborne
Sep 28, 1887
New Posting: LILY LANGTRY bounty increased to $7,500. Information suggests she's operating out of Tucson with a new gang. Women traveling alone in territory advised to carry permits.
— Deputy R. McCullough

Sheriff's Proclamation

To the good citizens of Deadwood and surrounding territories:

These individuals have been charged with crimes against the people of this territory. They are considered armed and extremely dangerous. Any information leading to their capture or elimination will be duly rewarded in gold coin.

Remember: Dead or alive. But preferably alive for those marked as such—the Sheriff's Office wishes to see them stand trial.

Do not attempt to apprehend alone. Form a posse or contact the Sheriff's Office immediately.

J. Clayborne, Sheriff Deadwood Territorial Sheriff's Office
