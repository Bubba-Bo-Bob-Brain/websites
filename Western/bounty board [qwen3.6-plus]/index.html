OFFICIAL Sheriff's Dispatch Log

October 1879 Sheriff Jeremiah Holt
Oct 3rd

Stagecoach robbed outside of Dry Creek. Three masked men, one identified as belonging to McGraw's gang. Driver injured, passengers unharmed. $12,000 in gold dust stolen. Pursuit initiated at dawn.

Oct 7th

Received telegraph from Governor's office. Federal marshals en route to assist with the McGraw situation. Expect reinforcements by week's end. All deputies to remain on high alert.

Oct 12th

Calloway spotted near the old mining camp west of town. She was alone and traveling light. Deputy Morrison gave chase but lost her trail in the canyon. Bounty remains active.

Oct 15th

Town meeting held regarding increased patrols. Citizens voted to raise bounty on McGraw by $500. New posters to be printed and distributed to surrounding territories by end of month.

Oct 18th

Whitmore's counterfeit bills discovered at the general store. Five fake notes seized and forwarded to the territorial capital for analysis. Description circulated to all neighboring sheriffs. The man is a ghost — but ghosts can be caught.

Oct 21st

Deputy Morrison apprehended Tommy Jenkins attempting to lift a pocket watch from the saloon. Small bounty collected. Boy released with warning — for now. His associations with known criminals bear watching.
