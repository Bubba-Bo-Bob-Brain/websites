✦ Sheriff's Dispatch Log ✦

June 15, 1885 9:42 AM

Stagecoach Robbery Report

Received word from Deputy Carson that the Tucson stage was held up thirty miles outside of town. Three masked bandits made off with payroll strongbox estimated at $3,200. Tracks lead southwest toward the canyon. Posse dispatched at dawn.

Status: Investigation Ongoing
June 14, 1885 3:15 PM

Bounty Claimed - Buster "Lefty" Flynn

Bounty hunter Thomas Reddick delivered the captured outlaw "Lefty" Flynn to county jail. Flynn was wanted for horse theft and assault. Bounty of $800 paid in full to Mr. Reddick. Prisoner awaits trial before Judge Harrison.

Status: Case Closed
June 12, 1885 11:30 AM

Sighting Report - "Crimson" Belle Star

Local merchant reports seeing a woman matching the description of wanted outlaw Belle Star purchasing supplies at general store. Subject was traveling alone, heading north on the Santa Fe trail. Caution advised - subject known to be armed and extremely dangerous.

Status: Priority Alert
June 10, 1885 7:00 AM

New Wanted Poster Issued

Wanted poster posted for "Rattlesnake" Pete (Peter Morrison) for bank fraud and forgery. Reward set at $2,000. Subject known to operate in Colorado territory. Handwriting experts confirm his involvement in the Denver Commerce Bank incident.

Status: Actively Seeking
June 8, 1885 2:45 PM

U.S. Marshal Correspondence

Received telegram from Marshal Everett in Tucson regarding coordinated effort to apprehend "The Gravedigger" Vance Holt. Federal warrant now active. All lawmen authorized to use lethal force if necessary. Holt considered armed and extremely dangerous.

Status: Federal Priority
