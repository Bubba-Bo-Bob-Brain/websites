THE CODE OF THE BORDER

RULES & REGULATIONS FOR REWARD RECOVERY

  1. IDENTIFICATION: Bounties will only be paid out upon positive identification of the suspect's remains or physical presentation of the individual to the Territorial Marshal.
  2. DEAD OR ALIVE STATUS: If a poster designates "DEAD PREFERRED", any use of lethal force is authorized. If "MUST BE ALIVE" is noted, any claim presented with a deceased subject will forfeit 80% of the total reward sum.
  3. JURISDICTION: Claims must be registered at the town of Deadwood or adjacent Marshal stations within forty-eight hours of capture.
  4. COLLATERAL DAMAGE: The Territorial Government will not reimburse bounty hunters for ammunition spent, horses lost, or injuries sustained in pursuit of fugitives.
