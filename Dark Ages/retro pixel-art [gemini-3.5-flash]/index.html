THE CHRONICLE

T

he Age of Darkness

Welcome, weary traveler, to the year of our Lord 1348. A strange shadow falls upon the lands of Europe. The Great Mortality sweeps through cities and hamlets alike, leaving silent streets and tolling bells in its wake.

Navigate the map, consult the local physician, and discover the grim realities of feudal existence, superstition, and survival during the dark ages.

M

Saint Jude's Monastery

Within these cold stone walls, silent monks labor under the dim light of tallow candles. They transcribe ancient texts, preserve forbidden knowledge, and pray for deliverance from the pestilence.

They believe the alignment of Saturn and Mars has poisoned the air, bringing forth noxious miasmas from the depths of the earth.

K

The Obsidian Keep

Lord Reginald has barred the massive oak gates of the castle. Safe behind high battlements and deep moats, the nobility dines on salted meats and fine wine, indifferent to the suffering of the peasants below.

Yet, rumors whisper that the plague has already breached the inner bailey through infested grain sacks...

H

The Stricken Hamlet

The thatched roofs of the village hide desperate souls. Farmers burn sweetwood and rosemary in hopes of purifying their humble dwellings. Trade has ground to a complete halt.

Superstition reigns supreme; some flagellate themselves in the square, while others search for scapegoats in the surrounding forests.

P

The Silent Pit

The consecrated grounds of the parish cemetery are long since filled. Outside the boundaries, a massive pit has been excavated. Consecrated in haste, it receives dozens of souls under the cover of night.

The lantern of the corpse-carter flickers in the distance, accompanied by his grim cry: "Bring out your dead!"
