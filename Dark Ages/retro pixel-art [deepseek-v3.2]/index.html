The Plague Years

1347-1351: The Black Death
T

he Black Death swept through medieval Europe, claiming an estimated 25 million lives. Plague doctors, with their distinctive beak-like masks filled with aromatic herbs, became symbols of both hope and fear.

Their methods were primitive by modern standards: bloodletting, herbal concoctions, and the infamous "sniffing sponge" thought to ward off miasma.
