I

n the Year of Our Lord, when the world was shrouded in the mists of ignorance, the light of knowledge flickered dimly in the scriptoria of monasteries. Scribes toiled by candlelight, illuminating manuscripts with gold leaf and vibrant pigments derived from crushed minerals and rare plants.

Each letter was a prayer, each illuminated page a testament to divine beauty. The Book of Kells, Lindisfarne Gospels, and other treasures emerged from this period of preservation, carrying the wisdom of antiquity through the darkness.
