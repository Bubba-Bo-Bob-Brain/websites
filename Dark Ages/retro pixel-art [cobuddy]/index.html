THE CHRONICLE

❧

A record of the ages, inscribed in fading ink

476 AD

Fall of Rome

The last emperor Romulus Augustulus is deposed. The ancient world crumbles, and the darkness settles like ash upon the land. Kingdoms fragment into petty fiefs.

500 AD

The Migration

Tribes roam the shattered plains. Goths, Vandals, and Huns carve their legends into the bones of empire. Monasteries become the last flickers of learning.

800 AD

Charlemagne Crowned

Upon Christmas Day, a new emperor rises. The Carolingian renaissance attempts to pull the world from its slumber. Golden letters begin to shimmer once more.

1066 AD

The Conquest

William the Conqueror's arrow finds Harold's eye. The Battle of Hastings reshapes the world. Castles rise like jagged teeth along the horizon.

1347 AD

The Black Death

The plague arrives like a specter. One-third of Europe perishes. The air fills with the scent of herbs and the tolling of endless bells. Physicians flee, wearing their beaked masks.

1453 AD

End of the Age

Constantinople falls. The last breath of the ancient world. Yet from these ashes, the Renaissance begins to stir — a new age dawns on the horizon.

THE PLAGUE DOCTOR

❧

He comes when the dead outnumber the living

The Beaked Physician

In the grim years of the Black Death, physicians adopted the mask to ward off the miasma — the foul air believed to carry disease. Filled with dried flowers, herbs, and spices, the beak was thought to purify each breath.

Yet the doctor himself was the harbinger of doom. When he appeared at your door, you knew what fate awaited. His long black robes trailing behind him like a funeral procession.
