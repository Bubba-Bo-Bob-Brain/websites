I

The Royal Chronicle

✦

In the year of our Lord 1347, darkness fell upon the land like a heavy cloak. The great pestilence traveled from the East upon ships of merchants and war alike, carried by the wings of fleas and the feet of black rats.

Villages fell silent save for the tolling of bells. The physicians wore masks of bird-like appearance, filled with herbs and aromatics. Yet still the shadow crept across field and fen, castle and cottage.
