Chronicles of the Dark Ages

In the year 1066, when the sun did hide its face and darkness fell upon the land, the realm entered a time of great uncertainty.

The people looked to their feudal lords for guidance, though many whispered of forgotten gods and ancient powers that walked among them.

Plague bearers walked the roads, their cries echoing through the night like the tolling of death's own bell. Yet in this darkness, stories were born that would echo through eternity.

The Black Death came in three forms: bubonic, pneumonic, and septicemic. Each carried the stench of mortality.
