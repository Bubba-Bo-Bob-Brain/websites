📜 Illuminated Manuscripts 📜

I

n the beginning of the Dark Ages, when the great libraries of antiquity were reduced to ashes and the knowledge of the ancients scattered to the winds, there arose a new order of scholars who would preserve the flame of learning through the long night of ignorance.

In the scriptoriums of distant monasteries, by the light of flickering tallow candles, these devoted monks copied and recopied the sacred texts, their hands cramping, their eyes straining, yet their spirits unbroken. Each letter was a prayer, each page a testament to the endurance of human knowledge.
