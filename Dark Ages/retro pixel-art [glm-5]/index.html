hronicle of the Age

✺ In the year of our Lord, after the fall of the great Roman Empire, Europe descended into an age of shadows and uncertainty. The light of civilization flickered like a candle in the wind, threatening to be extinguished by the encroaching darkness.

✺ Kings rose and fell like autumn leaves. Kingdoms fragmented into fiefdoms, and the peasantry toiled beneath the weight of feudal bondage. The Church stood as both beacon and chain, offering salvation while demanding submission.

✺ Disease swept across the land like a divine judgment. The Black Death claimed souls by the millions, leaving villages empty and fields fallow. Death walked among the living, his skeletal form a constant companion to those who survived.

The Beak Doctor

In times of great pestilence, those who tended to the sick donned masks of fearsome design. The beak, filled with aromatic herbs, was believed to ward off the miasmas that carried disease.

These physicians, both feared and revered, walked the streets at night, their lanterns casting long shadows upon the cobblestones. Some said they were servants of death itself.
