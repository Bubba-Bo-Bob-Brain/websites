Odin Allfather • 7 ravens listening
Odin Allfather Third hour of morning
I have sent Huginn and Muninn across the Nine Realms. Thought and Memory shall bring tidings of all that transpires. What word do you bring me this dawn?
Thor God of Thunder Fourth hour
The giants grow restless at our borders. Mjölnir hungers for battle, father. Shall I ride to Jötunheim and remind them why they fear the thunder?
Loki Trickster Fifth hour
Oh, must it always be thunder and hammers with you, Thor? I merely suggested we invite the frost giants to a feast. A diplomatic approach, you see. Besides, I know a certain wall-builder who might be interested in our proposal...
Freyja Lady of the Vanir Sixth hour
Peace, brothers. The Norns weave their threads regardless of our squabbles. I have consulted the seiðr — the threads show that wisdom, not war, will serve us best in the coming age. Heimdall watches the Bifröst and reports all is calm... for now.
Heimdall Watcher of the Bifröst Seventh hour
My eyes see all nine realms from the Bifröst. The rainbow bridge holds strong. I hear the grass growing on the earth and the wool on sheep — no threat approaches unnoticed. Gjallarhorn rests at my side, but I pray it need not sound.

Realm Lore

Asgard, the shining realm of the Æsir gods, sits at the crown of Yggdrasil. Connected to Midgard by the Bifröst rainbow bridge, it is home to Odin's hall of Valhalla, where fallen warriors feast and prepare for Ragnarök.
