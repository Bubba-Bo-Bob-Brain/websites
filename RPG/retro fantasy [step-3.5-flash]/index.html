Background

Born under a blood moon, Aeliana was orphaned at a young age and raised by the Order of the Silver Star. She mastered the bow while studying ancient magics, becoming a rare blend of martial prowess and arcane power. Her current quest: to recover the shattered fragments of the Celestial Lens before the Void Legion can claim them.
