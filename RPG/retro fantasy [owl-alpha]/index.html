The Spell Codex

Ancient Incantations & Arcane Formulae

1 / 5

Fireball

Evocation
🔥
Level 3rd
Casting Time 1 Action
Range 150 feet
Components V, S, M (a tiny ball of bat guano and sulfur)
Duration Instantaneous

A bright streak flashes from your pointing finger to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into an explosion of flame.

Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Dexterity saving throw. A target takes 8d6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

"The most spectacular opening move in any wizard's arsenal." — Archmage Elminster

I

Counterspell

Abjuration
🛡
Level 3rd
Casting Time 1 Reaction
Range 60 feet
Components S
Duration Instantaneous

You attempt to interrupt a creature in the process of casting a spell. If the creature is casting a spell of 3rd level or lower, its spell fails and has no effect.

For spells of 4th level or higher, make an ability check using your spellcasting ability. The DC equals 10 + the spell's level.

"The best defense is a well-timed silence." — The Codex Arcanum

II

Wish

Conjuration
⭐
Level 9th
Casting Time 1 Action
Range Self
Components V
Duration Instantaneous

Wish is the mightiest spell a mortal creature can cast. By simply speaking aloud, you can alter the very foundations of reality in accord with your desires.

The basic use of this spell is to duplicate any other spell of 8th level or lower. Alternatively, you can create one of a variety of effects limited only by your imagination — but the stress of casting this spell to produce any effect other than duplicating another spell weakens you.

"Be careful what you wish for. The universe listens — and it has a sense of irony." — The Mad Archmage Khelben

III

Animate Dead

Necromancy
💀
Level 3rd
Casting Time 1 Minute
Range 10 feet
Components V, S, M (a drop of blood, a piece of flesh, and a pinch of bone dust)
Duration Instantaneous

This spell creates an undead servant. Choose a pile of bones or a corpse of a Medium or Small humanoid within range. Your spell imbues the target with a foul mimicry of life, raising it as an undead creature.

You can cast this spell multiple times to control more undead, up to four at a time. Each casting requires additional material components.

"Death is merely a door. Some doors should remain closed." — The Litany of Light

IV

Meteor Swarm

Evocation
☄
Level 9th
Casting Time 1 Action
Range 1 mile
Components V, S
Duration Instantaneous

Blazing orbs of fire plummet to the ground at four different points you can see within range. Each creature in a 40-foot-radius sphere centered on each point must make a Dexterity saving throw.

A creature takes 20d6 fire damage and 20d6 bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. A creature in the area of more than one fiery explosion is affected only once.

"When the heavens themselves rain destruction, even the gods take notice." — The Cataclysmic Verses
