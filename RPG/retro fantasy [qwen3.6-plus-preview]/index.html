📖 Chronicles of the Realm 📖

Legends & Lore from Forgotten Ages

🏰

The Fall of Castle Thornwall

In the 3rd Age of Realms, the mighty fortress of Thornwall stood as the last bastion against the Shadow Horde. For seven years it held, until the night the stars themselves seemed to weep blood. The betrayal came not from without, but from within — the castle's own court wizard opened the gates in exchange for forbidden knowledge.

🐉

The Five Elder Wyrms

Before the age of mortals, five great dragons ruled the skies. Ignarion of the Eternal Flame, Cryothos the Frost-Bringer, Tempestara who commanded the storms, Terramoth who shaped the mountains, and Aetherwing who walked between worlds. Their blood still courses through the veins of dragonkind.

🗡️

The Blade That Chose Kings

Forged in the heart of a dying star by the first dwarven smith-king, the sword Kingsbreaker could only be wielded by those deemed worthy by the blade itself. Many have tried to grasp its hilt — only three in all of history have succeeded. The last was Aldric the Unifier, 400 years ago.

🌙

The Moonwell Prophecy

When twin moons align and the silver waters of the Moonwell run dark, a child of no lineage shall rise. Neither noble nor peasant, neither elf nor human, this one shall bear the Mark of Realms and hold the power to reshape the fabric of all existence.
