Rule #1: Trust Nothing Organic

If it moves, it is hostile. If it does not move, it is waiting. The flora mutated first — every plant is a potential ambush predator. Even the water remembers how to bite.

— Kael, Wastelander #4471 Status: DECEASED
💧 Toxin LV.7 ☣ Spore Dense

Rule #2: Filter Everything Twice

Water in the Mire looks clean. It lies. Three-stage filtration minimum. If your filter turns red, you have already been compromised. Abandon camp within the hour.

— Dr. Vasquez, Bio-Researcher Status: MUTATED
🦴 Skeletal Zone ⚠ Hostile LV.9

Rule #3: Never Travel at Night

The bone structures come alive after dark. They sing — a harmonic frequency that dissolves muscle tissue. Ear protection helps. Running faster helps more.

— Scout Mara, Zone Runner Status: UNKNOWN
🧬 Mutation Risk 🩸 Blood Type: ?

Rule #4: Mutate or Medicate

The suppressants buy you time. The mutations buy you survival. There is no middle ground. Choose your poison — literally. Every serum has a price measured in pieces of yourself.

— Anonymous, Found carved in bone Status: ASCENDED
📦 Cache Found 🔋 Power: 12%

Rule #5: Scavenge Smart, Not Greedy

Old-world tech is gold. But caches are often bait — set by the Hive to lure prey. Scan for bio-signatures before approaching. If it looks too good, you are already being digested.

— Trader Fen, Black Market Status: ACTIVE
🧠 Sanity: -2/day 🔊 Whisper Zone

Rule #6: Do Not Listen to the Whispers

The Spine broadcasts. Not sound — something deeper. It knows your name. It knows what you want. The only defense is a full cranial block. Cost: your sense of smell. Forever.

— The Last Priest, Temple of Flesh Status: HEARD IT

Lore Archive

Recovered transmissions, journals, and classified documents

2147.03.15 // AUDIO LOG #2847

The First Mutation

"They told us the bioweapon was contained. They lied. I watched Dr. Chen's skin ripple like water yesterday — she was the first. By morning, she was not human anymore. She was something... better? Worse? She smiled with too many teeth and walked through a concrete wall. We ran. Some of us did not make it through the door. The door was no longer a door. It was part of her."

Source: Dr. Vasquez, Lab 7 CLASSIFIED
2149.11.03 // JOURNAL ENTRY #1204

The Bloom Event

"It started underground. A network of fungal tendrils that connected every mutation zone on the continent. We called it The Bloom — when the mycelium breached the surface and the sky turned amber for seventeen days. Billions of spores, each carrying genetic instructions for the next wave of mutations. Nothing was immune. Nothing was unchanged. The world exhaled, and everything that breathed it in became part of something larger."

Source: Wasteland Archives, Sector 9 RECOVERED
2152.06.21 // TRANSMISSION #0091

The Resistance Broadcast

"To anyone still listening — still human, still whole — we are the Unmodified. We survive without mutation, without augmentation, without compromise. We are fewer every day. But we remember what it means to be human. Find us at the coordinates encoded in this signal. Bring water. Bring medicine. Bring hope. We are running low on all three."

Source: Unknown Broadcaster, Frequency 7.83Hz ACTIVE SIGNAL
2155.01.01 // RESEARCH NOTE #0001

Project Chimera

"The government did not just create bioweapons. They created a new kingdom of life. Taxonomy is useless now — these organisms defy classification. They think, they adapt, they evolve in real-time. I have catalogued 2,847 distinct mutation types, and new ones emerge daily. The most terrifying discovery? They are not chaotic. There is a pattern. A design. Something is directing the mutations, and it is not us."

Source: Dr. Helena Voss, Last Known Researcher MISSING
