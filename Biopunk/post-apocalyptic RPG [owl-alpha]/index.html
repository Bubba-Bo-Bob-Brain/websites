WASTELAND SURVIVAL GUIDE

Essential knowledge for the evolved survivor

Understanding Contamination Levels

The wasteland is divided into zones based on contamination intensity. Green Zones are relatively safe, Amber Zones pose moderate risk, and Red Zones are extremely hazardous.

💡

Always carry a Geiger-bio scanner. Mutation exposure can be cumulative and irreversible.

Mutation Combat Tactics

Your mutations are weapons. Acid Talons bypass armor, while Chitinous Armor absorbs physical damage. Know your enemy's weaknesses.

⚔️

Combine defensive and offensive mutations for optimal survival in Red Zones.

Bio-Organic Crafting

Salvage organic materials from fallen creatures to craft mutagen serums and bio-enhancements. The wasteland provides for those who adapt.

🧪

Fungal samples from Amber Zones yield the most potent crafting materials.

Zone Exploration Protocols

Never enter a zone without proper preparation. Amber Zones require basic filtration, while Red Zones demand full bio-sealed armor.

🗺️

Mark safe paths with bioluminescent markers. They last for 72 hours.
