Wasteland Survival Guide

Transcribed from weathered journals found in the ruins. Original authors unknown. Add your own notes — survival depends on shared knowledge.

01

Water Purification in Contaminated Zones

Never drink from standing water — even clear pools carry chromogenic spores. Boil for 20 minutes minimum. Add charcoal from burned hardwood if available. The green-tinted condensate on fungal structures looks like water but is a neurotoxin mimic. It will kill you smiling.

Found: copper tubing in Rust Vein ruins. Makes a crude distillation rig.
WATER CRITICAL FIELD CRAFT
02

Identifying Edible Mutations

Bioluminescent fungi are generally safe — their glow comes from luciferin, not toxins. Avoid anything that pulses in rhythm with your heartbeat; it's responding to you, which means it wants something. The amber-capped shelf mushrooms on deadfall are nutritious and common. If it bleeds red, don't eat it. If it bleeds green, maybe. If it doesn't bleed at all, run.

Sandy almost died eating a mimic mushroom. It bit back.
FOOD FORAGING IDENTIFICATION
03

Avoiding Spore Traps

Spore traps are the wasteland's landmines. They look like slight depressions in the soil ringed by tiny white fruiting bodies. Step on one and it releases a cloud of hallucinogenic spores that make you walk toward the Bloom. Always. Carry a breathing filter rated for particulates below 0.3 microns. Tie a cloth over your nose in emergencies — it won't stop everything, but it buys time.

They're spreading south. Found three on the Calcium Flats perimeter last week.
HAZARDS SPORES DEFENSE
04

First Aid for Bio-Corruption

When the corruption starts — green veins spreading from a wound — you have about six hours. Apply tourniquet above the site immediately. Soak the area in diluted ammonia if available; it slows the mycelial growth. If corruption reaches a lymph node, the limb is lost. Amputate cleanly, cauterize with heated metal. The old antibiotics don't work anymore. Only fungicide-laced bandages have any effect.

Dr. Kessler's fungicide salve recipe on page 47 of her journal.
MEDICAL CORRUPTION URGENT
05

Navigation by Bioluminescence

In the deep zones, GPS is dead and compasses spin uselessly near the Bloom's magnetic anomalies. Learn to read the bioluminescent markers: blue-green trails mark safe passages established by the Weeping Gardens' fungal networks. Violet light means you're heading toward the Bloom core. Amber light means Rust Vein territory. Red light means something is hunting you. No light at all means you're already lost.

The paths shift with the seasons. Re-scout every month.
NAVIGATION BIOLUMINESCENCE PATHFINDING
06

Hive-Mind Incursions

The hived don't attack individually — they coordinate. If you see one, assume thirty more are aware of you. They communicate through subsonic vibration and chemical signals below human perception. Disrupt their pheromone trails with strong-smelling compounds: ammonia, vinegar, burning rubber. Isolate any group member who starts "hearing the music" — they're being assimilated. You have maybe two hours before they turn.

Lost Marcus to a hive incursion in '39. He smiled as he walked toward them.
