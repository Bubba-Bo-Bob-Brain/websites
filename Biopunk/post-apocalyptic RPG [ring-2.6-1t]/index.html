Wasteland Survival Guide

Knowledge is the only currency that never decays. Study these pages carefully — survival depends on it.

01 Water Procurement

Never drink unfiltered water. Condensation traps yield approximately 2 liters per day in temperate zones. Mutated cacti in the Ash Flats contain potable fluid — slice open the outer membrane, avoid the violet core. Boiling neutralizes 80% of pathogens but does not remove mutagenic compounds.

⚠ Contaminated water induces random mutations. 40% chance of beneficial adaptation. 60% chance of organ failure within 72 hours.

02 Shelter Construction

Surface exposure during spore storms is fatal. Construct shelters using reclaimed ferrocrete and bio-adhesive secretions (harvested from Nesting Tubers). Reinforce walls with rebar — mutated fauna can exert 4x baseline force. Always maintain two exits. Underground shelters should have ventilation shafts sealed with HEPA-grade fungal filters.

⚠ Do NOT use wood from Blight Trees. The cellular structure disintegrates within 48 hours, compromising structural integrity.

03 Combat Mutations & Bioweapons

Your body is your primary weapon. Invest in Chitinous Plating early for survivability. Acidic Spinnerets are devastating but attract Hive Predators. Biogenic EMP bursts can disable rogue automatons but will cause neural damage if overused. Ranged players should cultivate Bio-Electric organs — a single discharge at full capacity can incapacitate a Megafauna-class target.

⚠ Over-mutation triggers Rejection Syndrome. Symptoms include spontaneous tissue necrosis and loss of cognitive function. Maintain mutation count below 12 without Cortical Stabilization.

04 Faction Diplomacy

No faction is wholly trustworthy. The Gene Weavers offer superior bio-mods but demand genetic samples in return. The Iron Veil provide protection but enforce brutal conformity. Unaligned settlements trade freely but attract raider attention. Present Mutation Seals to prove your augmentation registry — unregistered mutants are executed on sight in Veil territory.

⚠ Crossing into enemy faction territory without proper bio-signatures triggers automated defense systems. Disguise protocols require Infiltration rank 3 minimum.
