Field Notes

Survival wisdom from the wastelands

Page 1 March 15, 2089

WATER ACQUISITION

The fungal filters work, but you gotta change them every three days. Found that out the hard way when Jenkins started growing mushrooms out of his ears. Never drink from the Acid Marshlands. Even filtered, it eats away at your insides over time.

Best source: underground springs in the Bio-Reserve. The organisms down there have purified it over centuries.

Page 2 March 22, 2089

MUTATION SIGNS

First signs of contamination:

  • Itching that won't stop (Day 1-3)
  • Strange dreams about THEY the zone (Day 3-7)
  • Taste of metal in morning (Day 5-10)
  • Visual distortions (GET OUT NOW)
If your vision goes kaleidoscopic, you have less than 48 hours before stabilization or... the other thing.
Page 3 April 3, 2089

THE NANITE WASTES

Lost three people to the Nanite Wastes last week. The swarms aren't just dangerous—they're smart. They communicate. They learn. They remember.

TIP: Faraday cages still work. Scrap metal clothing = survival.

Page 4 April 15, 2089

BIOTRACKING CREATURES

The creatures out here aren't just animals anymore. They've evolved alongside the contamination.

Spore Weavers Large spiders that inject fungal spores. Their webs glow at night. Threat: MODERATE
Acid Toads Secrete corrosive mucus. Found near water sources. Threat: HIGH
Shadow Stalkers Camouflage based on contamination exposure. Nearly invisible. Threat: EXTREME
