THE WORLD HAS BEEN REWRITTEN

In the year 2157, the Great Contamination reshaped humanity. Those who survived evolved, adapted, or perished. Now, in the bio-luminescent ruins of civilization, you must navigate a world where flesh merges with machine and evolution takes unpredictable turns.

WASTELAND SURVIVAL GUIDE

Essential Resources

  • Purified Water - Always carry at least 3 days supply
  • Bio-Med Gel - For treating radiation sickness
  • Spore Bread - Nutritious fungus-based food source
  • DNA Stabilizer - Prevents harmful mutations

Threat Assessment

  • Flesh Tenders - Mutated humanoids that cultivate living plants
  • Scrap Reapers - Cyborg scavengers with bio-mechanical limbs
  • Primal Swarms - Colonies of insectoid creatures
  • Radiation Storms - Deadly electromagnetic pulses

Settlement Protocols

  • Establish perimeter with bio-sensors
  • Rotate guard duty every 4 hours
  • Maintain genetic diversity records
  • Quarterly mutation screenings
