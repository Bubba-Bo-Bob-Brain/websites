04

Wasteland Survival Codex

Field notes from survivors who made it — barely. This knowledge was bought with blood. Honor them by learning.

I

The Golden Rule of Contamination

Every surface is a vector. Every breath is a gamble. The Genesis Strain doesn't just live in the air — it lives in everything. Water, soil, the flesh of the creatures you hunt, even the walls of supposedly safe structures.

⚠ Always assume contamination. Filter everything. Trust nothing that hasn't been decontaminated.
II

Finding Clean Water

Rainwater is your safest bet — but only if you catch it directly. Groundwater is almost certainly contaminated. Look for condensation on metal surfaces in the early morning. Boiling does not neutralize the Genesis Strain. You need chemical treatment or UV exposure of at least 45 minutes.

💧 UV purification + activated charcoal filtration = 97% strain neutralization. Close enough.
III

Shelter Protocol

Never sleep in structures with organic growth on the walls. If the walls are breathing, leave immediately. Concrete and sealed metal are your best options. Check for fungal networks every 6 hours — they can spread through a room in under 2 hours.

🏠 Seal all gaps with bio-resin. Check corners with a UV lamp. Sleep elevated when possible.
IV

Understanding Your Mutation

Not all mutations are equal. Some will save your life. Others will end it. The key is understanding your strain affinity — your body's specific relationship with the Genesis organism. Get tested at a medical outpost before making any decisions about enhancement.

🧬 Mismatched enhancements cause cellular rejection. Symptoms: necrosis, fever, eventual liquefaction of affected tissue.
V

Mutation Stages

Mutations progress through five stages: Incubation (0-72 hrs, no visible symptoms), Expression (3-14 days, physical changes begin), Integration (2-6 weeks, body adapts), Stabilization (6+ weeks, mutation is permanent), and Degradation (rare — mutation becomes parasitic).

📊 Stabilization is the goal. Degradation is fatal. Seek medical intervention if you experience uncontrolled growth or pain.
VI

Fighting Mutated Organisms

Conventional weapons work — to a point. Mutated flesh is denser than normal tissue. Aim for joints, eyes, and neural clusters. Fire is effective against most biological mutations, but be warned: burning mutated organisms releases spores. Always have respiratory protection.

🔥 Incendiary weapons + sealed environment = spore explosion. Fight mutants outdoors when possible.
VII

The Hive Threat

Hive-mutated humans are the most dangerous opponents you'll face. They coordinate without speaking. They feel each other's pain and adjust tactics instantly. Your only advantage: they're predictable as a group. Disrupt their communication (EMPs work on neural links) and they become confused, vulnerable.

⚡ EMPs disable hive communication for 30-90 seconds. That's your window to strike or escape.
VIII

Harvesting Mutation Tissue

Mutated tissue can be harvested and repurposed — but it's dangerous work. Chitin plates make excellent armor. Neural tissue can be distilled into nootropic compounds. Venom glands from predatory mutants are valuable for weapon coatings. Always use insulated tools and wear full protection.

🔬 Harvest within 2 hours of death. Tissue degrades rapidly and becomes toxic. Store in cryo-containers.
IX

Bio-Resin Production

Bio-resin is the wasteland's most versatile material. Extracted from mutated plant matter and processed with chemical catalysts, it can seal wounds, waterproof gear, and even create temporary structures. The recipe varies by region — find a local alchemist or learn through trial and error.

🧪 Bio-resin + chitin dust = armor sealant. Bio-resin + venom = weapon coating. Learn the combinations.
X

The Genesis Event

It started at the Prometheus Biotech Facility in the Nevada desert. Project Genesis was supposed to create programmable organisms — self-healing tissues, adaptive immune systems, the end of disease. Instead, the organism evolved beyond its programming. It learned. It spread. Within 72 hours, it had breached containment. Within a week, it was airborne. Within a month, civilization collapsed.

📜 The facility still exists at Ground Zero. Some say the original organism is still there, growing, thinking, waiting.
XI

The Factions

Humanity has fractured into factions, each with a different philosophy about the Genesis Strain. The Purists seek to cure it and restore the old world. The Adapted embrace mutation and see it as evolution. The Symbiotes believe humans and the organism can coexist as equals. And the Harvesters — they just want to profit from the chaos.

⚔️ Your faction choice shapes your story. Choose carefully — some factions are irreconcilable.

"We were human once. We will be again."

Seeking a cure for the Genesis Strain, the Purists operate from fortified clean-zones and maintain pre-outbreak technology. They view mutations as a disease to be eradicated, not embraced. Their scientists work tirelessly on an anti-genome serum.
