04 WASTELAND SURVIVAL

Field notes from those who lived long enough to write them

Water Procurement

Never drink from standing water. The spores multiply rapidly in still pools. Use condensation traps or extract moisture from flesh-fruit. If you must filter, three layers of charcoal, sand, and crushed bone will catch most organisms.

Essential — Scout Kael, Haven Outpost

Zone Navigation

The compass lies near the Crucible. Magnetic anomalies from the original event scramble all instruments. Navigate by the bioluminescent fungal growths — they always face toward cleaner air. Trust your instincts over your tools.

Navigation — Dr. Aris Thorne, Lost Expedition 7

Mutation Management

Your first mutation is a gift. Your tenth is a curse. Track every change in your body journal. When the itching starts deep in your bones, take suppressants immediately. Once a mutation cascades, there's no going back.

Health — Medic Voss, Pre-Fall Archives

Faction Diplomacy

The Chorus will trade information for genetic samples. The Iron Vein wants tech. The Bloom wants you to stay away. Never accept food from a stranger — the Bloom's hospitality has a 73% assimilation rate.

Diplomacy — Ambassador Jin, Neutral Territories

Night Protocol

When the sun dies, the wasteland wakes. Secure your shelter before dusk. The phosphor-lights attract predators, so use red-filtered illumination only. If you hear singing, it's already too late — don't follow the sound.

Critical — Anonymous, recovered from audio log

Resource Scavenging

Pre-Fall ruins are treasure and tomb. Always mark your route. The architecture shifts — buildings move when you're not looking. It's not the wind. Take only what you need and leave offerings at the thresholds. The old ones still watch.

Scavenging — Scavenger Prime "Rook", Iron Vein
