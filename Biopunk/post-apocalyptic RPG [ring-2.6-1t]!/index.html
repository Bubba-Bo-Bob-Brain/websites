// FIELD MANUAL

Wasteland Survival Guide

Hard-won knowledge from survivors who walked the wastes before you. Read carefully — ignorance out here is a death sentence.

💧

Water Procurement

Always filter through bio-membrane sheets before consumption. Condensation traps near heat sources yield 2-4 liters daily. Avoid standing water — Gen-7 parasites remain active for up to 12 years.

Pro Tip Cactus mutations can be harvested for emergency hydration — but test with reagent strips first.
Critical pg. 12
🧬

Mutation Management

Monitor your Bio-Stability Index daily. If readings drop below 30%, immediately administer Stabilizer Compounds. Watch for secondary mutations — they indicate genomic cascade.

Pro Tip Controlled radiation exposure (under 50 rads) can trigger beneficial mutations without cellular destruction.
Advanced pg. 34
🏚️

Shelter Construction

Reinforced concrete from ruins provides best radiation shielding. Line walls with lead sheeting when available. Bio-luminescent fungi can serve as sustainable light sources.

Pro Tip Avoid structures with visible organic growth — that's territorial marker for Hive organisms.
Intermediate pg. 57
⚔️

Combat & Defense

Melee weapons coated with anti-bio agents prevent organism regeneration on blade. Energy weapons are effective but scarce. Improvised bio-grenades can be crafted from volatile spore pods.

Pro Tip High-frequency sonic devices disrupt Swarm communication — buy time for retreat.
Critical pg. 78
🌿

Foraging & Flora

Edible bio-luminescent mushrooms glow faintly blue — safe to eat when the glow is steady. Red-glowing specimens contain neurotoxins. Gen-3 crops can grow in contaminated soil.

Pro Tip The "Whisper Vine" produces edible tubers but screams when pulled — harvest at night to avoid detection.
Intermediate pg. 91
🩺

Field Medicine

Bio-gel poultices stop most bleeding within minutes. Regenerative serums are expensive but can regrow lost tissue. For limb loss, tourniquet within 30 seconds to prevent mutation spread.

Pro Tip Antibiotic molds found in old-world ruins are still effective against bacterial strains — test with skin patch first.
Advanced pg. 115
