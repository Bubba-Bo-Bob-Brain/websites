TOXIC JUNGLE

The Toxic Jungle is a sprawling, bioluminescent hellscape where flora has mutated into predatory horrors. Towering spore-trees release hallucinogenic clouds, and the ground is carpeted with carnivorous vines that drag unwary travelers into the acidic muck below. Rumors speak of a fungal intelligence lurking in the depths—a sentient mycelium network that whispers secrets to those who listen.

  • Threats: Razorleaf Crawlers, Spore Zombies, Mycelium Stranglers
  • Resources: Glowcap Mushrooms, Acid-Resistant Chitin, Neural Fungus
  • Lore: The Jungle was once a biotech research facility studying symbiotic organisms. When the collapse came, the experiments broke free...
