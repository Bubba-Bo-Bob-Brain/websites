WASTELAND SURVIVAL GUIDE

Field notes from those who lived

001

Water Sources

Never drink untreated water. Filter through activated charcoal, then boil for 10 minutes. Green-tinted water contains algae but is generally safe. Yellow = urine contamination. Run.

ESSENTIAL
002

Mutation Signs

Early symptoms: unusual hunger, skin itching, vivid dreams, temperature fluctuations. Check under fingernails for discoloration. If veins turn dark, seek containment immediately.

WARNING
003

Scavenging Protocol

Always check industrial zones first - they're picked clean but contain rare components. Residential areas: check basements. Never enter a building without marking your exit.

TACTICS
004

Combat Hostiles

Mutated creatures: aim for joints. Husks: headshots only - they don't feel body damage. Never engage a Pack without grenades. Remember: running is always an option.

COMBAT
005

Day/Night Cycles

Hives become active at dusk. Radiation peaks at midnight. Bioluminescent zones are safest during the day. Plan your routes accordingly - night travel means different threats.

TIMING
006

Trade & Alliances

Sanctuary traders: honest but expensive. Nomads: barter only, no credits. The Fleshwrights: they'll trade bio-components for... samples. Don't ask what they need samples for.
