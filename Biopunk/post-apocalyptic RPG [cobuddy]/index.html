CRITICAL

Water Filtration Protocol

Do NOT drink from open sources. The aquifers ran hot in 2338. If you must filter, use triple-carbon membranes and boil for 12 minutes minimum. I watched a squad drink from the reservoir at Junction 4. They didn't make it past sundown. — Doc Wren, Field Medic, Sector 3

WARNING

Spore Storm Identification

When the wind shifts east and you smell copper and burnt sugar — get underground. Fast. The aerial spores from the Mycelium Bloom settle in 6 minutes. Cover your respiratory intake with layered gauze soaked in vinegar solution. The ones who hesitated... well, you can hear them singing at night. Not human singing.

RARE

Trading with Feral Communities

The Collective at Pump Station 9 trades in raw biomass and blood rations. Never show tech. They see circuitry as a sickness. Offer organic materials — preserved fungi, clean meat, viable seeds. They respect patience. Speak slow. Don't make sudden movements. They're watching your hands. Always.

TACTICAL

Night Movement in Contaminated Zones

The bioluminescent flora adapts to your light source. Use it and they find you. Move in the gutter. Use the dark patches between fungal colonies. Your own thermal signature is your biggest liability — the heat parasites detect anything above 36°C. Cool your blood before you move. Submerge your arms. Yes, it's uncomfortable. Yes, it works.
