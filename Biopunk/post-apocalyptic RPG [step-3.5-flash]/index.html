        THE FIRST SIGNS

        It started with the glowing rain. At first we thought it was just another industrial spill, but then the animals... they changed. Birds with too many eyes, rats that could digest metal, and then people. Oh god, the people.

        ⚠️ Avoid rain collection without filtration
        💧
        Water Purification: Boil for minimum 10 minutes, add charcoal filter. Do NOT consume if glowing blue!
        — Scout Kaelen, Zone 7
        PAGE 2 POST-FALL DAY 1,253

        MUTATION AWARENESS

        Not all mutations are bad. Some survivors have developed incredible adaptations. I've seen a man whose skin could photosynthesize, a woman who could hear heartbeats through concrete walls. But beware the hostile mutations—those that consume rather than enhance.

        ⚠️ HOSTILE MUTATION INDICATORS
        • Unnatural pupil shapes (slit, compound, multiple)
        • Skin discoloration (glowing, pulsating, shifting)
        • Excessive body heat or cold
        • Aggressive territorial behavior
        • Ability to consume inorganic matter
        — Scout Kaelen, Zone 7
