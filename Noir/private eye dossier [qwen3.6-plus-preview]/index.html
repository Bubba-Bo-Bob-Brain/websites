LOS ANGELES POLICE DEPARTMENT CASE FILE #47-2891

The Vanishing at Pier 42

Lead Detective: Det. Jack Calloway
Date Opened: November 14, 1947
Status: ACTIVE — UNSOLVED
Priority: HIGHEST

On the fog-drenched night of November 12th, socialite Evelyn Marsh was last seen leaving the Ambassador Hotel in the company of an unidentified man. Her abandoned handbag was discovered at Pier 42 at 3:47 AM. No body has been recovered. Three suspects have been identified. All have alibis that don't hold water.
