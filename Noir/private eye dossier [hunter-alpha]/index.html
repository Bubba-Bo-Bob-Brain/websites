Evidence Log

Every piece tells a story. The question is which one tells the truth.

001

Crime Scene — Library

Victim slumped at desk. Single gunshot wound to the temple. Revolver found 3 feet from the body. No signs of forced entry.

CRITICAL
002

Threatening Letter

Anonymous letter found in victim's desk drawer. Handwritten. "Your time is running out, old man." Postmarked downtown.

IMPORTANT
003

The Revolver

Smith & Wesson .38 Special. One round spent. Registered to Victor Blackwood. Wiped clean of prints.

CRITICAL
004

Estate Floor Plan

Blueprint showing all entry points. Study has two doors — main hallway and servant's passage. Windows were locked from inside.

REFERENCE
005

Blackmail Telegram

Received three days before the murder. Sent from a payphone near the Blue Moon nightclub. No fingerprints on the envelope.

IMPORTANT
006

Lipstick on Glass

Crystal whiskey glass found near the body. Lipstick residue matches Vivian Cross's signature shade — "Midnight Crimson."
