THE MIDNIGHT DIAMONDS HEIST

Det. Jack Marlowe ACTIVE
CONFIDENTIAL

Case Overview

|

Incident Report

On the night of November 14, 1947, the Midtown Diamond Co. was burglarized of an estimated $250,000 in uncut diamonds. The alarm system was bypassed using a copied security code, and no guards were harmed. A single cigarette butt and partial fingerprints were recovered at the scene.

Lead Priorities

  1. Match partial fingerprints to known criminal database
  2. Locate the getaway vehicle and trace ownership
  3. Interview all security personnel with access to the alarm code
  4. Surveil known associates of Vincent Moretti

Witness Testimonies

Mabel Henderson November 15, 1947, 09:00 AM

I was closing up the shop when I heard a loud crash from across the street. I looked out the window and saw two men run out of the diamond store, get into a dark Ford, and speed off towards the west side. One of 'em was tall, wore a dark coat. The other was shorter, had a limp, I think.

X Mabel Henderson
Joe 'The Doorman' Russo November 15, 1947, 11:30 AM

I seen Vincent Moretti loitering around the diamond district twice last week. He was asking questions about the security system, when the guards changed shifts, all that stuff. I told him to beat it, but he just laughed and said he'd "see the display soon enough". Shoulda known he was up to no good.

X Joe Russo
Det. Sgt. Raymond O'Malley November 16, 1947, 02:15 PM

Found the getaway car in an alley off 5th and Main. Partial prints match Moretti, but we need a full print to make it stick. The car was registered to a shell company tied to the Romano Crew. We're getting close, Jack. Don't screw this up.

X Sgt. R. O'Malley
Louie 'The Bartender' Conti November 16, 1947, 04:45 PM

Moretti was here last night, like I told the other cops. He was with a dame I never seen before, real looker, red dress. They left around 10, went west on 4th. I don't know nothin' about no diamond heist, I swear. I just serve drinks here.

X Louie Conti
