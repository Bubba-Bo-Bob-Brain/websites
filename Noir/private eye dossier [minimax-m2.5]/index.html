CASE FILE 1947-XR9

THE BLACK LOTUS AFFAIR

CLASSIFIED - ACTIVE

Case Briefing

DATE: November 14, 1947

LOCATION: Downtown Los Angeles, CA

OFFENSE: Murder, Conspiracy, Extortion

LEAD DETECTIVE: Malcolm V. Reynolds

On the night of November 14th, wealthy businessman Harold Ashworth was found dead in his study at the Ashworth Manor. The death appeared as a suicide at first, but evidence suggests otherwise. Three suspects have been identified, each with motive and opportunity. The Black Lotus - a notorious crime syndicate - is believed to be involved. This case file contains all known information.

Witness Statements

WITNESS #001 November 14, 1947 - 2:30 AM

James Whitmore - Butler

"I was in the kitchen preparing midnight tea when I heard the gunshot. It came from the study, down the hall. I rushed immediately but the door was locked. I had to fetch the spare key from the maid's quarters. When we entered, Mr. Ashworth was slumped over his desk. The window was open, and I could swear I saw a figure running across the garden..."

Credibility: HIGH
WITNESS #002 November 14, 1947 - 4:15 AM

Sarah Chen - Maid

"I was asleep in the east wing when Mr. James woke me. By the time we got to the study, everything was already as the police found it. But I want to say, Mrs. Ashworth... she wasn't at home that night. She said she was visiting her sister in Pasadena, but I saw her car drive by the gate around 11 PM. I didn't think anything of it then."

Credibility: MEDIUM
WITNESS #003 November 15, 1947 - 10:00 AM

Tommy "Two-Fingers" - Street Informant

"Look, I ain't supposed to talk to cops, but this one's big. Word on the street is the Black Lotus been squeezing Ashworth for months. He owed them fifty grand. They don't just send warnings - they send messages. That ain't no suicide, Detective. That's a message for anyone else thinking about crossing them."

Credibility: LOW - BIASED
