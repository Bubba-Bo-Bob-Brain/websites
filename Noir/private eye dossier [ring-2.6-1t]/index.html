“The Vanishing of Eleanor Voss”

October 14, 1947 Downtown New Avalon Lead: Detective Silas Blackwood

The rain hadn’t stopped in three days. Eleanor Voss walked into our office on a Tuesday afternoon smelling of gardenias and desperation. She left a photograph, a key, and three hundred dollars on my desk. By Thursday morning, she was gone.
