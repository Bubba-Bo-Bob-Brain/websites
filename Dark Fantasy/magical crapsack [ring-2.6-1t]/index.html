The Blighted Regions

Six domains of ruin where the land itself has become the enemy of those who dwell upon it.

DOOMED

The Ashfall Expanse

Former Fertile Plains

Once the breadbasket of the Old Kingdoms, this vast stretch of land has been buried under perpetual volcanic ash from the eruption of Mount Ghrondarath. The sky is a permanent ochre haze, and what little rain falls is acidic, scarring the earth further. Skeletal trees claw at a sunless sky.

  • Soil toxicity: 98%
  • Breathable air zones: dwindling
  • Population: near zero
BLIGHTED

The Dreadmire

Cursed Swamplands

A labyrinth of black water and grasping vegetation where the boundary between the living and the dead has dissolved entirely. The water burns to the touch, and those who drown in its depths do not stay dead. Will-o'-wisps lead travelers to their doom among the sucking mud.

  • Water purity: nonexistent
  • Undead activity: constant
  • Survival beyond 3 days: impossible
ANOMALOUS

The Hollowed Peaks

Petrified Mountain Range

The bones of the earth are visible here — and not metaphorically. Entire mountains have turned to calcified tissue, as if the land itself was once a living thing that suffered some terrible affliction. Strange harmonics echo through the stone corridors, and those who listen too long begin to hear voices.

  • Geological stability: unknown
  • Psychic corruption radius: vast
  • Return rate of expeditions: 7%
CURSED

The Gloomveil Forest

Eternal Twilight Wood

A primeval forest where the canopy is so dense that no ray of sunlight has touched the forest floor in living memory. The trees are sentient — or something wearing their shapes. They whisper in languages older than civilization, luring the unwary deeper into paths that fold back upon themselves like the folds of a nightmare.

  • Natural light at ground level: zero
  • Whispering frequency: increasing
  • Missing persons (cumulative): 4,000+
DEFILED

The Sanguine Coast

Blood-Tainted Shoreline

The sea here runs red — not with metaphor, but with literal blood. Something beneath the waves bleeds endlessly, and the tide brings only ruin to the shores. Fishermen haul nets full of bones and black ichor. The few coastal settlements have gone silent, their lights extinguished one by one.

  • Marine ecosystem: extinct
  • Water composition: blood-like fluid
  • Coastal habitation: abandoned
ANNIHILATED

The Shattered Sanctum

Ruins of the Eternal City of Valtheris

Valtheris was once the crown jewel of civilization — a city of marble spires and boundless learning. Now its towers lie broken, its streets choked with ash and the remnants of failed evacuations. Something awoke beneath the Sanctum Palace, and in a single night, millennia of progress were unmade.

  • Structural integrity: collapsed
  • Spectral activity: pervasive
  • Living inhabitants: unknown entities only

The Affliction Tracker

An accounting of active curses, plagues, and supernatural blights ravaging Vorthanis.

The Withering Touch

Curse
X

An ancient hex of unknown origin causes all organic matter within a growing radius of the Blight Nexus to rapidly age and decay. Fruits rot on the branch, animals crumble to dust, and the skin of those afflicted cracks and falls away like old parchment.

Epicenter: Blight Nexus, Hollowed Peaks Radius: Expanding (1.3 miles/day) Active since: Year of the Black Sun

The Unending Rain

Phenomenon
IX

It has not ceased raining in thirteen years. What began as a dark omen has become the defining misery of Vorthanis. The rain is cold, relentless, and slightly acidic. Rivers have swollen beyond their banks, farmlands are drowned, and a pervasive dampness has settled into the bones of every living soul.

Scope: Continent-wide Water table: catastrophically high Duration: 13 years, 4 months, 7 days

The Hollow Plague

Plague
X

Those afflicted do not die — and that is the horror. The Hollow Plague consumes the soul while leaving the body animate. The resulting "Hollows" walk, speak, and eat, but there is nothing behind their eyes. They weep without knowing why. They scream without feeling grief. Entire villages have become halls of the living dead.

Transmission: soul-contact (proximity to Hollows) Cure: none known Estimated afflicted: 200,000+

The Chain of Echoes

Curse
VIII

Every sound made within the borders of Vorthanis echoes infinitely. A child's cry reverberates for days. A hammer strike rings for weeks. The ceaseless layering of sound has driven entire cities to madness. The afflicted claw at their own ears only to find the screams originate from within.

Decibel levels: permanently lethal range Hearing loss: universal among long-term inhabitants Madness rate in echo zones: 100%

The Blood Moon Sickness

Blight
IX

When the moon bleeds — and in Vorthanis, it bleeds every nineteen days — the weak undergo transformation. Their bodies twist, their minds shatter, and they become vessels for something ancient and ravenous. The strong merely go mad. No shelter is sufficient. No prayer is answered.

Cycle: every 19 days (blood moon) Transformation rate: 34% of population Mortality of transformed: eventual

The Silence of Gods

Phenomenon
VII

Divine communication has ceased entirely. Temples stand empty. Clerics lose their powers mid-prayer. The gods — if they still exist — have become spectators, or worse, prisoners. Those who relied upon divine healing now simply die. Those who relied upon divine guidance now wander blindly into catastrophe.

Duration of silence: 312 years Last confirmed divine act: The Weeping Faith organizations: in total collapse

The Flesh Bloom

Blight
VIII

In areas of concentrated death, fleshy growths erupt from the ground — grotesque flowers that pulse with a sick warmth. Those who inhale their spores develop tumors that bloom into smaller versions of these growths beneath their skin. The afflicted describe hearing a low, beautiful humming before the first bloom breaks the surface.

Spore range: 2 miles from any bloom Incubation period: 3–7 days Mortality rate post-bloom: 94%
