The Prophecy

Words spoken before time had a name

When the seventh moon bleeds crimson upon the obsidian spire, the Veil shall tear asunder. From the abyss between worlds, they shall come—the Forgotten Kings, crowned in shadow and wrath.

The earth shall crack and weep black fire. Rivers of mercury will carve new paths through the bones of the ancient world. And in that hour of reckoning, only the Marked Ones shall stand between the light and the eternal dark.

⚠ Beware the Whispering Dark. It knows your name. It has always known your name.

— Fragment VII, The Book of Ashes

The Codex

Fragments of forbidden knowledge

I

On the Nature of Shadows

Shadows are not merely the absence of light, as the scholars of the Ivory Tower would have you believe. They are living things, hungry and patient. Every shadow you cast is a tether to the Umbra Realm, a thread that grows thinner with each passing age.

When the thread snaps, you do not simply die. You become something else. Something that walks between the worlds, neither living nor dead, forever searching for the light it can never again touch.

Metaphysics Danger: High
II

The Seven Seals of Binding

In the age before memory, the First Council bound the Archfiends behind seven seals, each forged from a different aspect of mortal virtue:

  1. The Seal of Courage, forged in dragonfire
  2. The Seal of Mercy, tempered in tears
  3. The Seal of Truth, hammered from starlight
  4. The Seal of Sacrifice, quenched in willing blood
  5. The Seal of Hope, sung into existence
  6. The Seal of Love, the strongest and the first to crack
  7. The Seal of Death, which holds all the others together

⚠ The Seal of Love shattered in the Year of Ash. Six remain.

History Danger: Extreme
III

Whispers from the Void

Transcript recovered from the ruined monastery of Saint Aldric, dated to the Night of Falling Stars:

"It speaks to us still, from beneath the mountain. Not in words, but in hunger. We feel it in our dreams—a vast, ancient appetite that has waited ten thousand years for the seals to weaken."

The manuscript ends here. The remaining pages were found scattered across the monastery grounds, covered in symbols that no scholar has been able to decipher.

Entity Status: Unknown
