The Prophecies

Words spoken by the Blind Oracle of Ashmere, transcribed in blood upon the walls of the Forgotten Sanctum.

I

When the crimson moon bleeds upon the horizon of the Shattered Plains, the sealed gates beneath the Obsidian Throne shall crack, and from the fissure shall emerge the Herald of Ashes, bearing a crown of dying stars and a sword forged from the last breath of a murdered god.

✦ ☽ ✦
II

Three keys of bone, iron, and shadow shall be sought by the Seven Forsaken, each bearing the mark of a fallen house. Only when united beneath the eclipse of twin suns shall the Veil of Worlds be torn asunder, and the old ones shall wake from their eternal slumber.

✦ ☾ ✦
III

Beware the one who walks between the rain, whose footsteps leave no mark upon the earth. For this is the Thief of Names, who steals not gold nor jewel, but the very identity of those they encounter. To be forgotten by all is to cease to exist — and this is the fate the Thief offers as a gift.

✦ ☽ ✦
IV

In the deepest chamber of the Sunken Cathedral, where the water burns cold and the prayers of the drowned still echo, there lies the Grimoire Eternal — a book whose pages are written in the language of creation itself. He who reads it shall know all truths and lose all sanity. She who burns it shall end the world.

The Codex

Fragments of lore recovered from the ruins of civilizations that dared to challenge the darkness.

Before the Age of Ash, there stood five great kingdoms upon the continent of Morvath. Each was ruled by a Sovereign who had made a pact with entities beyond the Veil. For a thousand years they flourished, their cities gleaming with enchanted stone and their armies blessed with unholy strength.

But pacts with the old ones always come due. When the Crimson Eclipse came, the Sovereigns were consumed by the very powers they had bargained with, and their kingdoms crumbled into the wastelands that now scar the land.

— From the Chronicles of the Last Scholar

Scattered across the forsaken lands are artifacts of terrible power, each one bound to the soul of its creator and cursed by the blood of its victims. The most feared among them are:

  • The Crown of Whispers — Grants visions of the future but steals the wearer's voice
  • The Blade of Unmaking — Cuts through any material, including the fabric of reality
  • The Chalice of Drowned Souls — Fills with the tears of those who have perished at sea
  • The Mirror of True Sight — Shows all things as they truly are, driving most who gaze upon it to madness
— Catalogue of the Forbidden Archives

In the shadow of the fallen kingdoms, new powers have risen from the ashes. The most notable among them:

  • The Ashen Order — Monks who have sworn to contain the darkness, even as it consumes them
  • The Crimson Covenant — Cultists who seek to hasten the return of the old ones
  • The Iron Conclave — Artificers who forge weapons from the remnants of the fallen kingdoms
  • The Veilwalkers — Mysterious wanderers who exist between worlds, serving neither light nor dark
— Reports of the Royal Intelligence, Last Entry
