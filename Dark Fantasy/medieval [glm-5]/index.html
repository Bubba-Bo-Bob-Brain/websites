⚜ The Prophecy of Shadows ⚜

Transcribed from the Black Monastery, 1247

When the blood moon rises o'er the ancient keep, And ravens gather where the fallen sleep, The grimoire's pages shall turn once more, Opening wide the eldritch door.

Beware the one who seeks the tome,
For knowledge comes with flesh to own.
The words you read, the spells you speak,
Shall bind your soul, both strong and weak.

The shadows remember what light has forgotten.

✦

Three keys shall unlock the gates of dread:
A dragon's scale, a hero dead, a tear of sorrow shed.
