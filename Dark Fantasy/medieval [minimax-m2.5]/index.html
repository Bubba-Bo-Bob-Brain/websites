The Ominous Prophecy

When the Three Moons align beneath the Bleeding Star,
The King of Ashes shall rise from his ancient tomb.
The Veil between worlds grows thin, as shadows consume the light,
And the Children of the Night awaken from their slumber.

Only the Chosen Bearer of the Crimson Sigil
Shall hold the Blade of Eternity against the coming darkness.
Yet know this, seeker of forbidden knowledge:
The price of victory is written in blood.
