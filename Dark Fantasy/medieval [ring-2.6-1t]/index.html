The Prophecy of the Seventh Eclipse

When the sun surrenders to the raven's wing,
And the rivers run with ink of forgotten kings,
A knight shall rise, forged in shadow's breath,
Who carries light within the arms of death.

The Seven Seals shall crack beneath the weight
Of oaths unbroken, sworn before the gate.
Beware the flame that burns without a sun—
For it is the last light when all is done.

Seek the Ember Crown in the Vault of Sighs,
Where the blind king keeps his thousand eyes.
Only through the darkness shall the dawn be earned,
Only those who've touched the void shall know return.

De Ordinis Historia The History of the Order

Founded in the Year of the Bleeding Moon, 1047, the Order of the Eclipsed Flame was forged in the crucible of war against the encroaching darkness of the Hollow Dominion. Seven paladins, each having gazed into the abyss and returned, swore an oath beneath the dying light of a sun consumed by shadow.

For nine centuries, the Order has stood as the last barrier between the realms of the living and the legions of the Hollow King. Our knights are trained in the arts forgotten by the age of men — the Lingua Tenebris, the Sword of Dusk, and the sacred Rites of Ember.

We are the flame that burns in darkness. We are the shadow that shields the innocent. We are the last light.

Codex Tenebris The Dark Codex

Chapter I — The Founding

In the age when men still named the stars, seven warriors gathered beneath a sky turned violet. The Hollow King had risen, and with him came legions that defied the natural order. These seven — the Firstsworn — pledged their lives, their names, and eventually their very shadows to the cause of preservation.

They called themselves the Order of the Eclipsed Flame, for their emblem told their truth: that even in total darkness, a single flame of defiance could eclipse the void itself.

Chapter VII — The Hollow Dominion

The Hollow Dominion exists in the spaces between — between light and dark, between life and death, between what is real and what was forgotten. It is not a place one travels to; it is a place that finds you.

The Hollow King rules from the Obsidian Throne, a seat carved from the bones of gods who dared to oppose him. His voice is the silence between heartbeats. His army is infinite, drawn from every soul that died with unfinished purpose.

Chapter XII — The Ember Crown

Of all the relics in the Vault of Sighs, none is so coveted — or so feared — as the Ember Crown. Forged in the final breath of the First Flame, it grants its bearer sight beyond sight, power beyond reckoning.

But the Crown demands a price: with each passing hour, the wearer's humanity is consumed, until nothing remains but a vessel of pure, undying flame. The last knight to wear it — Ser Aldric of the Ashen Hand — still walks the halls of the Citadel, a wisp of fire in knight's armor, speaking in tongues no living tongue can parse.
