The Veil of Whispers

MYTHIC

A tattered veil woven from the threads of forgotten dreams, said to grant the wearer the ability to hear the whispered secrets of the dead. Those who don the veil report voices growing louder with each passing hour — until the voices are no longer whispers.

✦

Forged in the catacombs beneath the city of Velmara by a nun who traded her sight for forbidden knowledge, the Veil was blessed — or perhaps cursed — during the Rite of Unspoken Vows. It is said that the veil remembers every secret it has ever heard, and that it hungers for more. The previous owner, the infamous artificer Malthus Grave, was found speaking to an empty room for eleven days before the room began speaking back. His journal, recovered three months later, contained only one repeated sentence: "It knows my name. It knows everyone's name."

⚠ Handling without soul-binding enchantment may result in permanent psychic contamination.
