Chalice of Eternal Thirst

☠ Relic

Lore [+]

Said to be the cup used by the God-King of Vorthas during the First Feast, this chalice never empties—and never lets its drinker slake their thirst. The liquid inside is not wine, but the essence of those who drank from it before. The last owner, a noble from Eldermere, was found in their garden, mummified, with the chalice still clutched in their hand.

  • Drinking from the chalice heals 2d8+5 HP.
