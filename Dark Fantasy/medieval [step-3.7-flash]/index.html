Prophecy of the Eclipse

When the blood moon bleeds o'er the shattered spires,
And the dead walk free from their cold pyres,
The seal shall break, the shadow shall rise,
And the light of man shall fade from the skies.

Beware the beast with eyes of flame,
The thief who bears the king's true name,
The child of ash, the curse unbound,
When the final bell tolls under the ground.

Forbidden Grimoire

1

Ritual of Warding

To ward against the undead, mix salt with the ash of a burnt rowan branch, and trace the symbol of the sun around your dwelling. Speak the words "Lux tenebris repellere" (Light repel the darkness) three times at dawn.

2

Curse of the Banshee

If you hear the wail of a banshee, press a sprig of wolfsbane to your ear to block the sound. Do not look upon her face, for her gaze will freeze your heart in your chest.

3

Wyrm Slaying

The only way to kill a Wyrm of Ash is to pierce its underbelly with a spear forged from the heart of a starfall meteor. The wound will cauterize on contact, but the wyrm's blood will burn through any mortal metal.
