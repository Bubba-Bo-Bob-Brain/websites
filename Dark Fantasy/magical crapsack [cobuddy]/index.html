REGION The Blighted Landscape

The Ashen Wastes

Endless plains of grey powder stretch to a horizon that never curves. The sky is a permanent bruise of charcoal and rust. Nothing grows. Nothing lives. The wind carries the whisper of ten thousand extinguished voices.

Endless Hostile No Resources

Black Salt Marshes

Shallow pools of liquid sorrow seep between petrified trees. The water is warm, tasting of copper and regret. Those who drink too deeply forget their own names — and in forgetting, find only emptiness.

Corrosive Memory-Eating Warm

The Iron Peaks

Mountains that groan with the weight of ancient grief. Their slopes are slick with an oil that weeps from the stone itself. Lightning strikes without clouds, and each bolt carves new faces into the rock — faces that scream.

Seismic Lightning-Scarred Weeping

The Hollow Maw

A sinkhole miles across, descending into a darkness that has weight and hunger. Things climb out at night — not creatures, but concepts: despair, famine, the silence between heartbeats. They wear the faces of the recently dead.

Abyssal Hungering Night-Crawlers

AFFLICTION LOG Active Curses & Afflictions

The following curses plague the wretched inhabitants of the Blighted Expanse. Each is documented in the grimoire of recorded horrors.

🜁

The Rot of Purpose

Severity: UNBEARABLE

Victims lose all sense of motivation. They forget why they breathe, why they walk, why they cling to a life that offers nothing. Many simply stop. They become statues of flesh, eyes open, waiting for a purpose that will never come.

Paralysis of will Blank staring Complete apathy
Active Cases: 0
🜂

Whispers of the Hollow King

Severity: CATASTROPHIC

A voice — always just below hearing — narrates your failures in real-time. It knows your sins. It speaks your name in a tone of infinite disappointment. Sleep becomes impossible. The voice does not sleep either.

Insomnia Auditory hallucination Self-loathing
Active Cases: 0
🜄

Blightblood

Severity: CATASTROPHIC

The blood turns to black tar within the veins. Victims appear grey-skinned, cold to the touch. They emit a faint sound — a low, rhythmic throb, like a second heartbeat made of sorrow. They are still alive. That is the cruelest part.

Vascular blackening Hypothermia Melancholic thrumming
Active Cases: 0
🜍

The Flickering

Severity: DREADFUL

Reality stutters. For brief moments, the world freezes — a half-second where everything stops. During these freezes, victims see things that should not exist: rooms that have been empty for centuries, filled with chairs arranged for a feast that will never happen.

Temporal stuttering Visual intrusions Disorientation
Active Cases: 0
🜔

Skinwarden

Severity: CATASTROPHIC

The skin hardens into a shell, then begins to move independently — sprouting small, eyeless faces that turn toward sound. The original face remains, frozen in an expression of permanent alarm. Victims can no longer tell where their body ends and the horror begins.

Dermal calcification Parasitic growths Identity dissolution
Active Cases: 0

Moonmaddened

Severity: DREADFUL

There is no moon. There hasn't been one in centuries. Yet victims are drawn to the sky each night, searching for it. They speak to its absence. They weep for its ghost. Some have carved a circle in their own chest, placing the missing moon inside.

Compulsive sky-gazing Lunatic devotion Self-surgical obsession

LITURGY Dark Ritual Calendar

The observances of the Blighted Expanse. Follow them. There is no opting out.

1st of Ash

The Sowing of Teeth

Every household must bury a tooth in their threshold. The tooth must be from a living child — extracted willingly or otherwise. Those who refuse find their doors open to things that walk upright and weep.

OBSERVED
9th of Ash

The Burning of Names

All names are spoken aloud and then set to fire. Without names, the dead cannot be called back. Without names, the living cannot be found. This is considered a mercy.

COMPLETED
17th of Ash

The Communion of Empty Hands

Practitioners must eat nothing for seven days, then consume a stone ground to powder. The stone must be from a place where someone died wishing they hadn't. The meal is silent. The meal is forever.

PENDING
23rd of Ash

The Descent

One soul per village is cast into the Hollow Maw. The chosen one is not selected by worth or sin — they are chosen by the Maw itself, which sends dreams to one person per settlement for three nights prior.

OBSERVED
30th of Ash

The Closing

All fires are extinguished. All light sources are buried. For 72 hours, the world exists in absolute darkness. The only sound permitted is breathing. Those who speak during the Closing are never heard from again.

INVENTORY Catalog of Cursed Artifacts

Objects of power, each one a monument to suffering. Handle with the absence of caution.

AE-001

The Lantern of Last Glimpses

A wrought-iron lantern that burns with a pale, cold flame. Anyone illuminated by it sees their own death — not the manner of it, but the moment, vivid and irreversible. Many who see their death simply sit down and wait.

Provenance: Found in the chest cavity of a giant who died mid-step, still walking for six years after death.
AE-007

The Mirror of Every Failure

An oval mirror with a silver frame decorated in thorns. It does not reflect the present — it reflects every failure the viewer has ever experienced, simultaneously, in infinite detail. Staring too long causes permanent weeping.

Provenance: Recovered from a lake that existed only in reflections. The lake, when drained, held nothing but teeth.
AE-013

The Unfinished Testament

A book that writes itself in the handwriting of whoever opens it. It records the remainder of that person's life in perfect, excruciating detail — including every mistake they will make, every cruel word they will speak, every moment of cowardice.

Provenance: Written by a pen that was forged from the last quill of a bird that sang only funerals.
AE-021

The Crown of Shared Sorrow

A circlet of black iron set with a gem that pulses like a heartbeat. The wearer does not feel their own pain — instead, they feel the pain of everyone within a mile radius. In a world of endless suffering, the wearer's mind shatters within minutes.

Provenance: Worn by a king who ruled for one day before lying down in the throne room and never rising again.
AE-033

The Bell That Listens

A small bronze bell that never rings on its own — but rings softly whenever someone nearby tells a lie. The more significant the lie, the louder the tone. In a world built on half-truths and desperate deceptions, it has not stopped ringing for six hundred years.

Provenance: Cast from the tears of a deity that watched its children destroy themselves and chose silence over intervention.
