The Age of Woe

§
I

n the forgotten epochs before memory, the world was whole, blessed by the Radiant Concord and sheltered beneath the wings of benevolent stars. Those were the days of laughter, of harvests that never failed, of children who grew to see their own grandchildren.

Those days are dust now. Dust and suffering.

The Shattering came without warning. The Celestial Sphere cracked like an egg, and from that cosmic wound poured the Blight, a miasma of pure despair that touched every stone, every stream, every soul. The gods fell silent, or worse, began to listen to different prayers.

Now the world groans beneath the weight of accumulated misery. Each sunrise brings fresh torments. Each night harbors unspeakable horrors. The survivors huddle in their crumbling refuges, counting their remaining days with the grim certainty that suffering is the only currency that never devalues.

Catalog of Afflictions

Curses, diseases, and supernatural torments that plague the inhabitants of this blighted world. Each entry recorded through the suffering of those afflicted.

Active Plagues and Curses

☠

The Weeping Brand

Severity:

Victims bear a burning mark that weeps endlessly. The tears are not water, but the essence of stolen memories. Afflicted lose all recollection of joy within a fortnight.

Est. 847 A.W. ~2,340 Afflicted
☣

Grey Rot

Severity:

Flesh turns to ash-grey stone, spreading from extremities inward. Victims remain conscious as their body becomes a living statue. Final petrification takes 40-60 days.

Est. 912 A.W. ~89,000 Afflicted
👁

The Witnessing

Severity:

Additional eyes grow across the victim's body. Each new eye sees a different plane of existence, flooding the mind with incomprehensible visions. Sleep becomes impossible.

Est. 891 A.W. ~4,127 Afflicted
☠

Echoing Death

Severity:

The cursed die and return repeatedly, each death more agonizing than the last. They remember every death with perfect clarity. Most beg for true oblivion within their third cycle.

Est. 856 A.W. ~892 Afflicted
☣

Hollow Sickness

Severity:

The afflicted slowly lose all emotion, becoming empty vessels. They report feeling hollow, a vast emptiness where feelings once resided. Many simply walk away and never return.

Est. 934 A.W. ~34,567 Afflicted
👁

The Unraveling

Severity:

Victims begin to come undone at a metaphysical level. Memories dissolve, skills are forgotten, and eventually the sense of self disintegrates. What remains is a breathing void.

Est. 941 A.W. ~1,203 Afflicted

The Crown of Ten Thousand Sorrows

Origin: Forged from the melted tears of a weeping god

A crown of blackened silver that grants its wearer dominion over all who suffer. Each day it is worn, the wearer experiences the cumulative pain of every soul in their domain.

Curse: Sympathetic Agony

The wearer feels every wound, every illness, every moment of despair experienced by those they rule. At peak usage, the psychic weight has driven all previous owners to madness within a year.

The Mirror of True Suffering

Origin: Crafted by the Blind Oracle of Despair
