Blighted Landscapes

Where the earth itself suffers and the sky remembers its wounds

The Cinder Wastes

An endless plain of grey ash where nothing grows and nothing dies — because nothing lives. The ash falls like snow from a sky that has not seen sunlight in two hundred years. Travelers report hearing weeping from beneath the crust, though none have found a source.

The Hollow Marshes

A vast wetland of black water and twisted trees whose roots pulse with a sickly green luminescence. The marsh breathes — inhaling at dusk, exhaling at dawn — and what it exhales is the spore of Fleshrot. Those who die here do not stay dead. They rise as bog-husks, wandering the mires in endless, purposeless circles.

The City of Endless Weeping

It remembers everything.

The Chronicle of Ruin

A brief accounting of how everything came to be so terribly, irrevocably broken

Age of Ash 1

The First Burning

The saints were burned. Their prayers melted into the crown. The age of light ended. The age of ash began with the smell of charred hope and the silence of the dead gods.

Age of Ash 200

The Ironwood Blooms

The first Ironwood erupted from the Cinder Wastes — a tree that screamed. Its seeds scattered on the wind. Where they landed, forests of agony took root. The land learned to suffer.

Age of Ash 389

The Marsh Rises

The Hollow Marshes appeared overnight where farmland had been. Fleshrot emerged from the black water. The first bog-husks rose three days later. They are still walking.

Age of Ash 412

The Bloodmoon Rite

The Crimson Thirst was born from a ritual meant to end famine. Instead, it created a hunger that can never be sated. The Chalice appeared. The first wishes were granted. The first catastrophes followed.

Age of Ash 450

The Ashlung Spores

Volcanic eruptions in the Scorched Reaches released spores that took root in lungs. The Ashlung Blight began its slow, windborne spread. Breathing became a gamble.

Age of Ash 471 — NOW

The Fracture Approaches

The Seventh Seal weakens. The Misery Index climbs toward total collapse. The Blood Moon rises. All signs converge. Whether the end comes with a roar or a whisper, it comes. Soon.
