Fragmented Lore

The First Binding

In the age before time, when darkness was but a concept, the ancients bound the chaotic forces into vessels of containment. They carved runes of warding into stone and sealed the breaches between dimensions.

The Corruption

Hubris led the scholars to seek power beyond their understanding. The forbidden knowledge they unearthed corrupted their very souls, transforming wisdom into madness and light into shadow.

The Final Warning

Beware the seeker who dares to disturb the slumbering entities. For once awakened, there is no return from the abyss, and the darkness that follows consumes not just the body, but the very essence of existence.
