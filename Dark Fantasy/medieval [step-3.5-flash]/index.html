The Ominous Prophecy

When the twin moons bleed crimson and the shadows lengthen beyond the ancient stones, the Dark One shall rise from the abyss. The stars will whisper forgotten tongues, and the earth shall tremble beneath the weight of the coming storm. Only the chosen bearer of the silver flame can stand against the tide of darkness, though many shall fall before the final dawn. The path is fraught with peril, and betrayal lurks in every corner of the realm. Heed these words, for they are the last warning from the fallen kingdom...

Creatures of the Night

Shadow Wraith

Undead / Spectral
Danger: High Speed: Very Fast Weakness: Silver, Light

Souls trapped between worlds, these entities feed on fear and can phase through solid matter. They are most active during new moons.

Forest Drake

Dragonkin / Terrestrial
Danger: Extreme Speed: Moderate Weakness: Fire, Arrogance

Lesser cousins of true dragons, forest drakes inhabit ancient woodlands. Their breath weapon varies by subspecies, but all possess formidable claws and teeth.

Banshee

Fey / Spirit
Danger: High Speed: Fast Weakness: Holy Symbols

The wailing spirits of murdered women, banshees foretell death. Their mournful cries can shatter glass and rupture eardrums. Some can be reasoned with.

Lich

Undead / Arcane
Danger: Catastrophic Speed: Slow Weakness: Phylactery Destruction

A wizard who has extended their life through necromancy, storing their soul in a hidden phylactery. They command legions of undead and wield devastating magic.

Wendigo

Cursed / Cannibal
Danger: High Speed: Fast Weakness: Fire, Holy

Humans transformed by dark curses after consuming human flesh. These gaunt, monstrous beings possess incredible strength and an insatiable hunger for flesh.

Nightmare

Fiend / Ethereal
Danger: High Speed: Extreme Weakness: Holy, Dawn

Demonic steeds that appear as black horses with flaming manes and eyes of burning coals. They are said to be the mounts of dark riders and can induce terror in those they encounter.
