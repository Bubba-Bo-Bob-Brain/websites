Bestiary of the Veiled Realm

Hollowborn

Spawned from the cadavers of oathbreakers, the Hollowborn wander the Ashen Wastes. Their mouths sew shut with sinew, yet their screams echo in the minds of the living.

Habitat: Crypts, battlefields Weakness: Holy flame

Mourning Moth

These spectral insects weave through moonlight, their wings inscribed with the names of the dead. To hear their clicking is to hear your own eulogy.

Habitat: Moonlit graveyards Weakness: Cold iron

Dusk Stalker

A predator of twilight, the Stalker’s body flickers between shadow and flesh. It mimics the voices of its victims to lure prey into the mist.

Habitat: Forests, fog banks Weakness: Sunlight

Annals of the Obsidian Court

In the year of the Shattered Crown, the Obsidian Court arose from the ruins of Vorthain. Their sigil, a serpent devouring its own tail, was etched into the flesh of traitors with molten silver. The Court’s rule is one of whispers—decrees issued in dreams, executions carried out by shadows.

Their most infamous relic, The Black Grimoire, is said to contain the true names of every king who ever ruled. To speak a name aloud is to invite the Court’s assassins to your hearth. Yet, some secrets are too dangerous to remain hidden...

The Sigil of the Obsidian Court

The Grimoire’s final page is blank, save for a single line: "The last king shall rise when the crow drinks the moon’s blood." Scholars debate whether this is prophecy or a summoning ritual. The Court has burned all who sought to test its truth.

Beware, seeker. This tome is bound in the hide of the First Scribe, and its words carry his curse. To read beyond this point is to invite the Court’s gaze upon you.
