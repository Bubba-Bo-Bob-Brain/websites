The World That Forgot Hope

☠

Seven centuries ago, the Shattering tore open the Veil between worlds. What poured through was not death — death would have been mercy. Instead, it was corruption: a slow, gnawing rot that seeped into the very marrow of existence. The gods fell silent. The stars dimmed. And the Blight began its patient, eternal feast.

Now, the Blighted Kingdoms stretch across what was once a verdant continent. Kingdoms of ash and bone, where the living envy the dead and the dead refuse to rest. Every dawn brings new horrors. Every night, the darkness grows teeth. There is no salvation — only the desperate, defiant act of breathing one more poisoned breath.

Chronicles of Cataclysm

⚛

A record of the Ages of Ruin, etched in blood upon the Walls of Lamentation

◆

The Shattering

The sky cracked like black glass. From the wound poured the Blight — not darkness, but an absence of meaning. Those who witnessed it went mad. Those who didn't went madder still, for they could feel the world dying around them.

Cataclysm Arcane Event
◆

The Silence of the Gods

Prayers went unanswered. Temples crumbled. The last divine oracle spoke a single word — "Forget" — before her eyes turned to ash. The gods had not abandoned the world. They had been devoured.

Divine Terror
◆

The Weeping Plague

Eyes wept black tears that burned like acid. Skin peeled in patterns that formed words in a language no mortal should read. The infected did not die — they became something else. Something that still screams in the deep places of the earth.

Plague Corruption
◆

The Iron Harvest

The soil itself rebelled. Fields of grain transformed overnight into forests of rusted metal that grew and grew, piercing through villages, through people, through hope itself. Farmers reaped blades instead of wheat.

Blighted Nature Cataclysm
◆

The Pact of Thorns

Desperate warlocks bound themselves to the Blight, hoping to control it. They became the Thorned — immortal, suffering conduits of corruption. They rule now from palaces of bone, granting boons that always cost more than they give.

Dark Magic Power
◆

The Last Candle

The Lanternkeepers lit the Last Candle in the Spire of Enduring Flame — a beacon meant to hold back the darkness for one more generation. It flickers now, guttering low. When it goes out, so does the last true light in the world.

Fading Hope Impending Doom
◆

The Present Darkness

The Candle dims. The Thorned grow restless. Something stirs in the wound in the sky — something vast and hungry, turning its gaze upon the world once more. The age of suffering nears its crescendo.

Blighted Landscapes

⛰

The kingdoms of ruin that stretch across the dying continent

The Ashlands

Where the first Blight-touched city fell. Now an endless plain of grey ash, perpetually stirred by winds that carry whispers of the dead. Fires burn beneath the surface — cold flames that consume memories instead of flesh.

Threat Level

The Weeping Marshes

Water that remembers. The marshes hold the sorrow of every drowned soul, and the water reaches for the living with cold, desperate fingers. Best traveled during the day — at night, the water stands up and walks.

Threat Level

The Iron Wastes

A forest of rusted metal that grew from corrupted farmland. The trees are blades, the leaves are razor wire, and the sap is blood. Travelers hear the metal singing — a song that promises power and delivers only lacerations.

Threat Level

The Dimming Enclave

The last settlement bathed in true light, clustered around the Spire of Enduring Flame. Here, humanity's remnants huddle against the encroaching dark. The Candle's glow grows dimmer each year. When it dies, so does hope.

Threat Level

The Dark Bestiary

♔

Creatures born of the Blight, catalogued by those who survived long enough to write

☤

Weeping Ones

Former humans afflicted by The Weeping

Hollow-eyed figures that weep rivers of black acid. They wander in groups, their skin covered in glowing script. They do not attack — they simply stand and watch, and whatever they watch begins to change.

Danger: Proximity Hazard
⚗

Rust Stalkers

Blight-fused predators of the Iron Wastes

Wolves made of corroded metal, their bodies a patchwork of blades and wire. They hunt by sound — their own footsteps are silent, but they can hear a heartbeat from a mile away. They leave rust-colored trails wherever they pass.

Danger: Extremely Lethal
❀

Thorned Ents

Victims of Thornrot, fully transformed

Once human, now trees of black thorns and screaming bark. They are still conscious, still aware, and they weep sap that smells like perfume. If you listen closely, they beg for death in languages that predate human speech.

Danger: Sadness Hazard
☁

Mistform Choir

Final stage of The Singing affliction

Entities of pure sound and fog that drift through the Hollow Peaks. Their combined song can be heard for leagues — a beautiful, terrible melody that draws listeners to walk off cliffs or into the waiting arms of the Blight.

Danger: Existential
⚛

The Unnamed

Unknown — possibly ancient, possibly new

Something vast that moves beneath the Ashlands. No one has seen it fully. Those who glimpse even a fraction of its form lose the ability to describe shapes. It is the source of the ash. It is the source of everything. Perhaps.

Danger: Unknown / Immeasurable
