The First Blight

Year of the Weeping Sun, 847

When the Great Seal fractured, the sky tore open like wounded flesh. From the rift poured the Miasma—a corruption that seeped into soil, water, and soul alike. The ancient wards crumbled. The old gods screamed. And the world learned a new word: suffering.

Cataclysm The Fracturing

The Age of Ash

Year of the Hollow Crown, 1203

Three centuries of unending twilight followed. Crops withered in the fields. Rivers ran thick with the residue of corrupted leylines. Kingdoms fell not to war, but to the slow, grinding misery of a world that had forgotten the taste of sunlight.

Famine The Dimming

The Plague of Whispers

Year of the Broken Chain, 1589

It began as a murmur at the edge of hearing—a voice calling from the dark. Those who listened found their minds unraveling. The afflicted wandered into the wastes, speaking in tongues no living being should know. None returned. Some say they still wander, hollowed and hungry.

Madness The Whispering

The Present Torment

Year of the Bleeding Star, 2024

Now the Blight deepens. New afflictions manifest daily. The corrupted lands expand, consuming what little remains of the old world. Survivors huddle in fortified enclaves, praying to gods who long ago turned their faces away. Hope is a luxury none can afford.

The Mirror of Shattered Selves

Class: Eldritch Relic

Crafted from obsidian glass by the Mad Artificer Valthor, this mirror shows not your reflection, but every version of you that suffered a worse fate. Gazing too long causes the reflections to reach through the glass.

The Crown of the Weeping King

Class: Sovereign Curse

Once worn by King Aldric the Broken, this iron circlet fuses to the wearer's skull. It grants visions of the future—only tragedies, only endings. Every king who wore it abdicated within a year. Or worse.

The Tolling Bell of Morwen

Class: Sonic Abomination

A handbell that rings on its own at midnight. Each toll causes those within earshot to experience the death of someone they loved. The bell has never been silenced. It is currently housed in the deepest dungeon of the Silent Monastery.
