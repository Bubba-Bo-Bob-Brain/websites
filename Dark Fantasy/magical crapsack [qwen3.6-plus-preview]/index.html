I

The Waning Chronicle

◆

In the beginning, the gods forged the world in fire and blood. They sang the mountains into being and wept the oceans into existence. But their voices grew hoarse, and their tears ran dry. Now, in the final epoch known as the Age of Ash, the world is a husk — a rotting carcass upon which the remnants of civilization feed like carrion birds.

The sky has not known the sun in three centuries. A perpetual shroud of volcanic soot and necrotic miasma chokes the heavens, casting the land in an eternal, bruised twilight. The few stars that pierce through are said to be the eyes of dead gods, watching in silent judgment as their creation unravels.

Humanity clings to existence in fortified enclaves, walled cities built atop the bones of older, greater civilizations. Outside these walls, the Blighted Wastes stretch endlessly — a landscape of cracked earth, petrified forests, and rivers that run thick with ash and the occasional unidentifiable fluid that was once called water.

The Three Great Witherings

  • First The Sundering of the Firmament — when the sky cracked and the poisonous vapors of the deep earth seeped into the world above.
  • Second The Blood Plague — a sentient disease that consumed three-quarters of all living things and left behind the Rotlands.
  • Third The Silence of the Gods — the day all prayer ceased to be answered, and the divine withdrew from mortal affairs forever.

Reliquary of the Damned

Cursed Artifacts and Relics of Unspeakable Power

◆

The Crown of Thorns Eternal

Forged from the nails that crucified the Last Prophet. Wearing it grants visions of the future — each one more horrific than the last. The thorns grow deeper with each vision.

Artifact Class: Divine

The Mirror of Unbecoming

A silvered glass that shows not your reflection, but the creature you will become when the world ends. Gazing too long causes the transformation to accelerate.

Artifact Class: Eldritch

Godsbane, the Betrayer's Edge

A blade that drinks the divinity from anything it cuts. It has killed three gods and grown sentient with each one. It whispers to its wielder, driving them to greater acts of deicide.

Artifact Class: Godslayer

The Chalice of Endless Thirst

Fills with whatever liquid the drinker most desires. But each sip deepens an unquenchable thirst. Those who drink from it eventually consume themselves from the inside out.

Artifact Class: Torment

The Chains of the Penitent King

Once bound the wrists of the first tyrant. They cannot be broken, only transferred. Whoever wears them feels every pain they have ever inflicted upon others, simultaneously.

Artifact Class: Judgment

The Tome of Final Words

Records the last words of every creature that dies. The book is never full. Reading aloud the entries summons the spirits of the dead — but they are not pleased to be disturbed.

Artifact Class: Necrotic
