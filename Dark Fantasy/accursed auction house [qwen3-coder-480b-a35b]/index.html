Relic Chronicles

The Crimson Grimoire

Bound in the desiccated flesh of a heretic sorcerer, this tome was penned during the Obsidian Plague of 1347. Each page is inscribed with blood that never dries, and the ink shifts subtly when viewed peripherally. Opening the book requires a sacrifice - typically a drop of the reader's blood or a lock of hair from a loved one. Those who study its pages report dreams of burning cities and voices whispering in tongues that predate humanity.

The Grimoire's most notorious spell, "Invocation of the Hollow Crown," is said to summon a shade of the caster's future self - a being that knows all their failures and regrets. The ritual requires a full moon and the bones of a crow arranged in a pentagram around the reader.

Soulrend Blade

Forged in the ninth circle of Hell, this blade was tempered with the screams of damned souls. Its edge can cut through more than flesh - it severs the bonds between body and spirit. The weapon chooses its wielder, bonding with those who harbor deep resentment or desire for vengeance.

Each kill strengthens the bond between sword and wielder, but also corrupts the wielder's soul. After ten deaths caused by the blade, the wielder becomes bound to serve its will. Legends speak of a warrior who became one with the weapon, transforming into a wraith that hunts those who wronged him in life.
