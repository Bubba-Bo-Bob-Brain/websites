Catalog of Cursed Artifacts

Objects tainted by dark forces. Handle with extreme prejudice. Many bear the mark of the Damned.

🗡️
Curse Level: EXTREME

Blade of Eternal Agony

A sword that never dulls, but slowly consumes the wielder's vitality with each strike. Those who wield it eventually become mindless husks, forever swinging at shadows.

Origin: The Bloodforge Victims: 347

The blade whispers the names of its victims in the wielder's dreams. Eventually, the wielder's name is added to the list.

💍
Curse Level: HIGH

Ring of False Hope

Bestows temporary supernatural luck, but each fortunate event is balanced by a catastrophe elsewhere. The wearer is often blamed for the resulting tragedies.

Origin: Gambler's Bane Victims: 89

Those who remove the ring find that their "luck" persists as a curse—everything they touch decays.

📖
Curse Level: SEVERE

Tome of Forgotten Names

Contains the true names of 10,000 souls. Speaking a name grants absolute control over that person—until the tome's own name is spoken, binding the reader instead.

Origin: The Naming War Victims: 10,000+

The tome is slowly rewriting itself with the names of those who have read it. It is believed that when the last page fills, all whose names appear will perish simultaneously.

🪞
Curse Level: MODERATE

Mirror of Shifting Truths

Shows the viewer their deepest desire, but the reflection gradually replaces the viewer's memory of reality. Eventually, they cannot distinguish mirror from world.

Origin: Vanity's Price Victims: 56

The mirror sometimes shows futures that haven't happened yet—but only the terrible ones. These visions inevitably come to pass, making the mirror a self-fulfilling prophecy engine.

⏳
Curse Level: EXTREME

Hourglass of Stolen Time

Reverses aging for the owner, but the stolen years are taken from random victims elsewhere, who age rapidly and die. The hourglass is never empty.

Origin: Temporal Thief Victims: 1,203

When the hourglass runs out (it never does), the owner's time is forfeit—they cease to have ever existed.

🕯️
Curse Level: HIGH

Candle of Last Rites

Burns with black flame that never goes out. Its light reveals the manner of death for anyone nearby. Prolonged exposure causes the viewer's own death to appear in the flame.

Origin: Mourner's Bargain Victims: 78

The candle's flame is fed by the dying breaths of its victims. It is said that when it burns brightly, someone nearby will die within the hour.

Plague & Affliction Tracker

The diseases and curses that ravage our world. Monitor outbreaks. Understand symptoms. Despair accordingly.

The Withering

Severity:
85%

Symptoms

  • Gradual loss of color vision
  • Skin becomes brittle as parchment
  • Memory erosion (recent memories first)
  • 最终阶段: complete petrification

Transmission

Airborne spores from decayed victims. Contagious even after death.

Outbreak Status: CRITICAL

Soul Rot

Severity:
92%

Symptoms

  • Gradual emotional numbness
  • Inability to experience joy
  • Appetite for decay and corruption
  • 最终阶段: soulless husk, animated only by hunger

Transmission

Contact with cursed objects or places of great despair. Can be latent for years.

Outbreak Status: EPIDEMIC

Mindflay

Severity:
78%

Symptoms

  • Auditory hallucinations (whispers)
  • Paranoia and persecution complex
  • Telepathic link to other victims
  • 最终阶段: complete psychic disintegration

Transmission

Proximity to psychic entities or exposure to forbidden knowledge.

Outbreak Status: SPORADIC

Blood Plague

Severity:
95%

Symptoms

  • Blood turns black and viscous
  • Unquenchable thirst for fresh blood
  • Skin becomes cold and clammy
  • 最终阶段: transformation into mindless blood wraith

Transmission

Blood-to-blood contact. Highly contagious through bites or open wounds.

Outbreak Status: PANIC

Dark Ritual Calendar

Mark these dates. The veil between worlds thins. Sacrifices must be made. Or avoided. Depending on your alignment.

31 OCT

Grand Convergence

Three celestial bodies align over the Blackspire Mountains. The veil between planes is at its thinnest. Dark rituals performed on this night have triple potency.

Ritual Threat Level: APOCALYPTIC
11 NOV

Night of Whispers

The spirits of the forgotten speak through the wind. Those who listen may learn forbidden secrets—or be driven mad by the voices.

Omen Threat Level: HIGH
21 DEC

Winter Solstice Blood Moon

The longest night coincides with a rare blood moon. Ancient covenants demand a sacrifice of twelve pure souls to prevent the sun from rising again.

Sacrifice Threat Level: CATASTROPHIC
01 JAN

New Year's Binding

The first sunrise of the year binds or breaks ancient oaths. All curses are renewed or nullified. No one knows which until the dawn.

Omen Threat Level: UNKNOWN
14 FEB

Day of Broken Hearts

Not a celebration. On this day, all who have ever betrayed love are hunted by spectral hounds. The guilty are never safe.

Ritual Threat Level: SEVERE
20 MAR

Equinox of Balance

For one hour, light and dark are equal. During this time, the barrier between life and death dissolves. The dead walk freely, but so do the living—briefly.

Omen Threat Level: EXTREME
