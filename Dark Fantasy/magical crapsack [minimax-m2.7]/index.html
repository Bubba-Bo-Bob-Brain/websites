Cursed Artifacts

Chapter III

Relics of doom and instruments of suffering, these artifacts hunger for the anguish of mortals.

I

The Crown of Eternal Reign

Regalia of Dominion

Forged in the fires of the Abyss and cooled in the tears of a thousand dying kings, this crown grants absolute authority over all who gaze upon its wearer. But the price is absolute loneliness—every soul who wears it loses all they love, driven to watch empires crumble while they alone remain.

The Curse:
  • Immortality bound to the crown
  • All loved ones die by your hand
  • Memory of all who served you
⚠ Handle with extreme caution ⚠
II

Grimthorne, The Lieslayer

Sentient Weapon

A blade that cannot lie—for it speaks only truths too terrible to bear. Every strike reveals secrets the victim wished hidden. Its wielder knows every betrayal, every whispered lie, every hidden sin of those they strike. Few can bear its terrible knowledge without losing their minds.

The Curse:
  • Forced to witness all sins
  • Cannot lie to others
  • Every kill destroys a memory
⚠ Truth is its weapon, your mind its sheath ⚠
III

The Lantern of Final Light

Relic of Hope's Last Stand

Said to contain the last ember of the dying sun, this lantern provides light in the endless darkness. But as the flame dims, so too does hope fade from the world. When it finally gutters out, only shadow shall remain. Its keepers guard it jealously, knowing the fate it holds.

The Curse:
  • Ember fades with hope
  • Keeper bound until death
  • World darkens as it dims
⚠ The last light trembles ⚠
IV

The Mirror of Sorrows

Divination Device

Gaze into its surface and witness the suffering of all souls across the realm. The mirror shows no past or future—only the endless present suffering of mortals. It is said the mirror is actually alive, drinking in the grief of all who look upon it, growing stronger with each viewing.

The Curse:
  • Compassion fatigue
  • Cannot look away
  • Mirror feeds on tears
⚠ Witness all, feel nothing ⚠
V

The Chalice of Endless Thirst

Vessel of the Insatiable

A vessel that can never be filled, no matter what liquid is poured within. It demands blood exclusively, and the more it drinks, the greater the thirst becomes. Its keeper is consumed by an unending desire that transforms them into a monster of insatiable hunger.

The Curse:
  • Unending blood thirst
  • Cannot be destroyed
  • Bonds to nearest soul
⚠ Never pour, never drink ⚠

The Chronicles of Woe

Chapter V

Events of Note

Year 0 AE

The First Blight

From the Wound in Reality emerged the Blight. The sky turned ash-grey, and every living soul felt a piece of themselves wither and die. The gods themselves fled, leaving mortals to face the darkness alone.

42 AE

The Fall of Valdris

The great city of Valdris, jewel of the north, sank into a crater of its own making. The screams of its million inhabitants lasted for seven days and seven nights.

156 AE

The Plague of Kindness

A madness spread that made victims incapable of cruelty. They starved trying to share their last bread. The Blight considered this a great joke.

289 AE

The Silence War

Three factions fought for control of the last sound—music, voice, and whisper. All lost. Now only the void speaks.

412 AE

The Broken Crown

The last true king shattered his crown rather than rule a dead kingdom. The shards became cursed relics that still cause suffering to this day.

523 AE

The Last Hope

A child was born with the power to end the Blight. The Blight found him first. There are no more prophecies.

666 AE

The Current Age

The End Times approach. The Misery Index climbs ever higher. The final ritual draws near. There is only darkness now.
