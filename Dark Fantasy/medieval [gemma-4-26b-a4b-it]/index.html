The Age of Ash

I

t began not with a roar, but with a whisper in the dark. The stars themselves seemed to dim, as if the very heavens were retreating from the encroaching void. The Great Accord was shattered by the touch of the Void-Touched, and the kings of old fell like autumn leaves in a gale of shadow.

Now, only the embers remain. The kingdoms are but ghosts, haunting the ruins of their own ambition. We wander through the mist, searching for a light that may have long since been extinguished.
