The Ominous Prophecy

✧

In the seventh moon of the dying year, when the veils between worlds grow thin as spider's silk, the shadowed ones shall walk among mortals once more. Their coming shall be heralded by three signs:

  • The rivers shall run black as ink
  • The forests shall fall silent, bereft of birdsong
  • The stars shall arrange themselves in the shape of a coiled serpent

Only those who bear the mark of the ancient bloodline, who carry fire in their hearts and steel in their souls, may stand against the encroaching darkness. The time of reckoning draws near.

Transcribed in the Year of Our Shadow, 1347

This codex is bound in the hide of a beast long forgotten. Handle with reverence, for some knowledge is a burden.
