The Mirror of Erised Black

Shows the viewer not their desires, but their damnation.

[ Reveal Lore ]

Crafted by a blind mirror-smith who worked only at midnight using mercury harvested from the blood of werewolves. The mirror does not reflect light — it reflects truth. Those who gaze into it for more than seven seconds begin to see themselves as they truly exist in the spiritual realm: rotting, hollow, and afraid. Three documented viewers have taken their own lives. The fourth viewer's name has been stricken from all records.

The Locket of Twin Hearts

Two hearts beat inside. Neither belongs to the wearer.

[ Reveal Lore ]

Opened once every century by its keeper, the locket contains two still-beating hearts belonging to lovers who betrayed each other in a past life. When worn, the locker forces overwhelming empathy upon the bearer — they feel every pain, every betrayal, every dying breath of every soul within a hundred-yard radius. The current keeper hasn't slept in nine years.

⚠ Previous bearers report hearing heartbeats in their dreams for years after parting with the locket.
Est. Value: 9,500–14,000 🩸 8 bids

The Hourglass of Borrowed Time

Flip it once, and someone, somewhere, dies young.

[ Reveal Lore ]

Each grain of sand in this hourglass was once a human life — stolen. When turned, the sand flows and somewhere in the world, a random soul's remaining years are siphoned into the glass. The original owner, a Chronomancer named Thessaly Vorne, aged backward until she became an infant, then ceased to exist entirely. The hourglass has been flipped 1,247 times. Each flip is logged in a ledger that bleeds when opened.

⚠ The auction house accepts no liability for temporal displacement, premature death, or existential dread experienced by the winning bidder.
Est. Value: 40,000–60,000 🩸 31 bids

The Quill of Final Words

Whatever it writes becomes the person's last sentence.

[ Reveal Lore ]

Plucked from a phoenix that died mid-flight, this quill writes in blood that isn't the writer's. When someone uses it to compose a letter, the recipient reads it as their own final words before dying. The quill doesn't discriminate — it delivers to anyone the writer addresses, even if the delivery takes decades. It has never missed.

⚠ Writing your own name with this quill is strongly, desperately, universally discouraged.
Est. Value: 22,000–30,000 🩸 19 bids
