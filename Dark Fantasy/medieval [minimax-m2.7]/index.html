The Prophecy of the Eternal Night

I. When the three moons align in darkness' embrace,

And the last dragon's heart ceases its pace,

The Shadow King shall rise from his tomb of flame,

To reclaim the throne where darkness holds claim.

II. The ancient pact shall break, the seals turn to rust,

And from the Abyss will emerge broken trust.

Seven heroes must rise, or the world shall fall,

To answer the call of the darkness' recall.

III. Only by blood and sacrifice shall the light return,

When the old gods weep and the new gods yearn.

Seek the fragments scattered across the broken land,

Before darkness consumes the world entire and grand.

The Dark Chronicles

Historical accounts of the realm's darkest hours, preserved by the monks of the Obsidian Monastery.

The First Binding

Age of Dawn, Year 1

In the time before memory, the great gods waged war against the Primordials. The battle lasted three hundred years, ending when the Hero-King Aldric bound the shadow entity Malachar into the Abyss using the Seven Seals of Power.

"From darkness we came, to darkness we shall return."

— Inscription on the Seal of Dawn

The Fall of Valdris

Age of Strife, Year 547

The once-great city of Valdris, jewel of the northern realm, fell in a single night. The Shadow Legion emerged from the mountains, their numbers endless, their hunger eternal. None survived to tell of the horrors within.

⚠ Warning: The ruins remain cursed to this day. Entry is forbidden by order of the Council.

The Prophecy Revealed

Age of Shadows, Year 892

The Oracle of the Black Lake spoke the prophecy that haunts us still. The Seals weaken. The Shadow King stirs. The end approaches, heralded by the alignment of the three moons and the awakening of ancient evils.

"Seven shall rise, bound by fate and blood, to face the darkness or be consumed by it."

The Call to Arms

Current Year, Year 1003

The shadows grow longer. The creatures multiply. The people whisper of sightings near the borders—of things that should not exist, moving in the darkness. The Council of Light has issued a call to adventure, seeking those brave or foolish enough to face the coming darkness.

Will you answer the call?
