The Veiled Prophecy

When the seventh moon bleeds crimson over the Obsidian Spires, the Gates of Mourning shall creak upon rusted hinges. From the deep, the Silent King shall rise, his crown of forgotten screams echoing in hollow places. Three signs shall herald the unraveling: The weeping of stone, the laughter of shadows, and the breath of the void upon mortal flesh. Only the blood of the oathbreaker can seal what was sundered. Thus speaks the Last Chronicler, in ink mixed with ash and regret.
