ᚦ The Prophecy ᚦ

Spoken by the Blind Oracle of the Ashen Spire, Third Moon of the Waning Age

═══════ ೋღ❦ ღೋ ═══════

When the Obsidian Throne crumbles to dust and the seven kingdoms bleed beneath a weeping sky, there shall come one who is Neither Living Nor Dead.

⸻ ❖ ⸻

From the Ashen Wastes beyond the Pale, where forgotten gods dream their endless slumber, the Horn of Valdrimor shall sound thrice— and the Veil between worlds shall tear like rotten silk.

⸻ ❖ ⸻

Three signs shall herald the coming dark: a moon that bleeds, a crown without a king, and a child born of shadow who shall speak with the voice of the damned.

⸻ ❖ ⸻

Let those who would resist gather their steel and their prayers, for the Age of Cinders is upon us— and only by the blood of the Covenant Eternal may the world be unmade and forged anew.

ᛞ Bestiary of the Damned ᛞ

The Hollow Knight

Classification: Undead Revenant
Threat: ☠ ☠ ☠ ☠ ☠

Once a paladin of the Silver Covenant, Sir Aldric the Undying refused death itself. His armor fused with bone and rust, he wanders the Ashenmoor, seeking the battle that will grant him final rest. His sword still gleams with holy light—though it cuts both the living and the dead.

⚐ Ashenmoor Wastes, Ruins of Castle Thornwall

The Weeping Seraph

Classification: Fallen Celestial
Threat: ☠ ☠ ☠ ☠ ☠

Cast from the Celestial Choir for the sin of pity, the Seraph's tears fell upon the mortal realm and became rivers of sorrow. Its six wings are tattered and burned, yet its presence causes all who gaze upon it to weep uncontrollably. It does not attack—it merely watches, and weeps, and remembers.

⚐ The Valley of Lamentations, Cathedral of Lost Faith

The Bone Colossus

Classification: Construct — Necromantic
Threat: ☠ ☠ ☠ ☠ ☠

A towering amalgamation of ten thousand bones, assembled by the Lich-King Morvath during the Siege of the Last Bastion. It walks with the weight of an earthquake, and its roar is the chorus of every soul consumed in its creation. It cannot be killed—only scattered, and it always rebuilds.

⚐ The Shattered Plains, Morvath's Ossuary

The Whisperwood

Classification: Corrupted Fey
Threat: ☠ ☠ ☠ ☠ ☠

Not a single creature but an entire forest that has achieved a malevolent sentience. The trees speak in the voices of those who died within their shade, luring travelers deeper into the grove. Those who enter rarely leave—and those who do are never quite the same, hearing the whispers for the rest of their days.

⚐ The Whispering Glade, Eldwood Forest

ᚠ The Lore Archives ᚠ

Recovered fragments from the Library of Ashenmoor, translated from Old Valdric

═══════ ೋ📜 ೋ ═══════
⚔ The Silver Covenant
F

Founded in the Year of the First Dawn, the Silver Covenant was an order of paladins sworn to protect the mortal realms from the horrors that lurk beyond the Veil. For a thousand years, they were the bulwark against darkness—until the Betrayal at Thornwall, when their greatest champion, Sir Aldric, was struck down by the very king he had sworn to protect. His death shattered the Covenant, and the order scattered to the winds. Some say Aldric did not truly die that day. Some say he walks still.

🔮 The Veil Between Worlds
T

The Veil is not a wall but a wound—an injury in the fabric of reality inflicted during the War of the First Gods. Through this wound seeps the raw essence of the Shadow Realm, a dimension of pure entropy and hunger. The Veil grows thinner with each passing age, and the creatures that press against it grow bolder. The Oracle's prophecy speaks of its final tearing. Should the Veil fall entirely, the Shadow Realm will consume all that exists.

🐉 Valdrimor the Ashen
V

The last of the Elder Drakes, Valdrimor slumbers within the volcanic heart of Mount Cinderpeak. Unlike its kin—beasts of fire and fury—Valdrimor is a creature of ash and prophecy. Its scales are the color of cooling lava, and its breath does not burn but withers, aging all it touches by centuries. The Horn of Valdrimor, a relic forged from one of its own shed scales, is said to have the power to wake the drake—or to command it. Many have sought the Horn. None have returned.

🔥 The Age of Cinders
A

Every civilization has its twilight. The sages of the Ashen Spire believe that the world exists in cycles—Ages of Gold followed by Ages of Rust, Ages of Light swallowed by Ages of Shadow. The coming Age of Cingers will be the final age: a time when the sun grows dim, the seas turn to steam, and the very mountains crack and bleed fire. It is not merely an apocalypse but a transformation—the world dying to be reborn, though what emerges from the ashes may bear no resemblance to what came before.
