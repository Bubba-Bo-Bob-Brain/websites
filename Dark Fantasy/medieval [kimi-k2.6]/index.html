The Shattered Realms

◆

Ashborne

The fallen kingdom, where castles crumble into dust and the wind carries whispers of forgotten glory. Once the seat of the Sun Throne, now ruled by specters and sorrow.

Sovereign: None — Abandoned
Population: Estimated 300 souls, hiding in the catacombs
▲

The Blackspire Mountains

Jagged peaks that pierce the clouds, home to the Obsidian Monasteries where blind monks chant in tongues no living man comprehends.

Sovereign: The Nameless Abbot
Population: Unknown — the monks do not count their dead
◐

The Sunken Mire

Endless wetlands where the dead do not rest easy. Will-o'-wisps lead travelers to watery graves, and ancient things stir in the peat.

Sovereign: The Lady of Reeds
Population: None that draw breath
✤

Thornwall

The last bastion of mortal resistance, where living walls of black rose protect what remains of humanity's defiance.

Sovereign: Queen-Regent Isolde the Bleeding
Population: 12,000 — dwindling

The Black Chronicle

1287

The Night of Unmaking

The sky tore open above the Sun Throne, and something looked through. King Aldric the Beloved was found the next morning, his eyes turned to glass, still smiling. His last decree, scratched into his own skin with fingernails, commanded the realm to "prepare the feast."

Witness: Brother Cadfael — sole survivor of the royal guard. Committed to the Blackspire monks. Never spoke again.
1288

The March of the Hollow

Three thousand knights rode from Ashborne to seal the wound in the sky. Their armor returned riderless, marching in perfect formation. They patrol still, guarding roads that lead nowhere, challenging travelers who died centuries ago.

The armor cannot be destroyed. It reforms within the moon's cycle. Do not approach the crest — the hollow still remember heraldry.
1312

The Sinking of Mirehollow

The town simply ceased to be above ground. Survivors speak of the earth "opening like a mouth." The Lady of Reeds emerged from the waters for the first time, crowned in river weed, and spoke a single word that every listener understood differently. All agree it was a name.

Those who heard their own name are now counted among her court. They walk the wetlands at twilight, inviting others to "come home."
1345

The Bleeding of the Thorns

Queen-Regent Isolde offered her blood to the living walls of Thornwall, and they answered. The roses bloom black now, and their thorns grow inward. She has not aged a day since. Her courtiers whisper that she no longer sleeps, only stands at her window, watching the horizon.

The thorns drink weekly now. Volunteers are... increasingly difficult to find. The Queen offers her own veins, but the roses reject her. They want variety.
