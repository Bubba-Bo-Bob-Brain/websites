⚱ The Cursed Artifact Vault

Relics of unbearable misfortune — touch at your own peril

The Soul Ripper Dagger

Forged in the blood of a dying star by the First Assassin

Each kill steals a fragment of the wielder's own soul. After a thousand victims, the wielder becomes nothing more than a hollow vessel — a weapon without a hand.

Victims: 947 Current Wielder: Unknown Containment: Iron box, sealed with holy wax

The Weeping Crown

Worn by the Mad King who drowned his kingdom in tears

Those who wear it hear the constant weeping of the drowned. Sleep becomes impossible. Eventually, the wearer's own tears become a river that carries them to a watery grave.

The Bone Lantern

Carved from the fingerbone of a saint who renounced his faith

Its flame burns with the light of lost salvation. Those who gaze into it see the faces of everyone they have ever wronged. The light never goes out, and neither does the guilt.

The Black Heartstone

The still-beating heart of the World-Tree, corrupted by primordial evil

Those who hold it feel the slow, agonizing death of an entire world. Their own heart begins to beat in sync with its dying rhythm. Within a year, their heart stops forever.

The Mirror of Lament

Crafted from the frozen tears of a goddess who lost everything

It does not reflect your face. It shows you the person you were before the world broke you. The contrast is unbearable. Many have taken their own lives upon seeing what they have lost.
