The Prophecy of the Crimson Moon

When the seventh moon bleeds crimson upon the obsidian throne, the Veil shall tear asunder and the Forgotten Ones shall walk among mortals once more.

Thus speaks the Oracle of the Hollow Mountain, in the year of the Shattered Crown:

"Beware the child born under the Eclipse of Chains, for they shall carry the Mark of the Void.

In their left eye, the reflection of a thousand burning souls.

In their right, the silence before the end of all things."

The signs have begun to manifest. The ravens no longer sing. The ancient trees weep black sap. And in the deepest chambers of the earth, something stirs that should have remained buried for eternity.

The Forbidden Bestiary

The Shadow Wraith

☠☠☠☠☠

A manifestation of pure despair, born from the accumulated grief of a thousand deaths. It feeds on hope, leaving only hollow shells of those it touches.

Weakness: Cannot cross running water blessed by moonlight.

The Shadow Wraith was once a powerful sorcerer who attempted to achieve immortality by binding their soul to the Negative Material Plane. The ritual went horribly wrong, and they became the very thing they sought to control.

The Bone Collector

☠☠☠☠

An ancient construct of animated skeletons, held together by necromantic energy. It wanders the battlefields of old wars, gathering bones to add to its ever-growing form.

Weakness: Destroying the central skull disrupts the binding.

Legend says the Bone Collector was created by a dying general who refused to accept defeat. Their final spell bound their consciousness to the bones of their fallen army, creating an eternal warrior that knows no rest.

The Void Serpent

☠☠☠☠☠

A creature from the space between dimensions. Its body is a wound in reality itself, and to gaze upon it is to see the true nature of the cosmos—and go mad.

Weakness: Vulnerable to sounds of pure harmony.

The Void Serpent is said to be a fragment of the primordial chaos that existed before creation. It was imprisoned in the Abyss by the First Gods, but the chains grow weaker with each passing age.

Forbidden Rituals

The Rite of Unseeing

Required Components:

  • Three black candles made from the fat of an executed heretic
  • A mirror that has reflected a death
  • The tears of one who has lost everything
  • A circle drawn in salt mixed with grave dust

Warning: This ritual grants the ability to see spirits, but the user will never again know a moment of true peace. The dead will always be visible, always watching.

The Binding of Shadows

Required Components:

  • A shadow cast by no living thing
  • Silver chains forged during a lunar eclipse
  • The true name of the shadow entity
  • A willing sacrifice of one's reflection

Warning: The bound shadow will serve, but it will constantly seek to corrupt its master. Each use brings the practitioner closer to becoming a shadow themselves.

Ancient Lore

The Age of Myth

In the beginning, there was only the Void—an infinite expanse of nothingness. From this nothing, the First Gods emerged, born from the dreams of a sleeping cosmos.

The Sundering

The war between the Lightbringers and the Void Lords shattered the single world into the realms we know today. The scars of this war are the rifts between dimensions.

The Age of Mortals

When the gods withdrew from the mortal plane, they left behind fragments of their power. These artifacts, scattered across the world, are the source of all mortal magic—and all mortal folly.

The Coming Darkness

The prophecies speak of a time when the barriers between worlds will fail completely. The Void Lords will return, and the final war will begin. This is not a question of if, but when.
