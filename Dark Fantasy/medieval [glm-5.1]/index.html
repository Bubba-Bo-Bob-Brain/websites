The Prophecy

“From the blood of the fallen, the crown shall rise anew.”

I

When the last flame of Aldorath dims to ember,
And the seven towers crumble into the sea,
The Forgotten King shall stir beneath the mountain,
His chains of iron worn to dust by centuries.

II

The Bloodmoon shall rise on the winter solstice,
Casting its crimson gaze upon the broken lands,
And from the shadows, the Harrowed Ones shall march—
Wraiths of old, bound by oath and malice alike.

III

Only the bearer of the Ebon Sigil,
Forged in the heart of a dying star,
May seal the breach between worlds.
But the price shall be a soul's unmaking.

IV

Thus it was written in the Ashen Codex,
Thus it shall come to pass.
The wheel turns. The blood calls.
Ashes to ashes. Dust to dominion.

Bestiary of the Damned

“Know thy enemy, lest ye become their feast.”

The Harrowed Wraith

Classification: Spectral Undead

Bound by ancient oaths unfulfilled, these specters haunt the ruins of fallen keeps, their hollow cries echoing through corridors long abandoned by the living.

Threat
Rarity
Intellect

The Harrowed Wraiths were once knights of the Order of the Silver Dawn, sworn to protect the realm against the darkness. When the Betrayal of Ashenmoor occurred, their oaths were shattered and their souls torn from their bodies. Now they wander, neither living nor dead, seeking absolution that shall never come. Their touch drains the warmth from mortal flesh, leaving victims as cold husks eternally trapped between worlds.

Weakness: Moon-touched silver, rowan ash, and the spoken names of their former lives.

The Ashen Stag

Classification: Corrupted Beast

Once a noble guardian of the Thornwood, the Ashen Stag was warped by the Blight, its antlers now burning with unholy fire that consumes all it touches.

Threat
Rarity
Intellect

Before the Blight, the Ashen Stag was revered by the woodland folk as a spirit of renewal. Each autumn, it would shed its antlers and new growth would follow. The corruption twisted this cycle — now its antlers burn endlessly, and where they touch the earth, the soil becomes barren ash. It roams the charred remains of the Thornwood, a tragic monument to what was lost.

Weakness: Pure spring water from the Well of Dusk, and the song of the ash-born lark.

The Flesh Weaver

Classification: Abomination

A horrific amalgamation of countless bodies stitched together by dark alchemy, the Flesh Weaver lurks in the catacombs beneath the City of Grey Spires.

Threat
Rarity
Intellect

Created by the Mad Alchemist Vorath in his obsession with conquering death, the Flesh Weaver is a sentient tapestry of suffering. It possesses a hive awareness drawn from the countless minds trapped within it, whispering in a chorus of the damned. It is said to weep as it kills, for each death adds another voice to its eternal torment. None who have entered the deep catacombs have returned to speak of it — only their screams echo upward through the drains.

Weakness: Fire of the consecrated kind, and the alchemical solvent known as Vorath's Remorse.

The Hollow Drake

Classification: Undead Drake

The last of the drakes, raised from extinction by the Forgotten King's command. Its bones gleam white against the ashen sky, and its breath is the cold of the void itself.

Threat
Rarity
Intellect

Before its raising, the Hollow Drake was Valtherion, greatest of the Storm Drakes, who once served as mount to the High King Aldorath. When the kingdom fell, Valtherion was slain and its bones sealed beneath the mountain. Now animated by the Forgotten King's will, it retains a shadow of its former intelligence — and with it, a sorrow that makes the very air weep when it passes. Some say that within its hollow skull, a single ember of its old soul still burns, waiting to be freed.

Weakness: The Ebon Sigil alone can sever its bond to the Forgotten King. Otherwise, it is beyond mortal harm.

The Shadow Sister

Classification: Spectral Undead

Three sisters who made a pact with the void, now existing as one entity that shifts between three forms — maiden, mother, and crone — each more terrifying than the last.

Threat
Rarity
Intellect

The Shadow Sisters — Elara, Morgwen, and Sylaise — were once powerful seers who sought to peer beyond the veil of death. Their ritual succeeded, but at a terrible cost: their bodies were consumed and their souls merged into a single entity that exists in perpetual flux between three aspects. The Maiden lures the curious with prophecy; the Mother offers forbidden knowledge; the Crone devours. Together, they are the most cunning predator in the dark.

Weakness: The names of the dead must never be spoken in their presence, for it reminds them of what they lost.

The Grave Hound

Classification: Corrupted Beast

Pack hunters that roam the moors at night, their howls driving mortals to madness. Their eyes burn with a sickly green luminescence, and their jaws can crush enchanted steel.

Threat
Rarity
Intellect

Grave Hounds were once loyal hunting companions to the lords of the moorlands. When the Blight spread, it twisted them into relentless predators with an insatiable hunger for living flesh. They hunt in packs of six to twelve, coordinating with uncanny precision. Their howls carry a enchantment that induces despair and paralysis in those who hear them, making their prey easy to bring down.

Weakness: Wolfbane, iron bells, and the scent of fresh rain.

Chronicle of Ruin

“Thus the ages turn, each darker than the last.”

The First Age — The Age of Light

Year 0 — Year 1,200

The gods walked among mortals, and the realm of Aldorath flourished under the reign of the Sunborn Kings. Great cities of white stone rose from the earth, and the drakes soared freely across boundless skies. Peace reigned, but it was the peace of a candle unaware of the dark.

The Second Age — The Age of Iron

Year 1,201 — Year 2,800

The gods withdrew, leaving mortals to their own devices. Kingdoms rose and fell in cycles of conquest. The Order of the Silver Dawn was founded to guard against the darkness, and the drakes were bound as mounts to the great kings. The first whispers of the Blight appeared in the far north.

The Third Age — The Age of Ash

Year 2,801 — Year 3,500

The Betrayal of Ashenmoor shattered the Order. The Forgotten King was sealed beneath the mountain, and the Blight spread across the land. The drakes were hunted to extinction. Cities crumbled. The Thornwood burned. What remains is a dying realm, clinging to the last embers of hope — awaiting the fulfillment of the Prophecy.

The Fourth Age — The Age to Come

Year 3,501 — ?

The Bloodmoon approaches. The Forgotten King stirs. The Ebon Sigil remains lost. This age has not yet begun — or perhaps, it has already begun, and we simply do not yet perceive the shape of our doom.
