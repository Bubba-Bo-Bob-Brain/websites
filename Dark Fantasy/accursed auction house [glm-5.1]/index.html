Bidding Rules

I

Blood Binds the Bid

Every bid is a binding pact. To raise is to commit. There is no retreat without consequence.

II

The Seal Must Break

Cursed items are veiled by protective seals. Only those who dare break them may see the relic within.

III

Soul Fragments Only

Gold is worthless here. All bids are measured in Soul Fragments — the only currency the dead accept.

IV

When the Flame Dies

Each auction ends when its candelabra flame burns to nothing. The final bid before darkness claims the relic.

V

The Exchange Takes Its Cut

Upon sale, the Obsidian Exchange claims 10% of the winning bid in soul tax. This is non-negotiable.

VI

Ownership Is Conditional

Cursed relics may claim their owners. The Exchange bears no responsibility for hauntings, possessions, or unraveling of reality.
