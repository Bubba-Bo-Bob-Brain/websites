The Catalogue of Afflictions

Every curse has a name. Every name has a price.

☠

The Hollowbone Curse

Bones become porous, light as ash. Victims hear whispering from within...

First recorded in the Black Year, when the grave-digger Malach felt his femur crumble to dust while still walking. The curse spreads through shared meals, through breath, through love. There is no cure. There is only the waiting, and the sound — like wind through a flute made of dead men's ribs.

Symptoms: Weight loss exceeding physical matter. Auditory hallucinations of ancestral voices. Eventual skeletal dissolution while conscious.

Known Vessels: 342 confirmed. Estimated true count: 12,000+

◐

Echo-Self Affliction

Your reflection moves independently. It learns. It wants.

The mirror-touched do not sleep. They watch their echoes watch them back, growing bolder with each passing night. Some echoes weep. Some rage. All eventually reach through — first with words, then with fingers that emerge from glass like birth through blood.

Symptoms: Delayed reflection movement. Independent eye contact. Physical contact attempts. Complete replacement.

Known Vessels: Unknown. Echoes do not report themselves.

❀

Graysweet Longing

The taste of all things fades, save for one impossible flavor never to be found again.

The afflicted wander, sampling every substance in creation — stone, flame, their own flesh — seeking the flavor that haunts their tongue. Most starve. Some become gourmands of atrocity, convinced that suffering itself might carry the notes they crave.

Symptoms: Complete taste anosmia except phantom flavor. Obsessive consumption. Self-cannibalism in terminal stages.

Known Vessels: 8,901. High recurrence in former chefs and midwives.

✙

The Wound of Faith

Belief itself becomes physical. Prayers manifest as bleeding sores.

The gods are not dead. Worse — they are attentive. Every prayer answered in flesh, every devotion marked in scar tissue. The most pious bear the most wounds. The saints are unrecognizable, holy shapes beneath crusted layers of answered pleas.

Symptoms: Prayer-responsive lesion formation. Stigmata corresponding to deity iconography. Terminal transfiguration.

Known Vessels: All clergy. All believers. The gods listen to everyone now.

◴

Time-Debt Consumption

You remember futures that never were. The debt of possibility devours your present.

Every choice unmade accumulates interest. The time-debtors live multiple lives in parallel memory, each phantom existence demanding acknowledgment, each road not taken subtracting from the road they walk. Childhoods extend to senility. Deaths precede birth.

Symptoms: Anachronistic memory intrusion. Temporal dissociation. Spontaneous aging/rejuvenation. Causal collapse.

Known Vessels: Theorized universal. Most minds filter effectively. For now.

♥

Compassion Rot

Love festers. Kindness kills what it touches.

The last kind man in Ashenmarch embraced his daughter goodnight and woke to find her blackened, sweet-smelling, dead from the toxin of his affection. Now he walks the wastes, warning others with gestures, speaking never, touching nothing.

Symptoms: Lethal pheromone production in emotional states. Accelerated decomposition in loved ones. Isolation compulsion.

Known Vessels: 67. Deliberately underreported. Communities hide their cursed.

Thornveil Grasp

Habitability: None Corruption: 94% Survivors: Estimated 200

Once the Verdant Crown, breadbasket of three kingdoms. Now the thorns grow through the thorns — recursive vegetable agony, each bramble spawning smaller brambles in fractal descent. The air itself cuts. Breathers emerge from the Grasp speaking in the voice of the forest, which has no voice, which speaks only in the negative space of screams.

The Thornheart at the center pulses with something between sap and blood. Pilgrims seek it. None return unchanged. Most do not return.

Active Hazards

  • Sanguinary Pollen — induces compulsive self-embedding
  • Rootmarch — underground migration of predatory tubers
  • The Verdant Choir — collective vegetative consciousness, hostile

The Sunken Kingdom

Habitability: Drowned Corruption: 87% Survivors: None confirmed

The kingdom of Val Moraine drowned in a single night, not in water but in something thicker, something that preserved as it suffocated. The spires still stand, visible through the amber surface, and within them the court still dances — frozen in their final ball, moving now only in the tides that pass through their medium.

Divers who penetrate the deep return with amber in their lungs, with the kingdom's music in their ears, with the desperate need to return and join the endless waltz.

Active Hazards

  • Amber Tides — periodic surges of preservative medium
  • The Waltz Compulsion — auditory memetic infection
  • Preservation Paradox — biological stasis incompatible with life

Ashenmarch

Habitability: Borderline Corruption: 76% Survivors: ~15,000 in nomadic bands

The great plain where nothing grows because everything already grew and died and burned and grew again in cycled agony until the soil itself became ash, became memory of growth, became the idea of fertility made mockery. The nomads of Ashenmarch follow the Ashwind, which carries seeds that never sprout, that only remind the earth of what it has lost.

Yet they persist, these walkers, these rememberers of green. Their defiance is perhaps the most terrible thing in all the world — hope where hope is proven poison.

Active Hazards

  • Ashwind Blindness — cumulative retinal scarring
  • Memory Seeds — psychoactive false germination
  • The Longing — collective grief-induced catatonia

The Last Hearth

Habitability: Fragile Corruption: 43% Survivors: ~80,000 fortified

The final city. The last place where fire warms without consuming, where children are born with the standard number of limbs, where the night is merely dark and not actively hostile. For now. The walls grow taller each year, not from confidence but from desperation, from the need to look away from what approaches.

Within, the Hearthkeepers maintain the Flame That Must Not Die, though none remember why it must not, though its fuel grows ever more questionable, though sometimes in its light one sees things that should not be seen by eyes still capable of closing.

Active Hazards

  • Refugee Burden — population exceeding sustainable capacity
  • Flame Dependency — psychological and possibly metaphysical
  • The Walled Eye — restricted knowledge of external conditions

Veil's Edge

Habitability: Unknown Corruption: 12% Survivors: None / All

Where the world ends, or began, or both. The corruption is lightest here, which makes it most terrible — one can see what was lost, what could have been, the green and the gold and the blue that once were. The Edge is a wound in perception, a place where the Skywound is visible as it truly is: not a scar but a door, not closed but waiting.

Those who walk to the Edge do not return as walkers. They return as witnesses, or they do not return at all, or they return before they left, carrying warnings that arrived too early and too late simultaneously.

Active Hazards

  • Temporal Shear — causality instability
  • Perception Rupture — reality differentiation failure
  • The Door — unknown, possibly unknowable

The Urn of Unspun Thread

Origin: The Sunken Kingdom, pre-drowning Threat Level: Calamitous

Contains the thread that was never woven, the life unlived, the possibility made vessel. To open the Urn is to release a person who never was into existence — and they are always hungry for the reality they were denied.

Documented Effects

  • Spontaneous generation of complete individuals with false memories
  • Reality rejection in local area — physics becomes negotiable
  • Existential debt accumulation in observers

Last Known Bearer

The Seamstress-General of Val Moraine, who drowned with it rather than open it. Her corpse, recovered, still clutches the sealed vessel. Her corpse, examined, has been trying to open it since recovery.

The Consensus Mirror

Origin: Unknown — possibly extra-dimensional Threat Level: Existential

Reflects not what is, but what is agreed upon. In a world of consensus, it would be harmless. In a world of fractured perception, it is a weapon of mass reality. The Last Hearth has buried it seventeen times. It returns, always, to the center of their deliberations.

Documented Effects

  • Democratic reality alteration — majority perception becomes truth
  • Dissenter dissolution — those who disagree become less real
  • Eventual unanimity or void — no middle ground recorded

Last Known Bearer

The Council of Hearth, who use it only in absolute emergencies and never look directly at their own reflections. The current council has served for forty years without replacement. No one has noticed.

The Bell That Was Rung

Origin: Skywound Event, ground zero Threat Level: Apocalyptic

It rang once. The world broke. It has not been silenced, merely paused — the clapper held in place by mechanisms that require constant maintenance, by volunteers who know that when they fail, when their strength gives, the Bell will complete its note.

Documented Effects

  • First strike: atmospheric rupture, the Skywound
  • Second strike: theorized complete dimensional dissolution
  • Harmonic resonance: causes lesser bells to ring in sympathy

Last Known Bearer

The Mute Order, sworn to silence, maintaining the clapper's stillness through meditation and physical restraint. They do not sleep. They do not speak. They do not let go.

The Crown of Continuing

Origin: The Last Hearth, first foundation Threat Level: Insidious

Worn by the monarch of the Last Hearth, it ensures the kingdom persists — but not the monarch. Each bearer rules exactly until their death, which the Crown accelerates through the weight of necessary decisions. The kingdom prospers. The rulers age decades in months.

Documented Effects

  • Absolute strategic clarity — bearer always knows the necessary choice
  • Accelerated temporal experience — subjective years pass in days
  • Transfer compulsion — bearer must pass Crown before death

Last Known Bearer

Queen-Regnant Asha the Seventeenth, aged nine, who has ruled for three weeks and speaks with the weariness of a woman who has buried six children she never had time to conceive.

The Seed That Remembers

Origin: Veil's Edge, recovered by non-returned Threat Level: Unknown

A seed that contains the complete genetic memory of the world before — every tree, every flower, every blade of grass that ever grew. It could restore the green. It could also restore everything else that grew, that hunted, that the world needed to forget.

Documented Effects

  • Hypothetical ecological restoration
  • Hypothetical restoration of all prior ecologies, including predatory
  • Unknown germination requirements — possibly impossible

Last Known Bearer

None. The Seed waits in the vault, debated in every generation, too precious to destroy, too dangerous to plant. The longest argument in human history, conducted in whispers around something that might be hope.
