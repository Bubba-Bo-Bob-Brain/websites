The Ominous Vision

⚜

When the Crimson Moon rises thrice upon the Ashfall Mountains, and the stars weep tears of black glass, shall the Seal of Eternals break. The Void-Touched King stirs in his adamantine prison, whispering to the roots of the world.

Beware the Night of Shattered Chains, when the barriers between flesh and shadow grow thin as parchment. The Thirteen shall walk again, each bearing crowns forged from the bones of forgotten gods.

"From blood and bone and bitter stone, The Ancient ones reclaim their throne. When silence falls on raven's wing, The final doom shall darkness bring."

Thus spoke Archivist Malachar, before the madness took him. His final warning remains etched in Obsidian upon the walls of the sunken library.
