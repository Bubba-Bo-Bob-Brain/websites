Hanaiakamalama

The Southern Cross, suspended above the south celestial pole, guided navigators heading toward Aotearoa and the southern islands. When upright, it points directly south.

Navigation: Used to determine latitude when crossing to Tahiti and the Marquesas.

Carved wooden ki'i marked sacred sites, boundaries, and navigational landmarks. Each bore the mana of ancestors who crossed the ocean before.
