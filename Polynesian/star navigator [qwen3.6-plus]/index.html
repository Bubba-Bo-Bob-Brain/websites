The Path of the Stars

Wayfinding Knowledge

Ancient Polynesian navigators, or kilo hōkū, crossed thousands of miles of open ocean using only the stars, ocean swells, wind patterns, and wildlife behavior. This interactive chart honors their extraordinary knowledge.

Star Compass

The Hawaiian star compass divides the horizon into 32 houses, each associated with specific rising and setting stars. Move your cursor to rotate the star dome and explore different navigation houses.

Island Chains

Click on any island to reveal its zenith star—the star that passes directly overhead at that latitude. This was the primary method for determining latitude at sea.
