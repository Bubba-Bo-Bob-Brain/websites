Fragment VII: The Color Out of Space

It was not a color that eyes were meant to perceive. The farmer's wife described it as the sound of purple and the taste of ultraviolet. The well breathed with a rhythm that matched no living thing. Trees grew in spirals that violated Euclidean space.

Source: Arkham Asylum Records Date: 1882 (Approx.)
