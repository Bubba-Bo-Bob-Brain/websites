Cover Story

The Gilded Palaces of Fifth Avenue

By Marguerite Holloway | Photography by James Van Der Zee

The marble halls of Manhattan's grandest boulevard have become temples of modern luxury. From the gilded ballrooms of the Astors to the private galleries of the Rockefellers, a new aristocracy shapes the city's skyline.

Wrought iron gates open to reveal cascading fountains, while inside, Lalique crystal chandeliers scatter prismatic light across frescoed ceilings. The architecture speaks in a language of bold geometry and meticulous symmetry—a testament to the era's unapologetic opulence.

Yet beyond the facade of wealth lies a vibrant cultural renaissance. Harlem's jazz clubs, Greenwich Village's speakeasies, and the burgeoning film industry of Hollywood all feed a collective hunger for novelty and expression.
