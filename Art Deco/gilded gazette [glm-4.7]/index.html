FASHION & LEISURE

The Flapper's Manifesto

By Violet St. Clair • 5 Min Read

T

he silhouette has changed, and with it, the spirit of the woman. Gone are the restrictive corsets of the Victorian past; welcome the age of the 'garçonne'. It is a rebellion stitched in silk and sequins, a dance that begins at midnight and refuses to see the dawn. The hemlines may rise, but the stakes are higher than ever in the bustling streets of New York and Paris.

We explore the designers who are daring to cut the cloth, bringing a geometric boldness to evening wear that mirrors the skyscrapers climbing above our heads.

The Jazz Revolution

From the speakeasies of Chicago to the grand halls of Harlem, the trumpet sounds the call of a generation. It is syncopated, chaotic, and utterly brilliant.

Industrial Design

Steel, chrome, and glass. The machine age is not just about function; it is about a new kind of beauty—cold, hard, and breathtakingly fast.

ARCHITECTURE

Touching the Sky

The Chrysler Building stands as a testament to American ambition. Its terraced crown, clad in silvery stainless steel, pierces the clouds like a spear of the gods. Art Deco architecture is not merely construction; it is sculpture on a massive scale.
