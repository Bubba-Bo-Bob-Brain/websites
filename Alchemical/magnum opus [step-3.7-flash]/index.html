DE LAPIDE PHILOSOPHORVM

V

ISITA interiora terrae, rectificando invenies occultum lapidem. Visit the interior of the earth, and by rectifying you will find the hidden stone. This is the great secret of the Hermetic philosophers, concealed beneath layers of allegory and guarded by the most stringent oaths.

Solve et coagula.

Dissolve and coagulate. Through dissolution the old form dies; through coagulation the new form is born. The prima materia must be broken down into its constituent parts — the Sulfur, the Mercury, and the Salt — before it can be reassembled into the perfected substance known as the Lapis Philosophorum.

✦

PRIMA MATERIA: The original substance from which all metals are formed. It is the formless chaos, the blackness of Nigredo, the seed of potential. Without it, no work can begin.

MERCURIUS: The fluid principle, the spirit that binds and separates. It is the messenger of the gods, the quicksilver that dissolves the fixed and fixes the volatile.

SULFUR: The fiery principle, the soul of the metal. It gives color, combustibility, and the power of transformation. When purified, it becomes the golden tincture.

SAL: The crystalline principle, the body that preserves and fixes. It is the salt of the wise, the coagulated spirit that holds the form together through all changes.

☉ ☽ ☿

QVATVOR OPERATIONES

Nigredo — The blackening. The putrefaction of the material. All that was solid becomes black, corrupt, dead. In this darkness the seed of the new work is planted. Mercury and Sulfur unite in the blackness. The crow devours the matter.

Albedo — The whitening. From the black ash rises the white dove. The impurities are burned away. The soul is purified. The matter becomes white, pure, ready for the next stage. The moon reflects the sun.

Citrinitas — The yellowing. The golden light dawns. The sun rises from the darkness. The white matter takes on the golden color of the sun. The tincture begins to form. The dawning of the philosopher's consciousness.

Rubedo — The reddening. The final stage. The matter becomes red, the color of blood and fire and the rising sun. The philosopher's stone is complete. The Great Work is finished. Fiat Lux. Let there be light.

𓂀

The stone is not a stone. It is a state of being. It is the perfected human soul, reunited with its divine source. It is the golden flower of the alchemists, the water of life, the elixir that transmutes all base metals into gold — and all base souls into enlightened spirits.

Nota bene: The fire must be gentle at first, then increase.
