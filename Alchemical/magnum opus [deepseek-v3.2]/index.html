Recipe for the Prima Materia:

  • Take of the Mercury of the Philosophers one part
  • Of the Sulphur of the Wise one part
  • Mix in the Secret Fire until blackness appears
  • Purify seven times in the Dew of May
  • Coagulate in a sealed vessel for forty days
