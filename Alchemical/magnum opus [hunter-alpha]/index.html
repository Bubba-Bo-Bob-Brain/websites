Codex Secretus

Liber Primus

Sicut enim mundus creatus est ex confusione elementorum, sic et opus nostrum procedit ex materia confusa, quam philosophi vocant Chaos.

For as the world was created from the confusion of the elements, so our work proceeds from confused matter, which the philosophers call Chaos.

Nota bene: Prima materia est ✧

De Nigredine

Nigredo operis est putrefactio, quae est separatio animae a corpore. Mortificatio necessaria est, sine qua nulla resurrectio fieri potest.

The blackness of the work is putrefaction, which is the separation of the soul from the body. Mortification is necessary, without which no resurrection can occur.

☠ Mortificatio △

De Albedine

Post nigredinem venit albedo, luna super terram, purificatio per aquam vitae. Tunc anima mundatur a sordibus corporis.

After blackness comes whiteness, the moon upon the earth, purification by the water of life. Then the soul is cleansed of the filth of the body.

☽ Luna purificat ☽

De Citrinitate

Citrinitas est aurora procedens, sol oriens super terram. Calor ignis philosophici incipit manifestare sapientiam.

Citrinitas is the approaching dawn, the sun rising over the earth. The heat of the philosophical fire begins to manifest wisdom.

☉ Sol surgit ☉

De Rubedine

Rubedo est ultimus perfectionis gradus, lapis philosophorum completus. Tunc Rex ex sepulchro resurgit, coronatus rubra corona.

Redness is the final degree of perfection, the completed Philosopher's Stone. Then the King rises from the sepulcher, crowned with a red crown.

🜍 Lapis completus! 👑

Finis Operis

Opus consummatum est. Qui intelligit, intelligat. Finis coronat opus.

The work is consummated. Let he who understands, understand. The end crowns the work.
