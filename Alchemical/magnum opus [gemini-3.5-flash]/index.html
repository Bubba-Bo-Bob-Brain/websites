I. The Nigredo Phase (Blackness)

To enter the void, grind the cold bones of the Earth. Combine Plumbum (Lead) with Sal (Salt) under the shadow of Saturn. Let it dissolve in the crucible until all form is lost.

II. The Albedo Phase (Whiteness)

Wash the ash. Introduce Argentum (Silver) and purify the solution with the spirit of Mercurius. The white swan shall emerge from the dark waters.

III. The Citrinitas Phase (Yellowness)

Awaken the solar dawn. Grind Sulphur with the golden dust. A yellow light, the wisdom of the philosophers, must rise through intense heat.

IV. The Rubedo Phase (Redness)

The final marriage. Bring forth Aurum (Gold) and ignite the cosmic fire. The red elixir crystallizes, bringing eternity to the mortal plane.
