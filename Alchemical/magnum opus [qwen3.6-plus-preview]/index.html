📜 Codex Hermeticus

Folium I

De Nigredine

N

In the beginning of the Great Work, all matter must be reduced to its primal state — the prima materia. This is the stage of putrefaction, where the old self dies and decomposes.

"Nisi enim granum frumenti cadens in terram mortuum fuerit, ipsum solum manet."

— Unless the grain of wheat falls to the earth and dies, it remains alone.

Recipe for Nigredo:

  • Take the fire of calcination
  • Reduce to ashes
  • Dissolve in the waters of dissolution
  • Allow to breathe in putrefaction
Folium II

De Albedine

P

From the blackness emerges the white stone. The matter is purified, washed clean of its imperfections. The moon rises as the sun sets — this is the stage of ablution.

"Et tenebrae eam non comprehenderunt."

— And the darkness did not comprehend it.

Folium III

De Citrinitate

I

The yellow dawn breaks. Solar consciousness awakens within the matter. The citrination transforms the white stone into gold — not merely of metal, but of spirit.

Folium IV

De Rubedine

F

The final stage — the reddening. The Philosopher's Stone is born. All opposites unite. The work is complete. Lapis Philosophorum shines with eternal light.
