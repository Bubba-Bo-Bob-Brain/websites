Codex Hermeticus

⸖
✦ Liber I: De Materia Prima ✦

In the beginning, the Prima Materia lay formless in the darkness of the vas hermeticum. The adept must first descend into the nigredo — the blackening — where all that was known is dissolved in the athanor of contemplation.

"Solve et Coagula. Separabis a terra, et sic invenies lapidem."

✦ Stage I: Nigredo ✦

The black crow descends upon the work. Putrefactio transforms all substances to their prima materia. The alchemist must endure the dark night of the soul, for in death lies the seed of rebirth. The raven's head — Caput Corvi — marks the passage through annihilation.

⚠ The adept weeps at this stage — this is natural.
✦ Stage II: Albedo ✦

From the blackness rises the white dove — purification. The albedo washes the soul clean as lunar silver. The spirit is separated from the dross. One must bathe in the waters of aquae permanens, the permanent water that does not perish with the vessel.

☽ The Moon governs this phase. Silver flows like thought unbound.
✦ Stage III: Citrinitas ✦

The solar dawn breaks — citrinitas, the yellowing. Few adepts speak of this stage, for here the tincture begins to manifest. The work turns to the color of dawn, of saffron, of aurum potabile. The solar consciousness awakens within the crucible of the self.

☉ Gold sleeps within the silver — now it stirs.
✦ Stage IV: Rubedo ✦

The red king ascends. Rubedo — the reddening — is the marriage of Rex and Regina, the union of Sol and Luna in the chrysopoeia. The Philosopher's Stone is born: Lapis Philosophorum, the stone that is not a stone, capable of transmuting leaden existence into golden illumination.

"Et erit sicut lilium inter spinas, ita Rex inter filios regum."

🔴 The Work is complete. The Stone drinks its own tears and rejoices.
✦ The Rebis ✦

The Rebis — the doubled thing — is the final product: the unity of opposites, the marriage of Sulphur and Mercury, of Sun and Moon, of King and Queen. It is the Opus Magnum itself: the perfection of matter and spirit, the aurum philosophicum.

He who has ears, let him hear. The circle is closed. The work is done.
