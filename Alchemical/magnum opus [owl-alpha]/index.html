Liber de Compositione Lapidis

Primus Gradus — Nigredo

The work begins in darkness. The prima materia must be dissolved, corrupted, and reduced to the caput corvi — the raven's head. This is the putrefaction without which no generation can occur.

Heat the matter in the athanor until the blackness spreads uniformly. The dissolution of the fixed and the fixation of the volatile — this is the secret of the first gate.

"Solve et Coagula" — the first axiom of the art

Secundus Gradus — Albedo

From the blackened mass emerges the white. The albedo is the washing of the caput corvi, the lunar awakening. The matter now shines with the silver light of Diana.

Continue the gentle heat. The White Stone is now prepared — capable of transmuting base metals into silver. But the work is not yet complete. Seek the golden light.

"The Moon must marry the Sun" — coniunctio oppositorum

Tertius Gradus — Citrinitas

The yellow dawn breaks. Citrinitas is the solar awakening, the golden dawn that precedes the red sun. The matter now bears the color of ripe wheat and the light of Apollo.

Increase the fire. The matter passes through citrine to the threshold of red. The golden tincture is nearly achieved — but patience, the final work demands perfection.

"The Phoenix rises from the ashes" — per ignem ad lucem

Quartus Gradus — Rubedo

The Rubedo — the Great Red. The Rex is crowned. The Philosopher's Stone is achieved: the medicina universalis, capable of perfecting all things and transmuting base metals into pure gold.

The multiplication and projection are now possible. One grain transmutes a thousand. The Great Work is accomplished. Ite, missa est.

"Visita Interiora Terrae Rectificando Invenies Occultum Lapidem" — V.I.T.R.I.O.L.
