The accumulated wisdom of martial masters across generations, preserved in these scroll teachings.

拳 Fist Techniques (拳法)

直拳

Straight Fist

The foundation of all strikes. Drive power from the stance through the shoulder and arm, extending fully at the moment of impact.

Power: 65 Speed: 80 Range: Short

鞭拳

Whip Fist

A circular strike delivered with the back of the fist. Uses centrifugal force generated by hip rotation for devastating power.

Power: 85 Speed: 60 Range: Medium

掄拳

Swinging Punch

A wide arcing strike that builds momentum. Most powerful when chained into combinations after disrupting opponent's balance.

Power: 95 Speed: 45 Range: Long

腿 Leg Techniques (腿法)

踹腿

Heel Kick

A powerful thrusting kick using the heel. Drive upward into targets at head height or sweep low to unbalance opponents.

Power: 90 Speed: 55 Range: Long

橫踢

Side Kick

Chamber the knee high and thrust laterally with the blade of the foot. Excellent for keeping opponents at distance.

Power: 75 Speed: 70 Range: Medium

連環腿

Chain Kicks

A rapid series of three or more kicks in succession. Each kick sets up the next, creating an unstoppable assault.

Power: 60 Speed: 95 Variable

劍 Sword Techniques (劍法)

劈劍

Overhead Chop

Raise the sword high and bring it down with full body weight behind it. The signature technique of the Straight Sword style.

Power: 100 Speed: 40 Medium

撩劍

Rising Cut

Sweep the blade upward in a gentle arc. Used to deflect incoming attacks while simultaneously cutting the opponent's limbs.

Power: 55 Speed: 85 Medium

刺劍

Thrust

The fastest and most precise sword technique. Extend the blade in a straight line toward vital points. Victory in a single breath.

Power: 80 Speed: 100 Long
