传 Ancient Legends

壹

The Azure Dragon Emperor

◆

Three thousand years past, a child was born beneath the convergence of seven stars. By age twenty, he had unified the warring sects under a single banner. His blade, forged from meteorite iron and quenched in dragon blood, could split mountains with a casual swing.

At the height of his power, he ascended in broad daylight, leaving behind only his sword and a single verse carved into stone:

"The dragon soars beyond the clouds of men,
Yet leaves his claw-marks on the mortal den."
龙
贰

The White Crane Immortal

◆

No sect claimed her. No master taught her. The White Crane Immortal appeared from the northern wastes one winter morning, her steps leaving no mark upon the snow.

She dueled the Seven Demon Lords for three days and nights, never resting, never yielding. On the fourth dawn, the demons lay broken, and she had vanished as mysteriously as she came.

Those who glimpse a white crane in storm clouds whisper that she still watches, waiting for a worthy successor.

叁

The Broken Sword Alliance

◆

When the Demonic Sect threatened to engulf the righteous path in darkness, twelve sword cultivators made a desperate pact. Each shattered their beloved blades, forging the fragments into a single divine weapon.

The resulting sword could only be wielded by one who had no fear of death. Youngest among them, a girl of sixteen, took up the burden. She fought for seven days, and on the eighth, both she and the demon lord perished in a blinding flash.

The shattered remnants of that blade are scattered across the world, each piece said to grant a fraction of its power to the worthy.
