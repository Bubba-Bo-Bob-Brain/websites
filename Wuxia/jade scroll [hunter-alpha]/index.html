玄 武 真 經

The Mysterious Martial Sutra

天地玄黄，宇宙洪荒。
武道之途，始于足下，终于无极。
凡修此经者，需先正心诚意，
以静制动，以柔克刚。

Heaven and earth are dark and yellow, the cosmos vast and primeval.
The path of martial cultivation begins beneath one's feet and ends in the infinite.
Those who study this sutra must first rectify the heart and purify intent,
using stillness to overcome motion, softness to conquer hardness.

玄武 真經

武道無涯，學海無邊。
持卷修心，方得真傳。
江湖路遠，後會有期。

The martial way is boundless, the sea of knowledge without shore.
Hold this scroll, cultivate the heart, and the true transmission shall be yours.
The rivers and lakes stretch far - we shall meet again.
