Prologue

傳說遠古之時，有仙人授武經於黃帝，自此武道流傳人間。此卷乃千年古卷，記載天下武學精髓，含招式、修煉、內功之法。

Legend tells that in ancient times, immortals bestowed the Martial Scripture upon the Yellow Emperor, and thus the martial ways flowed into the mortal realm. This scroll, a relic of a thousand years, records the essence of all martial arts — techniques, cultivation methods, and the secrets of inner energy.
