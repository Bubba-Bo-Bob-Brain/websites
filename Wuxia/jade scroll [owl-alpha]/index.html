序文

Prologue

自太古混沌初開，天地靈氣充盈於山川河嶽之間。先賢觀鳥獸之行，察風雷之變，悟得導引吐納之術，以人體為鼎爐，煉精化氣，煉氣化神，煉神還虛，此乃修行之根本大道。

武道一途，浩瀚如煙海。外練筋骨皮，內練一口氣。招式為枝葉，內力為根基。根基不固，則招式如無根之萍，難成大器。故凡習武之人，必先正心誠意，明悟己道，方可漸入佳境。

此卷記載自古流傳之修行法門，分內功心法、外功招式、輕身步法、奇門兵器四路。習者當循序漸進，不可急於求成。切記：武道無止境，唯勤是岸。

Since the dawn of creation, when primal chaos first parted to reveal heaven and earth, the spiritual energy of the cosmos has flowed through every mountain and river. The ancient sages, observing the movements of beasts and the fury of storms, discovered the arts of guiding qi through the body — transforming essence into breath, breath into spirit, and spirit into the void.

The path of Wulin is vast as the ocean. Without a rooted foundation, all technique is but a reed upon the wind. Let this codex serve as your guide through the four cardinal arts of cultivation.
