Photosynthetic Solar Arrays

PSA-7 Series
TypeBio-Photovoltaic
Efficiency47.3% (record)
LifespanSelf-repairing (est. 80+ years)
MaterialsEngineered chloroplasts, silk-protein substrate
End of LifeFull biodegradation in 18 months

Overview

Photosynthetic Solar Arrays (PSAs) represent the convergence of quantum biology and materials science. Unlike traditional photovoltaics, PSAs harness quantum coherence mechanisms evolved by photosynthetic organisms.

Key Innovations

Quantum Coherence Capture - Exploits wave-like behavior of excitons for near-perfect energy transfer.

Self-Repair Mechanism - Biological components continuously regenerate through engineered mitosis.

Closed-Loop Lifecycle - Arrays are composted and nutrients returned to soil at end of life.
