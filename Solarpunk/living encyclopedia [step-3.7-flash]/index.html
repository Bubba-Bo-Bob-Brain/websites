Featured Entry

Mycelium Network Communication Systems

Biological mesh networks utilizing fungal mycelium to transmit data across eco-communities, replacing copper wiring with living, carbon-sequestering infrastructure. First deployed in the Pacific Northwest Rewilding Projects of 2147, these networks now connect over 82% of global solarpunk settlements.
