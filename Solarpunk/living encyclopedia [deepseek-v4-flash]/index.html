solarpunk

A cultural movement & aesthetic that envisions a future where technology and nature are in symbiosis. Decentralised renewable energy, communal governance, and ecological restoration form its backbone.

  • founded 2008
  • key principle abundance through cooperation
  • motto “the future is solar, not dismal”

solar·pedia is a collaborative wiki documenting the tools, communities, and rewilded ecologies that define the solarpunk imaginary. Every article is a seed — plant it, nurture it, share it.

This edition highlights bioremediation, the Sunflower Housing Co‑op, and the Keystone Seed Bank.
