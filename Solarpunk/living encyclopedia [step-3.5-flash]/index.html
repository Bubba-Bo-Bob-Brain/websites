Perovskite Solar Cells

Next-generation photovoltaic technology offering high efficiency, low-cost manufacturing, and tunable transparency for integration into buildings and everyday objects.

MC
JM
AP
+12
15 contributors

Overview

Perovskite solar cells represent a revolutionary advancement in photovoltaic technology, achieving laboratory efficiencies exceeding 25% while utilizing abundant, low-cost materials. Named for their crystal structure similarity to the mineral perovskite, these thin-film solar cells can be manufactured using solution-based processes that significantly reduce energy input compared to traditional silicon wafer production.

In solarpunk communities worldwide, perovskite technology has enabled unprecedented integration of solar generation into urban and rural environments—from transparent windows that generate power to flexible solar textiles for mobile applications.

Key Characteristics

High Efficiency

Lab efficiencies now exceed 25.5%, with commercial modules achieving 18-20% in real-world conditions.

Low-Cost Production

Solution processing and roll-to-roll manufacturing reduce capital costs by up to 70% compared to silicon.

Tunable Properties

Composition engineering allows customization of bandgap, transparency, and color for specific applications.

Flexible & Lightweight

Thin-film nature enables integration on curved surfaces, textiles, and portable devices.
