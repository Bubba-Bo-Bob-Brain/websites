Mycelial Restoration Networks

Living subterranean infrastructure that harnesses the bioremediation capabilities of saprophytic fungi to cleanse contaminated soils, purify water tables, and establish decentralized communication pathways between cooperative settlements. First deployed in the Lower Hudson Watershed in 2047, these networks have since expanded across three continents, processing over 12 million tonnes of industrial legacy waste while providing high-bandwidth biological data transfer at speeds rivaling fiber-optic systems.
