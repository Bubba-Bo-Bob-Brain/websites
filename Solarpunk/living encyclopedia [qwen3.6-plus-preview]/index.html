🌿 Featured Article: The Great Rewilding

The Great Rewilding refers to the coordinated global effort, beginning in the 2040s, to restore degraded ecosystems to self-sustaining, biodiverse states. It represents one of humanity's most ambitious ecological undertakings, fundamentally reshaping the relationship between human settlements and natural environments.

Unlike earlier conservation efforts that focused on isolated protected areas, the Great Rewilding emphasized connectivity — creating wildlife corridors, restoring watersheds, and reintroducing keystone species to trigger trophic cascades that would accelerate ecosystem recovery.

The initiative was catalyzed by the convergence of three factors: the maturation of synthetic biology tools for de-extinction and genetic rescue, the widespread adoption of vertical farming that freed up agricultural land, and a cultural shift toward ecological stewardship following the Climate Restoration Accords of 2038.
